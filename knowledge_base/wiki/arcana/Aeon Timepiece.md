Bosses / Aeon Timepiece

## Overview
The Aeon Timepiece is a boss essential to progression. It is fought at the top of the Aeon Clocktower on Timeless planets.

### Drops

Aeon Core x1
Eternal Page x20
Aeon Essence x20
[Pixels](https://starbounder.org/Pixel) x26,000
Aeon Cape (50%), Infinity Helm (50%)
