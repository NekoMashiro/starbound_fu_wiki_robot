# [Arcana] Alfieria

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Alfieria

---

Alfieria is the current Divine Dragon, and seemingly the leader of the Gilten. Not much is known about her other then that all Gilten seem to have a deep respect for her. 
The personal guards of the Divine Dragon are known as the Apostles, and they possess unrivaled strength.
