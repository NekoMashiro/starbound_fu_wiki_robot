Lore / Codex / An Icy Eminence

## Overview
An Icy Eminence is a book that can be purchased at the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
The Eminsnow planets are cloaked in frozen snow and cherry blossoms, while old clans that worship the Lunar deities are scattered among the icy mountains. It is through the Eminsnow's still frost and unmoving ice that the moonlight glows are preserved.
