## Overview
Arcana - Getting Started is a book that can be obtained in a number of ways:
- A new character starts with this item in their inventory
- Crafted directly from [C] for 1 pixel and 1 sheet of [paper](https://starbounder.org/Paper)
- Crafted at the Magician's Table for 1 pixel and 1 sheet of [paper](https://starbounder.org/Paper)
- Purchased from the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
Arcana - Getting Started

Welcome to Arcana! This mod adds a large quantity of planets and items, and most are made for late-game gameplay. To start your journey as a mage, you will first need to craft a magician's table at an inventor's table or by hand. To start the main progression, you can then travel to Arcane Stars and find Arcanium Ore from Arcane planets.
