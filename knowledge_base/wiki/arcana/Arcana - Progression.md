## Overview
Arcana - Progression is a book that can be obtained in a number of ways:
- A new character starts with this item in their inventory
- Crafted directly from [C] for 1 pixel and 1 sheet of [paper](https://starbounder.org/Paper)
- Crafted at the Magician's Table for 1 pixel and 1 sheet of [paper](https://starbounder.org/Paper)
- Purchased from the Bookstore on Nova Station.
