Ores / Arcanium Ore

## Overview
Arcanium is an ore findable on all Planets that orbit Arcane Stars (With the exception of Verdant Planets). It can also be found amidst the surface levels of Evoaquatic and Animus Planets.

There is a 20% chance for Arcanium Ore to drop whenever Arcane Water is placed into an Essence Extractor Mk.1.

## Usages
Arcanium Ore can be refined into Arcanium Bars at an Arcanium Furnace. It is an ingredient for the following: 

Arcanium Bar 

Arcanium Furnace
