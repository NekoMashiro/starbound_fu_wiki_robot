# [Arcana] Armor

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Armor

---

Arcana adds various armor sets from Tier 1 to Tier 9+, most of which are crafted from the newly introduced anvil and its upgraded versions. Many of these armor sets have armor set bonuses, which grants a unique buff to the player when a full set of armor is equipped.
