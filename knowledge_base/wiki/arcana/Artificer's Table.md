Crafting Stations / Artificer's Table

## Overview
The Artificer's Table is tier 4 of the main crafting station of Arcana. It is upgraded from the Cyberneticist's Table and is the final upgrade. It allows for crafting all recipes from the [Inventor's Table](https://starbounder.org/Inventor%27s_Table).
