# [Arcana] Artificer

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Artificer

---

The Artificer is the leader of the **Aurea Collective**. They oversee the preservation of artifacts as well as the development of Gilten technology.
The Artificer believes that "the Gilten must preserve and protect these artifacts from evil's reach, so that peace may prosper within the universe."

Not much else is known about them.
