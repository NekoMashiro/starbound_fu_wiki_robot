Lore / Codex / Astral Corridor

## Overview
Astral Corridor is a book that can be bought from the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
The ancient Astral Corridors can be accessed through warp doors found in the center of the Sunken Shrines. Soaked in Azure essence, these corridors harbor mostly Azure and aquatic creatures.
