Ores / Atumium Ore

## Overview
Atumium Ore is an ore that can be mined on Solarized Planets orbiting Tripolar Stars. It can be found in small clusters close to the surface or bigger deposits underground, in the biome closest to the core. IT can also be found in all Tripolar Star Planets and some Mirage Stars planets at the biome closest to the core.

## Usages
Atumium ore can be refined at an Auric Smelter using 2 ore and 2 solarium ore. It can be used for crafting weapons and armor, and Nullatus Bar.
