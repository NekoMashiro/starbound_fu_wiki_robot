# [Arcana] Aurea Collective

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Aurea_Collective

---

The **Aurea Collective** or just **Aurea** is a Gilten founded organization focused on the safekeeping and recovery of ancient artifacts and technology.
At its head is the Artificer who oversees preservation of artifacts as well as the development of Gilten technology.
