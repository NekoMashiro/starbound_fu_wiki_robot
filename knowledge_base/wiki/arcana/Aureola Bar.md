Bars / Aureola Bar

## Overview
Aureola Bar is a material that is smelted at a Modern Smelter (or higher) from the following items:

- 1 Anima Chunk
- 1 Morphosis Chunk
- 1 Arcanium Bar
- 1 Atla Gold Bar
- 1 Electrolite Superstone
- 1 Holosium Bar
- 1 Necroium Bar
- 1 Corrodium Bar

## Usages
Aureola Bars are used as ingredients in the following recipes: 

### Crafting Stations
