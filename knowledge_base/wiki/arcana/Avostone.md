Ores / Avostone

## Overview
Avostone is a material that can be mined from the Sanguine subbiome on Animus Planets orbiting Illuminated Stars. Small deposits of it can be found near the subbiome's surface, emitting a soft red glow. 

## Usages
Avostone cannot be further refined at an Arcanium Furnace. It is an ingredient for the following: 

Star Breaker 

Sapience 

Exousia Workstation 

Exousia Armory
