Ores / Azurium Ore

## Overview
Azurium Ore is an ore that can be mined on Azure Desert Planets orbiting Tripolar Stars. It can be found in small to medium clusters close to the surface. 

## Usages
Azurium ore can be refined at an Auric Smelter using 2 ore and 2 gold ore. It can be used for crafting weapons and armor, and Nullatus Bar.
