# [Arcana] Bars

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Bars

---

Ores mined from planets can be smelted into bars and other refined materials at an Arcanium Furnace or any of its updated forms. All lower-upgrade tier materials can also be crafted at the highest current upgrade tier.
