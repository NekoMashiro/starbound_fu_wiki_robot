# [Arcana] Blistering

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **分类**: Planets | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Blistering

---

Blistering is a primary biome type characterized by billowing clouds of cinders and ash, and a scorching ground of blistering cobblestone and obsidian. Blistering planets are of a ‘moderate’ threat level, geared toward tier 2 in progression. Their appearance is similar to volcanic planets, besides the lack of lava pools. No Cooling EPP upgrades are required. 

Blistering planets can be found orbiting arcane (green) star types. The only possible mini biome that spawns inside blistering planets is the bone biome, characterized by its decorative bones and skulls. Crabcano, Fennix, Ignome, Pyromantle, and Smoglin have a high chance of being found in blistering biomes, and Avian and Glitch settlements have a higher chance to appear as well.

## Navigation Console
 *The blistering is covered with the desert heat. Old ruins of ancient fire are scattered here.* 

## Materials
The surface layer of blistering planets is primarily made up of blistering cobblestone, along with obsidian, granite rock, and stone rubble. Ores such as coal, copper, gold, iron, core fragments, and magifire crystals are available near the surface.

## Weather
Weather in blistering biomes is mostly clear with a chance of cinder showers or light ashfall. The probability for weather types on blistering planets is as follows:

| --- | --- | --- |

## Wild Crops
There are a variety of harvestable crops found in each biome. These can be harvested by interacting with them or by digging them up. They'll drop produce and have a chance to drop a seed which can be replanted.
