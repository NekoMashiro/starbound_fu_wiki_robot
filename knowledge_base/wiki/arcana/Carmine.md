# [Arcana] Carmine

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Carmine

---

Carmine is a prominent member of the Aurea Collective, and is the person who formally invites the player to join it, as well as who guides them through the Seeker Trial. Carmine is very dedicated to the Collective's cause, and believes in it whole-heartedly. She is quite peppy and is quick to make new allies.

Carmine is a half Gilten half human hybrid. She lacks the distinctive wings of the holy dragons but has inherited their horns, as well as a belief in the Light.
