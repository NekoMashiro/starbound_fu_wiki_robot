Lore / Codex / Celestial Corridors

## Overview
Celestial Corridors is a book that can be bought from the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
Each of the three celestial elements can sometimes generate distortions known as the Celestial Corridors. The Solar Corridor, for example, is filled with solar crystals and otherworldly solar creatures. These corridors are often dangerous to navigate, and one should be prepared before adventuring into a Celestial Corridor.
