Bars / Dustrium Bar

## Overview
Dustrium Bar is a material that is smelted at a Modern Smelter (or higher) from 2 Raw Dustrium.

## Usages
Dustrium Bars are used as an ingredient in the following recipes: 

Connector

Dustrium Wire

Dustrium Sheet
