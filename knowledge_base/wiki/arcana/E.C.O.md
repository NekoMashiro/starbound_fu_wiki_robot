Lore / Codex / E.C.O.

## Overview
E.C.O. was a book that can be purchased at the Bookstore on Nova Station. In update 1.2, it was replaced with S.E.E.D. and is no longer obtainable by any means.

## Contents
The Workshop is known for dismantling planets and extracting as many resources as they can from sites, often leaving planets polluted. The same Workshop has, however, managed to create a device capable of cleansing entire planets similar to that of the ancient terraformers. The E.C.O., or Environment Cleanse Operator, requires a significant amount of resources to operate, but it has shown to be a highly capable terraforming device.
