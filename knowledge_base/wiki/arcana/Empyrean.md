# [Arcana] Empyrean

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Empyrean

---

**Empyrean** is a primary biome type characterized by an abundance of golden magic, large forests of golden foliage, and large Gilten Sky Arks
Many Gilten dungeons can be found on Empyrean planets
