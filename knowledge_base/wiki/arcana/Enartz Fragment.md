Ores / Enartz Fragment

## Overview
Enartz Fragment is an ore that can be mined on Vermilion Planets orbiting Mirage Stars. It can be found throughout the planet, in small sized chunks. Some sub biomes may have larger chunks, especially underground.

## Usages
Enartz Fragments can be refined at an Auric Smelter using 2 ore and 1 iron ore to make 1 Enartz.
