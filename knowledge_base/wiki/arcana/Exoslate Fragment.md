Ores / Exoslate Fragment

## Overview
Exoslate Fragment is an ore that can be mined on Vermilion Planets orbiting Mirage Stars. It can be found rarely throughout the planet, in very small chunks. Some sub biomes may have larger chunks underground.

## Usages
Exoslate Fragments can be refined at an Auric Smelter using 2 ore and 1 iron ore to make 1 Exoslate.
