Lore / Codex / Fleets of The Horizon

## Overview
Fleets of The Horizon is a codex that can be purchased at the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
The Horizon has recently ascended to be an Archa level threat, after discoveries of their involvement with the Lucid Eye. Their fleets have been rabidly expanding. Their purpose seems to be raising the abnormality called World Horizon. It's unsure of the exact effects it will bring, but it's likely that this abnormality will alter the universe on a very large scale.
