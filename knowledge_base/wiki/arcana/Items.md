**Items** are objects in Starbound. They can be obtained via different methods, including:
- Killing Monsters
- Mining various Blocks
- Smashing Pods
- Opening Status Pods
- Finding Chests
- Buying from NPCs
- Trading with NPC Operated Stations
- Getting from other players in multiplayer
- Harvesting Crops
- Catching Bugs with a Bug Net
- Fishing in various biomes and liquids
- Completing Quests

Most items are non-placeable, while others such as Blocks and Decorative objects are.
