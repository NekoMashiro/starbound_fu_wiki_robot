# [Arcana] Lore

---

Arcana's main lore takes place after the fall of the [Ruin](https://starbounder.org/Ruin), and involves various factions that rose to power after the event.
## The Galactic Trade Coalition
In the aftermath of the Protectorate's collapse and the Empire's frigid war against the Highmind machines, three megacorps united with other corporations across the galaxy to found the Galactic Trade Coalition. Accepting refugees and fleeing merchants, the Coalition quickly cemented itself as a free trade haven, bringing about a brief respite of peace.

### Orion Cybernetics
As the forefront of technological advances, Orion Cybernetics is one of the leading companies in the development of self-driven machines, as well as the research in neuro-implants and data intelligence. Their latest development in advanced AI, however, has led to a series of local unrest and anti-AI movements.

### Titancorp
Formerly known as Titan Industries.

Titancorp solidified itself as a trading giant after purchasing mass quantities of shipping companies, mining facilities and foundries, as well as the acquisition of the Workshops of Gears. Despite having access to advanced equipment and machinery, many of its mining-site employees still have to engage in dangerous to lethal working environments firsthand. The megacorp cares not: the factory must grow. 
### Ecolife Solutions
The pioneer of cutting-edge biotech research, Ecolife Solutions offers products specialized for environmental and biological services.
Thanks to their many breakthroughs in genetic and bio-experimentation, the scientists at Ecolife have perfected agricultural production and medical regeneration procedures. Mass scale terraforming too has been made trivial by their development of S.E.E.D., or the Simultaneous Environment Enrichment Device.

## Gangs & Pirates
The establishment of the Galactic Trade Coalition brought trade to a new height. Corporations thrived as the foundation of this commerce-centered society, and colonies spread rapidly thanks to the Coalition. However, this economic boom soon became the target of emerging pirates and galactic gangs.
### Anarkyons
Once a small smuggling gang, now the prominent drug empire operating within GTC territories. They're highly territorial and profit from black-market deals and drug trades, the latter of which they've perfected through countless chem-experiments in the Viridescents. Thanks to their backroom deals, they're able to conduct their operations without interference from most corporations and Coalition patrols.
### Carnival
After the sudden disappearance of the Curators and their art-driven lunatics, the Carnival quickly emerged as the most feared gang known to the Coalition, driven by raw desire for physical harmony. Even the mention of the Carnival brings shivers to veteran mercenaries, and one is doomed to die a gruesome death if they encounter the troupe's psychos. Unlike typical gangs, members of the Carnival aren't restricted by rules, and are free to do as they wish. From bloodthirsty murderers to hungry cannibals, all sorts of freaks are gathered in the Carnival's troupe. Though they are volatile in nature, they are able to maintain a simple hierarchy due to their worship of the Sanguine King. 
### Scavengers
The Scavengers can be found in all corners of the universe, surviving through the harshest of environments. Many outcasts of society as well as the poor would eventually join the Scavengers, and they will act without restraint for their own survival. The Scavengers are experts at taking things apart, and repurposing them for their own use. This makes them fearsome raiders, as well as a troublesome existence for traveling merchants even in the Empire's protected territories.
### Novaskulls
As a group of primarily Novakid troublemakers and like-minded rebels, pirating is nothing more than an entertainment for the Novaskulls. Despite their simpleminded nature, they've managed to pull off an infamous heist in the GTC's most secure vault. Yet, the heist is only one of the many incidents that earned them a plentiful bounty as well as a great reputation among pirates. 
### Steelblades
A nomad mercenary group travelling through the universe, helping those in need in the name of chivalry. Unlike how typical mercenary groups are treated, many pirate factions despise the Steelblades for offering protection services often free of charge, and interfering with their operations. Led by an enigmatic leader, they uphold a strict moral code at all times, with the aim of maintaining universal justice.
