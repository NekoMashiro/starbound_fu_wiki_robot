Lore / Codex / Luye Association

## Overview
Luye Association is a book that can be purchased at the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
The Luye has been known as a renowned tea exporting company by many, though the Luye Association was originally a group of assassins for hire, founded by a band of rogues. Whilst the Luye has long ceased combat operations, they continue to train their new recruits with assassination techniques.
