Ores / Magifire Crystal

## Overview
Magifire Crystal is an ore findable on Blistering Planets. It can be mined near the surface, with deposits emitting a soft pink light.

## Usages
Magifire Crystal, although unable to be refined in an Arcanium Furnace, is an ingredient for the following: 

Conflagration Shard 

Evernight Crystal 

Magifire Dagger 

Magifire Wand 

Hestia 

Illuminated EPP
