Essence / Magiflux Extract

Magiflux Extract is an essence obtained by placing either Arcane Water (100% rate) or [Healing Water](https://starbounder.org/Healing_Water) (80% rate) into an Essence Extractor.

## Usage
Crafting:

| Jupiter Cell | 12 |
