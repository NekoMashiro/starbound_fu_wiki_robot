Crafting Stations / Modern Smelter

## Overview
The Modern Smelter is an upgrade from the Arcanium Furnace. Originally, this was called the Orion Smelter.

### Requires
16 Electrolite Superstone 
1 [Copper Wire](https://starbounder.org/Copper_Wire)
1 Enhanced Battery

### Upgrade to next tier - Auric Smelter
1 Aureola Bar 
10 [Dirt](https://starbounder.org/Dirt)
10 [Timber](https://starbounder.org/Timber)
10 [Cobblestone](https://starbounder.org/Cobblestone)
10 Plant Matter
10 [Sand](https://starbounder.org/Sand)
10 [Sandstone](https://starbounder.org/Sandstone)
