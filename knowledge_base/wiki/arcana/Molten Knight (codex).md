Lore / Codex / Molten Knight (codex)

## Overview
Molten Knight (codex) is a book dropped by the Molten Knight boss upon defeat.

## Contents
After being banished from his homeland, the Molten Knight was accepted by the other dark knights at the volcanic forge. As its guardian, the knight is clad in heavy armor forged in the heart of a volcano, glowing with molten veins that pulse with fiery energy.
