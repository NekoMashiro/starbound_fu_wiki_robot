Ores / Oceanite

## Overview
Oceanite is an ore that can be mined on Azure Sea Planets orbiting Tripolar Stars. It can be found in Islands on the sea, or at the sea floor, both in small chunks.

## Usages
Oceanite is used as an ingredient in the following recipes:

Oceanic Composite
Oceanic Tech Card
