# [Arcana] Races

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Races

---

- [Stub]*

Arcana adds three new playable races, Arcanian, Gilten and Spectwing along with one non-playable race, the Gelsemis. Each has their own distinct background lore and unique aesthetics, such as ship design and architecture.

The player has the ability to unlock race-specific features through gameplay. Playable races also have separate armor tiers for changing the aesthetics of a character.
