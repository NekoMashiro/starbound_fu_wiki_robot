Ores / Rufescite Ore

## Overview
Rufescite Ore is an ore that can be mined on Vermilion Planets orbiting Mirage Stars. It can be found throughout the planet, in small sized chunks. Some sub biomes may have larger chunks, especially underground. Vermilion Essence can be found in the ore chunks as well.

## Usages
Rufescite can be refined at an Auric Smelter using 1 to make 4 Rufescite composite. It can be used for object crafting.
