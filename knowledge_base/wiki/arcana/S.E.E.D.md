Lore / Codex / S.E.E.D.

## Overview
S.E.E.D. is a book that can be purchased at the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
The Workshop is known for dismantling planets and extracting as many resources as they can from sites, often leaving planets polluted. Though, the resources they gather help to fuel terraforming devices produced by other corporations, notably the S.E.E.D. developed by Ecolife Solutions.

## Trivia
- Before update 1.2, this codex was called E.C.O.
