# [Arcana] Shining Sea

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **分类**: Planets | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Shining_Sea

---

**Shining Sea** is a primary biome type characterized by small islands and expansive oceans, islands are primarily made up of sand and palm trees, while the ocean floor is littered with aquatic life and various ocean foliage and coral. Shining Sea planets are a "Inconceivable" threat level, geared towards tier 6 in progression. . While no EPP is explicitly required to traverse this planet an Arcane EPP is recommended as without one any explorer without one will experience Arcane Energy Drain. 

Shining Sea planets can be found orbiting arcane star types, in the deep seas of this planet you can find [Hylotl Underwater Cities ](https://starbounder.org/Hylotl%20Underwater%20City)

## Navigation Console
 *Atla gold deposites and underwater structures are detected in the Shining Sea.* 

## Materials
The surface of Shining Sea planets are primarily made up of Seastone and Golden Sand. Ores such as Alta Gold Ore, and Arcanium Ore are available near the surface.
