Lore / Codex / Stars above the Desert

## Overview
Stars above the Desert is a book that can be bought from the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
The Azure Deserts are said to be enchanted with celestial power of the Azure. While the planets are primarily occupied by Arcanian settlements, remnants of a now sunken society can be found in the depth of the Azure oceans.
