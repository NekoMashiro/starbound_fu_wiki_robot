Lore / Codex / The Aeon

## Overview
The Aeon is a book that can be bought from the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
The Aeon is one of the higher elements that interferes with the flow of time and space. Residues of the Aeon's golden lights can be found in the Timeless, but researchers have not managed to directly interact with the essence of the Aeon.
