Lore / Codex / The Brightstill Ark

## Overview
The Brightstill Ark is a book that can be purchased at the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
Long ago, ancestors of the Arcanians dwelled in a city of clouds, surrounded by constructs created by the ancients.

Resting above all else, the floating city safeguarded its people for eons, sustained by the everlasting spring, the Lake of Fertility

However, the seemingly bottomless waters become further depleted with each passing year. The Grand Archivist forsaw, that the spring would one day run dry.

Unwilling to see the ancient spring dwindle further, the Archivist decided that another oasis must be found to preserve the sacred lake, and gathered the people to depart from the old city.

Like seeds of dandelion carried by the wind, the settlers have since then founded many more settlements in distant lands. The now empty city, the Brightstill Ark, remains undisturbed in the skies to this day.
