Lore / Codex / The Imperial Police

## Overview
The Imperial Police is a book that can be purchased from the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
Order is one of the foundations of the Empire, and the Imperial Police enforce what is needed to maintain peace at all costs, even at the cost of freedom and liberty. Utilizing non-lethal plasma weapons, they patrol and monitor the Exousian cities in their heavy white armor.
