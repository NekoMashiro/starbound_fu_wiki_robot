# [Arcana] The Player

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Player

---

## Character Statistics:
The player is normally two blocks wide and four blocks tall with a base health of 100, base energy of 100, and an energy regeneration rate of 58.5% per second. With armor equipped, energy regenerates at a rate proportional to the amount of Energy Regen on the player’s armor. Any action that expends energy will temporarily stop regeneration.
The player has 5 item bags which can hold 40 items and item stacks each. The leftmost bag is the main bag, followed by the material bag, the object bag, the reagent bag, and the food bag.

## Character Creation:
The character creation menu allows you to select from among seven vanilla races: Apex, Avian, Floran, Glitch, Human, Hylotl, and Novakid. Additionally, the Arcana mod adds two more races, Arcanian and Gilten. Each race has their own unique aesthetics and racial features which can be unlocked over the course of the game. You will also be able to select their gender, appearance, and what game mode you would like to play in.
