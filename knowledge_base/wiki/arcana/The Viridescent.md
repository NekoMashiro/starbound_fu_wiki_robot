Lore / Codex / The Viridescent

## Overview
The Viridescent is a book that can be purchased at the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
The Viridescent is a green, toxic planet filled with rivers of acidic ichor. Some ancient remains still linger, but the planet has long been inhospitable for most species. Toxic plant life can be found rarely, most of which have evolved to be lethal on touch, while some others adapted land-mobility and even flight.
