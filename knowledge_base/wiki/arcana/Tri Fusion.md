Lore / Codex / Tri Fusion

## Overview
Tri Fusion is a book that can be purchased at the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
A seemingly simple step beyond the regular fusion reactions, but nearly impossible to produce. Not to mention impractical as well, fusing three elements into one produces items that are far weaker than concentrating all resources into only one element. A Tri-Fusion's product would also be highly unstable and likely won't be of any use.
