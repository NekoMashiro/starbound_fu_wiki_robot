## Overview
**Voltatic Forts** are a unique dungeon found exclusively on Superstorm Expanse planets. The dungeon is made up of many box shaped rooms, with many hosting traps and monsters. The end of the dungeon features an arena with a powerful monster, the Electric Golem, with the room above that holding the dungeon's shield generator.
