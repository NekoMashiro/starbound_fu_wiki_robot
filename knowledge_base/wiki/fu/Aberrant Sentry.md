# [FU] Aberrant Sentry

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Bosses | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aberrant_Sentry

---

**Aberrant Sentry** is a boss in the Ancient Temple mission. There is a Tricorder quest to defeat it.

It is a stronger variant of Kluex Sentries from vanilla Avian mission.
