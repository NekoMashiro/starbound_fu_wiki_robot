# [FU] Aether Dust

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aether_Dust

---

Can be found on the surface of Aether planets.

Aether Dust has the unique property of only moving vertically, forming towers instead of mounds. This is identical to how loose materials (sand, silt, etc) act in Terraria.

This block often features in production setups because it can be created easily if you have control of various liquids on most planets and can be processed to obtain many ores at once (e.g. sifted for Titanium Ore, Penumbrite Ore, Xithricite, mixed with Lava and sifted for Diamonds etc.).
