# [FU] Aether World

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aether_World

---

> *See the page Aetheric for the land-based variant of Aether World.*
**Aether World** is a beautiful, yet very deadly high-tier world made of Aether. Vertical-falling Aether Dust is common here.

These planets are somewhat rare. They can only be around Black Stars.

Aether Worlds are the sole source of Xithricite Ore.

## Variants
This planet has two variants (both are shown as "Aether World" on the starmap):
- ocean-based (see below): an infinite ocean of liquid Aether.
- land-based (see the page **Aetheric**): with trees that give Aen Wood Log, glowing Miasmet blocks and potentially gigantic Doomwyrm mini-boss.
