# [FU] Alternator Generator

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Alternator_Generator

---

**Alternator Generator** is the early-game way to generate Power. It burns items like Wooden Log. It produces 4W of power (which is enough for one Iron Centrifuge or two Sifters).

Later in the game it can be replaced with a more powerful Combustion Generator.

## Accepted fuels

| Fuel Types | Burn Time | Where to get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wooden Log | 10 | Chop trees |
| Coal | 16 | Mine it from the ground (similarly to Copper Ore, etc.) |
| Golden Wood | 20 | Produced by some bees |
| Redwood Bark | 20 | Chop gigantic trees on Primeval planets |
| Penumbral Bark | 30 | Chop gigantic trees on Penumbra planets |
| Alien Weird Wood | 40 | Chop unusually shaped mutated trees |
| Energy Wood | 50 | Chop trees that look like glowing orbs |
| Aen Wood Log | 60 | Chop trees on land-based Aether World planets |
| Purrpetual Energy | 120000 | Strapping a piece of Tearnut Butter Sandwich onto a cat |

## Heat mechanics
The Alternator Generator must heat up to reach full power when fuel is first added, similarly it also takes time to cool down. For each second the generator increases or decreases its heat by 5%, reaching full power and 90% heat after 18 seconds.

## Tips
- Chainsaw can chop trees quickly.
- If you add a battery (such as a Simple Battery), this 4W generator can be used to power Rock Breaker (needs 6W, so it will run at 2/3 of maximum speed) and Extraction Lab (needs 10J per operation).
