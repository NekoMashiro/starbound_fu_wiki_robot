# [FU] Arc Smelter

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Power | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Arc_Smelter

---

**Arc Smelter** automatically smelts 2 ores into 1 bar (e.g. Densinium Ore into Densinium Bar) and has a chance to produce additional materials. Its speed is 4 operations per second. It can also smelt Metallic Hydrogen and Deuterium Rod.

Arc Smelter replaces earlier Blast Furnace, which can't smelt the ores of Legendary rarity and produces less additional materials.
