# [FU] Arctic

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Arctic

---

**Artic** planets are tier 5 vanilla planets. They feature an infinite ocean of regular Water (not Salt Water) and have a Cold II hazard.

They can be seen as half-frozen version of Ocean planets.
