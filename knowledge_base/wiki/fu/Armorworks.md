# [FU] Armorworks

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Armorworks

---

**Armorworks** is a crafting station for making weapons, shields, armors and EPPs (backpacks) from FU and vanilla.

It completely replaces Anvil (vanilla crafting station), and unlike Anvil, it doesn't truncate the long descriptions of items, has more tabs (e.g. a separate tab for boomerangs and chakrams), allows to search by keyword (e.g. all armors with the word "ranged" in their description). You don't need an Anvil if you have Armorworks.

Some tier 1 weapons (all Primitive and Bone weapons, e.g. Primitive Shortspear or Bone Scythe) are hand-crafted and don't need any crafting stations. Weapons from Iron Bar and Core Fragment (also tier 1) are more advanced, so they require Armorworks.

## Tips
- If you don't have any Water, you can use a Well to obtain it.
- Craftable weapons are usually stronger than randomly generated (RNG) weapons that are found in the chests, etc.
- EPP augments are made at the Tinkering Table (and its upgrades).
