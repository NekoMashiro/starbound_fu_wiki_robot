# [FU] Atmospheric Filter

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Atmospheric_Filter

---

**Atmospheric Filter** broadcasts the effects of one EPP Augment (such as Ghost Shield) to a radius of 150 tiles around it.

This is a one-slot storage, and the EPP Augment must be placed inside. Without the augment it doesn't do anything.

Later in the game you can replace it with a more advanced Atmospheric Regulator, which has space for 3 augments, has a range of 500 tiles and grants some basic immunities.
