# [FU] Atmospheric Regulator

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Powered Station, Power, Needs Revision, Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Atmospheric_Regulator

---

**Atmospheric Regulator** protects against common environmental hazards (see below) in a radius of 500 tiles around it.

You can place up to 3 EPP Augments into the Atmospheric Regulator, and their effects (all their bonuses and immunities) will be projected to the entire area that Regulator can affect.

Atmospheric Regulator is a more advanced version of Atmospheric Filter, which doesn't give any immunities and has space for only 1 EPP Augment.

## List of immunities
The following immunities are granted even without EPP Augments:
- Lack of air
- Poison, Proto-Poison
- Extreme Heat/Cold/Radiation
- Extreme Pressure
- Sulphuric Atmosphere
- Electrically Charged Atmosphere (on Rocky Moons) and Electrified debuff
- Harmful gases (except Caliginous Gas)

Anything that is not listed above is not covered by Atmospheric Regulator itself. You can, however, add an EPP Augment to protect yourself from additional hazards (for example, to add immunity to Erchius Ghost, place a Ghost Shield inside the Atmospheric Regulator).

## Tips
- Atmospheric Regulator is useful even if the planet is safe (doesn't have any hazards): you can place EPP augments like Item Magnet, Feeding or Speed Augment into the Atmospheric Regulator to benefit from their buffs while on your base.
