# [FU] Automated ore production

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Ores, Tutorials | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Automated_ore_production

---

A significant challenge is the automation of an unlimited production of Ores, which are required for the production of many items, including production chains themselves as Teleporter Cores are created either from Diamond or Durasteel Ore.

Strategies to automate easily the production of each ore are detailed here. Many ores can be created automatically in more complicated ways, but only simple chains are covered to help players create the leanest production setups.

Liquid mixing without Liquid Mixer specification means that either in world mixing with a Liquid Network or mixing in the Mixer are possible.

## Geodes crushing
One can obtain almost all ores but at a very slow rate crushing Geodes. In some cases it is the only method available. It works in the following way:
1. Cut stem:geodefoliage trees automatically, e.g. with a Harvester Turret,
1. Gather Geodes e.g. with a Grabber
1. Crush them in a Rock Crusher.

This will yield almost every ore in very small traces but is not a recommended method due to its speed. Of course, using Watchers you may involve a maximal amount of trees as your computing resources allow you, but if you have other methods and duplicate them many times, you will get a better result.
