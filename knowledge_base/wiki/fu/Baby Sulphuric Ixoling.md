# [FU] Baby Sulphuric Ixoling

---

**Baby Sulphuric Ixolings** are a variety of [Ixoling](https://starbounder.org/Ixoling) that live primarily in arid deserts, and toxic heated environments. These are newborn and are only beginning to develop their highly armored exoskeletons. Be wary when finding a Baby Ixoling, as only after growing in size and strength do they often break away from the protective presence of their Ixodoom broodmothers. Ixolings are small crawling enemies which appear most commonly in Ceremonial Hunting Caverns, primarily due to the presence of an [Ixodoom](https://starbounder.org/Ixodoom). They'll appear in groups and can be dispatched relatively quickly because of their low health. At this young stage, an Ixoling poses little threat to most creatures, being soft shelled and vulnerable. This variety possesses more powerfully developed exoskeleton, capable of Traversing dangerous environments more directly. The shell of this Ixoling takes on a deep yellow color with brown interlaced plating, in an effort to help camouflage the creature against the creatures more capable predators. Like other varieties of Ixolings, the Sulphuric Ixoling is capable of crawling along walls and up vertical surfaces. The plating on this variety of Ixoling is much thicker, encouraging hungry predators to seek other prey rather than to try and break through the toughened shell of the Sulphuric Ixoling.

The Sulphuric Ixoling is capable only of leaping toward a target with bared, developing needle like teeth and sharp pointy legs. Not the most capable in combat alone, these creatures most often travel in packs - choosing to attack en' mass, using numbers and overwhelming attacks to bring down bigger and stronger prey.

## Luna's Lab Notes
~ These creatures actively walk up and down walls and vertical surfaces.

~ The Sulphuric Ixoling is immune to both being poisoned, and set on fire.
