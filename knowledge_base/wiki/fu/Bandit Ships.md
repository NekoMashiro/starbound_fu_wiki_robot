# [FU] Bandit Ships

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bandit_Ships

---

The red & black color scheme and shape of a Bandit Ship is a sign of the common dungeon.
## Details
Randomly generated Bandit Ship encounters are vanilla. 
They contain various Bandit enemies in a very linear ship. The cockpit contains a marauder as a sort of 'microboss'.

The loot table mostly consists of pixel currency, genes, low-tier ores, salvaged mech parts, used tissues, Erchius fuel, mech blueprints, and space/space station blocks.
