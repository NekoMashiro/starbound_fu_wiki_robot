# [FU] Bandit Tents

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bandit_Tents

---

Bandit tents are random spawns, similar to wild merchants.
These tents usually include 2-3 bandits.
The Bandits wield RNG weapons.
These tents can be found on almost every planet, except Gas Giants.
However, not all bandit tents are the same. These spawns pick random furniture.

The list of random furniture includes:
Green Tents, Campfires, Barrel Fires, Prison Cafeteria Tables, Oil Lamps, Wooden Crates, and Metal Crates.

Metal Crates can contain loot.
