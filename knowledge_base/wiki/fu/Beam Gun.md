# [FU] Beam Gun

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Beam_Gun

---

The primary fire is a solid, piercing beam of light much like the Mining Laser, out to a medium distance. Enemies caught in the beam take damage several times a second. The alt-fire launches three orbs in a fan pattern that stick to terrain and explode on contact, or after a few seconds. This attack deals significantly less damage than other, typical sticky-bomb attacks.
