# [FU] Bee Refuge

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bee_Refuge

---

**Bee Refuge** is a store that exchanges bees (Larvas, Queens and Drones) for money and crafting materials.

It can be found in the Science Outpost, or purchased at Infinity Express for home use.
