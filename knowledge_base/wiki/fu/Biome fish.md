# [FU] Biome fish

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Mechanics | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biome_fish

---

**Biome fish** are monsters that can be caught with a Fishing Rod when casting it into infinite oceans on the following planets: Aether Sea, Arctic, Atropus Sea, Dark Arctic, Magma, Nitrogen Sea, Ocean, Strange Sea, Tidewater, Toxic. There is no fish in lakes, pools or core layer oceans of other planets.

See the page Fishing for the possible loot.
