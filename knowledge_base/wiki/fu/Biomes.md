# [FU] Biomes

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biomes

---

FU adds a plethora of planetary and minor biomes, but also enhances all [vanilla biomes](https://starbounder.org/Biome). Some biomes require specialized gear to safely traverse due to hazards not found in vanilla Starbound.
