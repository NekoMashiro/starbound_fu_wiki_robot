# [FU] Blastcap Launcher

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blastcap_Launcher

---

**Blastcap Launcher** is a weaponized variant of Translocator. It fires a targeting disk, but instead of teleporting you to the disk, it causes an explosion where the disk is.

It can be upgraded 4 times, which increases the starting damage (40) to 60/80/100/120.

Blastcap Launcher can be good for mining soft blocks (Dirt, etc.), but is vastly underperforming against the strong blocks like Magmarock or Obsidian.
