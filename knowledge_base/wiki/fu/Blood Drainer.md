# [FU] Blood Drainer

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Resource-generating backpacks | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blood_Drainer

---

**Blood Drainer** is a resource-generating backpack. As long as you wear it in your EPP slot, small amounts of Blood will slowly appear in your inventory. And that's not creepy at all.
