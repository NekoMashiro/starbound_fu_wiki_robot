**Bloodstone** are removed planets (they no longer appear in the game). They were too similar to Atropus. However, Bloodstone biome can still appear on Unknown planets.

It has ugly trees and questionable puddles of Blood, which makes this a weird place to visit. If was once home to life forms much like us... until their experiments wiped them out, leaving behind only gory grossness. Which explains the trees that stare at you. Good times.
