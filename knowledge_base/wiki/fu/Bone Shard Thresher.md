# [FU] Bone Shard Thresher

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bone_Shard_Thresher

---

The Bone Shard Thresher is a peculiar Bioweapon that has elements of both boomerangs and shotguns; It fires a tightly clustered ball of 'shards' that will hang in the air and slowly return towards the user, bouncing off of the first surface it hits, The individual shards pass through tiles & eventually time out into pools of blood. Due to it's unusual behaviour; it appears to be intended to help cover a player's retreat or punish pursuing enemies; It is particularily useful against flying enemy types that swoop and linger.

Like the ocu cannon and other bioweapons; this weapon corrupts tiles into other types

Due to it's relatively cheap crafting cost, it is a wise investment in the Metaphysics tree.

Presumably, it is functionally akin to the Vashta Cutter
