# [FU] Bone Wand

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bone_Wand

---

**Bone Wand** is an extremely strong tier 1 weapon. It throws an explosive fireball onto the enemies and sets them on fire. **Warning**: if you hit yourself, Bone Wand will harm the player! Aim it carefully and don't step on burning ground.

Fireball will appear at the mouse cursor when you start charging the wand. After it is charged, the fireball flies towards the place where cursor was at the moment of releasing the mouse button. This allows you to fire from behind walls, etc. by summoning a fireball in the air.

## Tips
- Bone Wand is one-handed and can be dual-wielded for even more destruction.
- Bone Wand may be hard to fire in corridors with low ceiling. When fighting underground, lure the enemies into larger caverns. Having a spare weapon is advised.
- The ground keeps burning for several seconds. Lure the enemies into this.
