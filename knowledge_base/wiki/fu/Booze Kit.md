# [FU] Booze Kit

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Booze_Kit

---

**Booze Kit** has everything for brewing alcohol, most importantly Fruit Press (to make fruit mash), Mashing Tun (to make ale ingredients), Fermenter (to make wines), Distillery / Still (to make hard liquor) and Rain Barrel (to soak Wheat).
