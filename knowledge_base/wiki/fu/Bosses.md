# [FU] Bosses

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bosses

---

Frackin Universe adds a variety of challenging bosses and mini-bosses. In addition to normal creatures found in planets, these creatures tend to have much higher health (for the tier they are found in), deal much higher damage, and tend to come rarely.

## Bosses (incomplete)
Here is an incomplete list of mini-bosses and bosses contained within Frackin Universe.
- The **Deep Seer**, a fish-like miniboss that can be found in Atropus Seas
- The **Veilendrex**, a fish-like miniboss that can be found in Aether Seas and on the Grand Pagoda Library

- The **Doomwyrm**, a giant worm mini-boss that can be rarely encountered on Deadly Caverns biomes and Aether Underground biomes.

- A **War Bot** mini-boss can be found on Cybersphere, montainous and wasteland Planets, but very rarely.
- A **Precursor War Bot** boss, a relatively difficult boss, based off of a pre-existing vanilla Starbound mini-boss that can be found in the precursor ruins mission.

- A **Heavy War Drone **can be found in some cyber sphere mini biome and the precursor ruins mission. They are generated procedurally

- The **Mama Poptop**, typically the first mini-boss encountered, found in the penultimate chamber of the Mine Dungeon found on Lush Planets.
- The **Aberrant Sentry**, a Kluex-themed sentry mini-boss located within the Temple's basement in the Ancient Temple Mission. Drops a shield.

- The **Megarachnid**,** **a spider-like mini-boss, found in Spider Nest mini-biomes. Drops the Web Shooter, a Grappling Hook variant with a faster speed and longer range compared to its Durasteel counterpart.
- The **Shoggoth**, a formless horror found at the end of Delta Freya II. Probably the only big boss of the mod
- **Star-Spawn Acolyte,** A miniboss is found in the Delta Freya II Mission
