# [FU] Bounce Wrap

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bounce_Wrap

---

**Bounce Wrap** is a consumable that makes you bouncy for 60 seconds. You also become virtually immune to fall damage (it's not removed completely, but the damage taken is divided by 1000).

Its bouncing is stronger than bouncing caused by Ighant or Springvault plants.
