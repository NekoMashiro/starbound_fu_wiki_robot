# [FU] Brain Slug

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Brain_Slug

---

The Brain Slug alters your movement, such that your character no longer moves at a steady speed. Instead, your character accelerates when moving, starting from a very slow speed and building up momentum as you move. Acceleration is slower while on the ground, but faster in the air.
