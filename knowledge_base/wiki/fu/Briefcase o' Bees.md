# [FU] Briefcase o' Bees

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Briefcase_o'_Bees

---

**Briefcase o' Bees** releases a swarm of angry bees that are hostile to everyone, including the player, as well as several friendly bees that you can catch with a Bug Net.

You can unlock its recipe by using the Briefcase o' Rewards item, which is graciously bestowed by Kevin as a gift for your hard work.

## Contents
11 species of non-hostile bees can sometimes be found inside this briefcase: Aquarum, Artisan, Assassin, Bumblebee, Carpenter, Crystalwing, Cuckoo, Digger, Honey, Inventor, Leafcutter.

There are also 10 possible types of bee-like hostile monsters like Giant Metal Bee.

It can also contain a non-hostile Garflipp.
