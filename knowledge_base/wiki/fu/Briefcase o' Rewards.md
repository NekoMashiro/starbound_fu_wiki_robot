# [FU] Briefcase o' Rewards

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Briefcase_o'_Rewards

---

**Briefcase o' Rewards** is a gift from Kevin after completing one of his quests. Surely nothing will go wrong if you open it.

Opening this gift unlocks a recipe to craft Briefcase o' Bees.
