# [FU] Bulbop Hop

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bulbop_Hop

---

The Bulbop Hop has a pastel pink outer-shell, distinguishing it from it's kin the Bulbop. Additionally, the Bulbop Hop has similar behavior as the more common Bulbop, hopping around the terrain and making quick leaps towards the player to deliver a body-blow attack, however the Bulbop Hops are faster and jump considerably farther. In addition they are more prone to appearing in large packs than Bulbops.

Lacking in combat, this creature can hop decently high to smash into prey in the air, or close distance to a target. However, once engaged, their damage is minimal and they only really have the one tactic - slam into anything that moves until it stops moving.

## Luna's Lab Notes
~ The Bulbop Hop launches itself so high with a jump that if a player runs underneath it's hopping arc, the creature will continuously jump back and forth over the player unable to get to them as they cannot shorted their hopping distance or height.
