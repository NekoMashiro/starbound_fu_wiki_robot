# [FU] Cave Detector

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cave_Detector

---

**Cave Detector** tool shows you the location of nearby caves (areas without solid blocks). It can be used to explore underground faster (by going towards areas where you won't need to dig).

Upgrading increases the radius of pulses and decreases cooldown.
