# [FU] Charisma

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Mechanics | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Charisma

---

**Charisma** improves the buy/sell prices at Space Stations for both items and trade goods.

In addition, for every 33.33% of Charisma, you can have +1 more crew member to follow you on planets.

Charisma has no effect on any other kind of shop (such as Terramart) or prices of Trader NPCs.

## Sources of Charisma bonuses and penalties
> Armor
> Bloodhound Armor (5% per piece, with a bonus 2.5% per level when upgraded)
> Gold Armor (15%, set bonus)
> Diplomat Armor (25%, set bonus)
> Troll King Armor (-100%, set bonus)

> Clothing
> Nonpareil Shirt, Nonpareil Pants, Hawker's Top, Hawker's Trousers, Toga Top, Toga Bottom (5% each, with a bonus 2.5% per level when upgraded)

> EPP Augments
> Charm Necklace (20%)
> Broken Master Manipulator (20%)

> Racial
> Kirhos have +25% to charisma ("Great Traders" bonus), while Pharitu have -10%, and Mantizi and Thelusians have -25% ("Poor Traders" penalty).

> Other
> Madness can have an unpredictable effect on how Charismatic you appear to others.
> Successfully maximizing your investment in a Trade Station will also provide a trade effectiveness boost to all stations you interact with, up to a max of 10 invested stations.
> The Douche Hat penalizes with -20% charisma. Anyone willing to benefit from charisma should avoid this item like the plague.
