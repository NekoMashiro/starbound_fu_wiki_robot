# [FU] Cheated death

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Minor mechanics | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cheated_death

---

** Cheated Death** effect means you got lucky to survive a lethal hit due to a bug in Starbound.

## Details
This bug makes you unkillable if you have health regeneration, went down to 0 hitpoints (which should be lethal), but then health regeneration restored it to be above 0 hitpoints, and you survived.

When this bug is detected, "Cheated Death" effect is applied. It prevents you from regenerating for some time, which prevents this bug from repeating over and over.

## How is the bug detected
Bug is detected by health being below 1 hitpoint (but above 0 hitpoints). It may result in death: if health is 0.6, then there is a 60% chance to survive and get Cheated Death, and 40% chance to die.
