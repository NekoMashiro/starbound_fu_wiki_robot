# [FU] Cock Block

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Joke items | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cock_Block

---

**Cock Block** is a throwable weapon used by mean people. Aside from its primary purpose, it also covers nearby blocks in chicken blood.

Resulting blood is not approved for Occasus cult rituals.
