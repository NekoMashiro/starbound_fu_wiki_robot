# [FU] Cold Moon

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cold_Moon

---

**Cold Moon** is a class of moon, instances of which are found in the cold orbital regions of most stars. It includes moons of type Crystalline, Strange Sea, Rocky Moon, Moon, Snow, Tundra, Nitrogen Sea, Ice Waste, and Frozen Moon.
