# [FU] Collar Holder

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Collar_Holder

---

**Collar Holder** allows you to wear pet collars on yourself (instead of your pet), receiving the same or reduced bonuses. You equip Collar Holder into the chest slot, thus forfeiting the possibility of armor set bonuses.

## Limitations
- Oblivious Collar has no effect on the player (monsters won't ignore you).
- Collars like Ice Bomb Collar (triggered by death of the wearer) are also not applicable.
- Glitchtop Collar has a different behavior (pet will acquire full immunity to the element, while player stacks more resistance with every hit received).
