# [FU] Combustion Generator

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Combustion_Generator

---

**Combustion Generator** is an early-game generator that burns fuel to generate up to 12W of power.

## Heat mechanics
The Combustion Generator must heat up to reach full power when fuel is first added, similarly it also takes time to cool down. For each second the generator increases or decreases its heat by 5%, reaching full power and 90% heat after 18 seconds.

## Tips
- Hydrogen can be easily automated by using an Item Transference Device to move Water (from Well or its upgrades) into an extractor (Hand Mill, Extraction Lab, etc.). This will give you 12W with "free" fuel. Solar Panel (which doesn't require fuel to begin with) produces much less.
- Giant trees (Redwood, Penumbral) yield large amounts of fuel when chopped. You can use Chainsaw to chop them faster (and later Harvester Beamgun to chop them instantly).
