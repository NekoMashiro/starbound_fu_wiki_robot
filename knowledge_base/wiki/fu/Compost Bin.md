# [FU] Compost Bin

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Compost_Bin

---

**Compost Bin** is a 16-slots storage. While in this container, any food will rot 10 times faster.

This can be used to quickly obtain Rotten Food for use as Fertilizer in Trays (either directly or after refining it into Mulch). It can also be crafted into Goo.
