# [FU] Cooked meat foods

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Food by diet type | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Cooked_meat_foods

---

These meats are properly cooked, so Omnivores can eat them without being poisoned. Carnivores don't receive a buff that they would receive from raw meat.

 Herbivores are poisoned by eating these foods.
