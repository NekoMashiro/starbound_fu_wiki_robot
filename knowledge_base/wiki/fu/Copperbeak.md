# [FU] Copperbeak

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Farm Beasts, Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Copperbeak

---

**Copperbeak** is a Farm Animal that produces Copper Ore. It grows up from Baby Copperbeak, which hatches from Copperbeak Egg.

- Egg: Hatch time: 1000s if placed on ground, 320s in Incubator. Sold by various NPC traders.
- Baby: doesn't produce ore yet. Hunger time: 10.
- Adult: Mate time: 60; Hunger time: 20
