# [FU] Cosmic Crucible

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Precursor | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cosmic_Crucible

---

**Cosmic Crucible** is a unique device that can upgrade items far beyond their normal stats. They can be found in Underground Precursor biomes, in the Precursor Ruins mission, and in a hidden area of the Science Outpost.

Weapons can be improved from level 1 up to level 10.

Updated as of v5.6.347

The Cosmic Crucible and the tiles below it are impervious to any attempts to remove it. If you somehow remove the Cosmic Crucible, no items will be dropped (not even the anvil).

## Extra Dungeons Upgrade Anvil
Some Extra Dungeons, and the Heaven instance found at the end of in-world dungeons, contain a variant of the Cosmic Crucible, appearing as a red variant of the vanilla [Weapon Upgrade Anvil](https://starbounder.org/Weapon_Upgrade_Anvil). This anvil acts in an identical manner to the Crucible, with the same interface, capabilities, and essence costs.

## Fake Cosmic Crucible
There is also a fake Cosmic Crucible that is rigged with instakilling bees (that can also bypass admin mode) by Kevin. The rigged version is inaccessible by normal means, and can be only spawned in, which immediately releases the bees.
