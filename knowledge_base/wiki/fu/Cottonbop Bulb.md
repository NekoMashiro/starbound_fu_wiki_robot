# [FU] Cottonbop Bulb

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Farm Beasts | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cottonbop_Bulb

---

**Cottonbop Bulb** acts as a crop seed, but when you try to harvest it, it spawns a friendly Cottonbop monster. The seed itself needs to be planted as usual (into tilled soil) and watered.

## Mechanics
- You can periodically interact with these friendly Cottonbops. This will yield Cotton Fibre, Plant Fibre and more Cottonbop Bulbs. It takes 500-900 seconds for Cottonbop to become ready for harvest.
- Unlike the other Farm Beasts, Cottonbops don't need to be fed.
- Friendly Cottonbops don't damage you on contact. They will be hostile to your enemies, and they will attack your enemies. They can be killed by your enemies.
- Cottonbops can fly, and they will move in the same way as the wild ones. They can fly far away from you if you unleash them upon the world.
- You can use Relocator to move friendly Cottonbops.

## Tips
- You can bring several Relocators full of Cottonbops to a boss battle, and unleash them in the boss room.
- For decorative purposes, you can release Cottonbops on Tabula Rasa or Barren planets to make it look like they have animals.
