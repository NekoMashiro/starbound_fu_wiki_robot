# [FU] Crappy Crew Bed

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crappy_Crew_Bed

---

**Crappy Crew Bed**, if placed on BYOS ship, will increase your "maximum crew" limit by +1.

It's incredibly cheap (needs only Iron Bar, which can be obtained on the first Garden planet), but you can only place 4 of these beds. If you try to place the fifth, it will drop as item.

To increase your limit by more than +4, you need to build more advanced (and expensive) beds, such as Crew Bed, Crew Bed (Vat), Crew Bunk (Wall), etc.
