# [FU] Crappy Sprinkler (roof)

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crappy_Sprinkler_(roof)

---

**Crappy Sprinkler** is a cheap substitute for an advanced Sprinkler or Sprinkler (roof). It fires droplets rarely.

These half-broken sprinklers will still water everything, but sometimes a block is non-watered, and it takes them forever to hit it.

## Alternatives
- Aeroponic Tubing and Hydroponic Tubing - these soil blocks are always watered.
- Trays
