# [FU] Creepy Doll

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Creepy_Doll

---

**Creepy Doll** appears to be a ghost girl floating still. She is noticeable in the dark by her blue glow. Once you move close enough to her, she will spin around and fly through the air hitting you with ice damage. If you're hit enough, you will be frozen. She will stay where you are unless you move, then she will fly at you again. She moves quickly when she spots you, so it is suggested to take her out with a ranged weapon before she spots you. She is immune to Physical and Shadow damage and is only truly vulnerable to Cosmic and Fire damage. Once hit, she will stay still and be invulnerable for a short time.

Creepy Dolls live in haunted graveyards and possibly other haunted places.
