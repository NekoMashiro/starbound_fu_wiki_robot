# [FU] Crown of the Idiot

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crown_of_the_Idiot

---

Provides a debuff that gains madness over time. Can be paired with an Atropus world to gain madness twice as fast. It can be purchased from the Tenebrhae (Shadow) merchant at the Science Outpost for 32000 px.
