# [FU] Crystal Erchius Fuel

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Erchius | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Crystal_Erchius_Fuel

---

**Crystal Erchius Fuel** is a vanilla fuel for ships. It can be mined on Moons, but carrying it in the inventory will make you targeted by Erchius Ghost.

It is extremely convenient for fueling the ship in early game, but later it becomes outclassed by cheaper and more potent types of fuel, as well as fuel that doesn't need to be actively mined. It also can't power high-tier reactors (only tier 2 Combustion Generator).
