# [FU] Cultist Mine

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cultist_Mine

---

The Cultist Mine is a rare variation of the regular "Outlaw Mine" mech encounter, a Novakid outlaw mine overrun by the Occassus cult, and bears the title of its uncaptured versions in the cockpit interface. The entrance to this dungeon is on its right side, and so the player must fly left from their spawn position to reach it.

Built inside of an asteroid, the Cultist Mine contains random rooms but in the same layout.
They are populated by Occasus cult members and Cultist Scandroids. In addition to the Cultists, there are Ocumeras, Vampire bats, and standard bats in the connecting tunnels. Contained Novakid Outlaws are also found here.

Similar to Outlaw Mines, there are four patches of ore to mine:
- Two small patches of tungsten are in tunnels connecting rooms. 
- One medium patch of titanium is hidden by a secret passageway.
- One medium patch of diamonds is found in a secret room.

The loot includes random weapons, pixels, mech parts, ores, and more.
The special rewards for completing a Cultist Mine are ancient essence and two mission maps in the final room.
