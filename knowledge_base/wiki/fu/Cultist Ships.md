# [FU] Cultist Ships

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cultist_Ships

---

A rare variation of the Bandit Ships.

The Cultist Ships contain Occassus cult members & Cultist Scandroids.

These ships are still linear but have a different design & color scheme compared to standard Bandit Ships.

Cultist Ships are not randomly generated except for the Cultists and their weapons.

They contain similar loot to the Bandit Ships. The special reward for beating one is the Occassus Mech Body blueprint.
