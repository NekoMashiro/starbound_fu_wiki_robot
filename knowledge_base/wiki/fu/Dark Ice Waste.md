# [FU] Dark Ice Waste

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Ice_Waste

---

**Dark Ice Waste** planets are Ice Wastes that are either very far from their home star, or are orbiting a black star. With no light, there is zero warmth to speak of. Storms are even more frequent, making even the best cold protection useless on these planets. Do not go here without ample equipment. You *will* die.

Blizzards, dark matter storms, cold snaps... why would you come here, again?

On the bright side, they are a good source of Isogen Ore.
