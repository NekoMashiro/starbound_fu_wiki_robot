# [FU] Dark Sulphur

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Sulphur

---

**Dark Sulphur** planet is an even more inhospitable variant of a Sulphuric planet.

Common ores are abundant, and you can even find some Densinium Ore, though in less quantities than on Cyber Spheres.
