# [FU] Desert Moon

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Desert_Moon

---

**Desert Moon** is a cold, airless, sand-filled moon. They are mostly safe and offer a good opportunity to mine Erchius fuel of both kinds without a threat of the ghost, as well as Plutonium. They tend to have low gravity.

Desert moons never appear as main planets - only as satellites. They can be found in hot, habitable, and cold areas of Red, Blue, and Binary stars, as well as in all areas of Dying stars.

## Codex Description
- This desert moon is dry, frozen, dusty and unpopulated. Low temperatures mean you should bring some limited measure of cold protection.
- A lifeless desert moon. Take care, as the temperature is slightly freezing and will eventually prove fatal if you are not cautious.
- A cold, sandy desert moon. A thin layer of cold protection should be sufficient to protect you.

## Protection Required
There's no air on the desert moon, so you will need breathing protection. You will also need low-tier cold protection.

## Notable Features
You can find Crystal Erchius Fuel, Moonstone and Plutonium on desert moons.

There are no crops or trees.
