# [FU] Diamond Lantern

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Diamond_Lantern

---

**Diamond Lantern** is the brightest light-emitting backpack in the game. It provides white light of maximum possible intensity. It also periodically launches an invisible, yet light-emitting sparkle projectile.

Unlike its alternative Xenon Pack (which is possibly more strong in a big open cavern), Diamond Lantern is a radial light (illuminates in all directions equally) and can penetrate the blocks to a degree.
