# [FU] Disruptor Rifle

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disruptor_Rifle

---

A MAGNIFICENT DEVICE TO SEND YOUR ENEMIES TO HIS GRASP, FIRING GREAT BARBULES WHICH SHALT MAKE UNBELIEVERS SUFFER THE CONSEQUENCES.
ALTERNATIVE TRIGGER VOMITS AN ORBULE, WHICH RIPS A HOLE THAT LINGERS IN THIS FALSE UNIVERSE AND VOMITS FORTH FURTHER BARBS FROM HIS SLAVERING MAW.

IT MAY ONLY BE OBTAINED AT A GREAT ALTAR, REQUIRING AN ARTIFACT OF THE ELDERS, FROM THEIR LOST WORLDS. FURTHER INCANTATION IS REQUIRED.
