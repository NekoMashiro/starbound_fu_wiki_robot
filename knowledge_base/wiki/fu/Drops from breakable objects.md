# [FU] Drops from breakable objects

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Mechanics | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Drops_from_breakable_objects

---

**Drops from breakable objects** are items that you get from smashing various bushes, ice crystals, rocks, etc. Most of them can be broken with any weapon, but some require a Matter Manipulator.
