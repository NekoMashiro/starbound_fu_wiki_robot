# [FU] Dungeons

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dungeons

---

Adds a plethora of new dungeons, and even modifies from existing ones. Here is a list of dungeons FU adds or modifies.

Note: This list does not include microdungeons or space encounters. See their sections for those.

### Modified Vanilla
#### Quest Dungeons
These dungeons are Vanilla dungeons/quests that Frackin Universe makes significant changes to. These can include hidden areas, extra spawns, or even a complete overhaul.

- The Erchius Mining Facility : Complete and total overhaul, new enemy types, etc.

- The Grand Pagoda Library : More enemies and more variety of enemies.

- The Baron's Keep : More enemies, layout changes. Secrets.

- The Dantalion : More enemies.

#### Planetside Macrodungeons
- Cultist Lair : Brought into circulation, populated, given shield protection.

- USCM Bunker : Restored into circulation, populated with Frackin Enemies.

- SciFi Dungeon : Restored into circulation, changed from a stargazer base to a miniknog one.

- Apex Research Lab : Restored into circulation.

- Apex Test Chamber : Restored into circulation.
