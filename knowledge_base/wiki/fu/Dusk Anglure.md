# [FU] Dusk Anglure

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dusk_Anglure

---

The **Dusk Anglure** is a medium sized creature akin to it's relative the [Anglure](https://starbounder.org/Anglure). Adapted to environments of twisting shadows and lightless days, this creature also hunts through deception using it's bio-luminescent stalk atop its head to lure in prey. There are not many lights in the areas these creatures live, and so the presence of their bio-luminescence attracts all sorts of creatures to them - easy hunting. Be wary of a glowing light in the middle of a dead of night for it might just be your end rather than your salvation.

The Dusk Anglure fights by vomiting torrents of black tar and muck at a creature, likely the extracted soils and left over chunks of previous meals. This attack does well to blind a target long enough for the Dusk Anglure to unleash a blast of swirling blue energy, with enough force to damage blocks. While not the most dangerous of attacks, it is when you are attacked at night, and at your most desperate when these creatures tend to strike. This is what makes an Anglure so deadly..

## Luna's Lab Notes
~ The Dusk Anglure only deals normal damage, not any type of elemental damage which is surprising given it's attack types and the areas in which they live.

~ The Dusk Anglure lets off an eerie light blue glow that has a similar shining pattern as flashlights in game. Extraordinarily creepy in dark environments.
