*Shorthand for "Enviro Protection Pack."*

## Summary
These items, worn on the back, negate or lessen environmental Status Effects. Only one EPP can be equipped at any given time - however, they can modified with an Augment and/or paired with Armor to balance resistances. Most craftable EPPs are made at an Armorworks and its upgrades.

Note: There is a difference between *dangerous* and *deadly*; for example, a Heating EPP will not work for Deadly Cold, nor will it provide breathing.

## Vanilla EPPs
Note that vanilla EPPs' functionalities in Frackin' Universe are different from that in vanilla *Starbound.*
