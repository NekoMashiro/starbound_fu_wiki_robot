# [FU] Energiflower

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energiflower

---

**Energiflower** is an easy-to-obtain low-tier fuel for high-tech Quantum Reactor (providing 80 power, while the best fuel, Liquid Deuterium, provides 620). Unlike other fuels, this is a growable plant, so it can be obtained with rather low level of technology (without the need for Arc Smelter or advanced centrifuges).
