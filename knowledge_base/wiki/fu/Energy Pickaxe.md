# [FU] Energy Pickaxe

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Pickaxe

---

**Energy Pickaxe** is a non-craftable Pickaxe. It deals 8 tile damage (per hit) to 4x4 area. It has infinite durability, but uses 5 energy per hit. 

Despite its impressive stats, this pickaxe is almost never useful by the time you find it (tier 5+ worlds only), because it gets outclassed by craftable Morphite Manipulator (which deals 12 tile damage).
