# [FU] Engineering tree

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Engineering_tree

---

**Engineering tree** allows you to research STL/FTL drive and other ship/mech parts, extractors, tools like Mining Laser or Hookshot, EPP Augments, teleporters, terraformers and high-tech crafting materials.
