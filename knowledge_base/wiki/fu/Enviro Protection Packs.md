# [FU] Enviro Protection Packs

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Enviro_Protection_Packs

---

These items are worn on the back and they negate or lessen environmental Status Effects. Only one EPP can be equipped at any given time, however, they can be modified with an Augment and/or paired with Armor to balance resistances. Most craftable EPPs are made at an Anvil/Forge/Replicator, but several are instead made at a Spinning Wheel/Sewing Machine/Clothing Fabricator or a Foraging Table/Agricultural Station while others are acquired as loot.

Note: There is a difference between dangerous and deadly; for example, a Heating EPP will not work for Deadly Cold, nor will it provide breathing. Vanilla EPP's functionalities in Frackin' Universe are different from that in vanilla Starbound.

Additionally, EPP Augments can be added to EPP's for additional effects.
