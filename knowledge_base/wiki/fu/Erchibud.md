# [FU] Erchibud

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Farm Beasts, Imported, Erchius | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Erchibud

---

**Erchibud** is a Farm Animal that produces Crystal Erchius Fuel. It grows up from Erchibud Baby, which hatches from Erchibud Egg.

- Egg: Hatch time: 1000s if placed on ground, 500s in Incubator. Can be bought at Terramart. Can also be found in the end of Erchius Mining Facility mission. 
- Baby: doesn't produce anything. Hunger time: 20 minutes.
- Adult: hunger time: 22 minutes. Has a special Lunar diet, see List of Farm Beast Foods for details.

Unlike normal animals, Erchibud produces Erchius Crystal instead of Poop. It also produces Liquid Erchius Fuel instead of Water.
