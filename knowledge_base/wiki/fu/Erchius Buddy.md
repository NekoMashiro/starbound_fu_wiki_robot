# [FU] Erchius Buddy

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Erchius_Buddy

---

**Erchius Buddy** is an EPP that protects you from Erchius Ghost.

You can find it as loot if you defeat the boss of Erchius Mining Facility with your own weapons (without using the giant laser in the boss room). Beware that the boss is exceptionally tanky, so crafting this EPP is easier than gearing up for the boss.
