# [FU] Erchius Ghost (Gas Giant)

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Erchius | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Erchius_Ghost_(Gas_Giant)

---

**Gas Giant Ghost** is a stronger variant of Erchius Ghost. 
- It appears on Gas Giants if you carry Effigium Ore, Solarium Ore, Densinium Ore, Irradium Ore, or Trianglium Ore.
- Any immunities to normal Erchius Ghost will also help against the Gas Giant Ghost.
