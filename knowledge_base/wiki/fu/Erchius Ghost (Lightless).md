# [FU] Erchius Ghost (Lightless)

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Erchius | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Erchius_Ghost_(Lightless)

---

**Lightless Ghost** is a stronger variant of Erchius Ghost. 

- It appears on Lightless and Shadow Moon planets if you carry any Effigium Ore, Crystal Erchius Fuel or Liquid Erchius Fuel.
- Any immunities to normal Erchius Ghost will also help against the Lightless Ghost.
