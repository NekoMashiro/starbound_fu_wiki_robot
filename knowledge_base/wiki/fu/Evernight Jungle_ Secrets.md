# [FU] Evernight Jungle: Secrets

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Evernight_Jungle:_Secrets

---

There's 3 locked room scattered through the Evernight Jungle, 2 of them located in the Underground Path and the last one at the very end of the dungeon.

## Locked door at the end of the dungeon
This door might actually be open for you. Unlike most other doors in extra dungeons, you don't need to find a hidden switch in the dungeon to open it. This door actually closes if you are a heartless monster. Namely, if you killed the cat in the second part of the dungeon to grab those few pixels. Karma.

## Locked door in the ruins
To open the locked room inside the Underground Path, you will need to activate a console hidden in the Frog Village. Head to the far end of the Village to the right. You will find a small passage inside the cliff that you can enter with your Sphere tech. It will lead you to a small cave with the console and a chest.

After activating this console, head back to the Underground Path. At the end of the part inside the ruins, before reaching the area guarded by the Frogs/Cultists, you will find the door to be open now. However, you will still need to cross a room full of traps to reach the goodies. You will first just need to make your way through some stationary saws. Then you will need to dodge more saws, but this time boulders are rolling toward you and you will need to jump above those aswell. The last part should be easier: press the button on the wall to release some gas that make you go faster, and roun through the hallway while jumping above the saws. Lasers will start fallinf from the roof so don't stop. You will then reach the room with the goodies, including 2 Ancient Essence chests. 

## Locked door in the Frog/Cultist area
Above the previous locked door, you will find an area defended by Frogs/Cultists with a house with a locked door. To open this door, dive into the well to the right of the area and activate the switch at the bottom. The well is guarded by a single Frog. Then head back to the house to find the door open. Don't get your expecation to high, this room only has Pixels dispensers and a fridge.
