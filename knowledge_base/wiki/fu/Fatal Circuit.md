# [FU] Fatal Circuit

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fatal_Circuit

---

Fatal Circuits are seen by many as one of the most technologically advanced examples of cybernetic engineering to date. A specimen that has been through several stages of development and testing, it's organic brain now only controls lower functions, as it's empathy and emotions have shown to get in the way of commands and system functioning when confronted with moral dilemmas.

This cyborgs creation can be traced back to a highly dedicated team that many can pay homage too today for their hard work and dedication to the project. The lead designer of the project is [Tiy](https://starbounder.org/Tiy) - An individual seeking to make a dream come true. A project made tangible by programmers - [Kyren](https://starbounder.org/Kyren), [Tom](https://starbounder.org/Tom), [Metadept](https://starbounder.org/Metadept), [Healthire](https://starbounder.org/Healthire) and made visible by the skilled team of designers and artists including - [GeorgeV](https://starbounder.org/GeorgeV), [Supernorn](https://starbounder.org/Supernorn), [SamuriFerret](https://starbounder.org/SamuriFerret), [Neco](https://starbounder.org/Neco), [Lili Ibrahim](https://starbounder.org/Lili_Ibrahim), and [Abi](https://starbounder.org/Abi). Truly a dream team.

Augmented with powerful weapons, the Fatal Circuit possesses three primary means of attack. The first method of attack is using its enhanced, durasteel reinforced claws to tear foes apart. When operating at full power, these claws can punch through outpost walls and tear holes in tougher metals with the force behind the attacks. Secondly, the Fatal Circuit is capable of discharging stored power, shocking any creature near it, and then firing a ball of electricity at distant targets. Lastly, the Fatal Circuit will open the outside layer of metal located on it's neck region, and fire a quick boomerang shaped explosive. This attack can not only travel through creatures, but also deals significant damage, moves quickly, and explodes with enough force to destroy softer materials and blocks.

They have been found on Cyber Sphere worlds and rarely in abandoned labs.
