# [FU] Faulty Erchius Converter

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Precursor, Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Faulty_Erchius_Converter

---

The **Faulty Erchius Converter** is a broken version of the Erchius Converter that looks virtually identical to the real thing, but it instead turns fuel into Bags of Farts and the latter into Crunchy Chicks.

It has a small chance to be in Precursor chests (which exist in Precursor biomes and Precursor mission).
