# [FU] Fd:Biomes

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fd:Biomes

---

Numerous biomes are added through Frackin' Universe, in addition to the standard biomes included in Starbound. Many biomes (such as Aether Sea, Atropus, and Super Dense) have rare or unique effects not seen in the base game. Therefore, many of these biomes will require specialized equipment in order for the player to safely explore them.
