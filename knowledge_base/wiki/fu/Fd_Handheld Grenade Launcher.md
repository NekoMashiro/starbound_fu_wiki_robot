**Handheld Grenade Launcher **is a one-handed procedurally generated ranged weapon model.

It can be common, uncommon, and rare. Common Handheld Grenade Launchers will shoot explosive grenades, while uncommon and rare ones will shoot elemental grenades.

Note: though Grenade Launchers (re-named to Handheld Grenade Launchers in Frackin' Universe) are in vanilla *Starbound*, their characteristics are modified significantly in Frackin' Universe. For the original characteristics, consult the official *Starbound* wiki, Starbounder (http://starbounder.org/).

## Base Statistics
- **Base DPS: **6.0-6.5 health/s (Common), 6.5-7.0 health/s (Uncommon/Rare)
- **Fire Time: **1.5-4.0 seconds between shots (~0.3-0.7 shots/s)
- **Energy Usage: **21.5-22.75 energy/s (Common), 22.75-24.5 energy/s (Uncommon/Rare)
- **Grenade Types:** Impact, Bouncy, Sticky, Proximity Mine
- **Knockback: **30 (strong knockback)
- **Crit Chance: **5% (Common/Uncommon), 6% (Rare)
- **Base Crit Damage Bonus: **+6 damage (Common), +8 damage (Uncommon/Rare)

## Elemental Damage
Uncommon and rare Handheld Grenade Launchers are assigned one of seven elements (non-physical damage types) on generation. For all elements, only rare Handheld Grenade Launchers will inflict the appropriate elemental status effect in addition to dealing elemental damage.
- **Fire:** Inflicts Burning
- **Ice:** Inflicts Frost
- **Poison:** Inflicts Poisoned
- **Electric:** Inflicts Electrified
- **Radiation:** Inflicts Radiation Burn
- **Shadow:** Inflicts Shadow Gas
- **Cosmic:** Inflicts -Armor debuff

## Special Attacks
Handheld Grenade Launchers, like most one-handed weapon types, cannot generate with special attacks.
