# [FU] Fd:Mini-Rocket Launcher

**Mini-Rocket Launcher** is a two-handed procedurally generated ranged weapon model.

It can be common, uncommon, and rare. Common Mini-Rocket Launchers will shoot explosive rockets, while uncommon and rare ones will shoot elemental explosive plasma rockets.

## Base Statistics
- **Base DPS: **8.0-9.0 health/s (Common), 8.5-9.5 health/s (Uncommon/Rare)
- **Fire Time: **0.5-1.5 seconds between shots (~0.7-2.0 shots/s)
- **Energy Usage: **32.0-36.0 energy/s (Common), 34.0-38.0 energy/s (Uncommon/Rare)
- **Knockback: **20 (average knockback)
- **Crit Chance: **2%
- **Base Crit Damage Bonus: **+4 damage (Common), +5 damage (Uncommon), +6 damage (Rare)

## Elemental Damage
Uncommon and rare Mini-Rocket Launchers are assigned one of seven elements (non-physical damage types) on generation. Both uncommon and rare variants will inflict status effects with special attacks. For all elements, only rare Mini-Rocket Launchers will inflict the appropriate elemental status effect with the primary attack.
- **Fire:** Inflicts Burning
- **Ice:** Inflicts Frost
- **Poison:** Inflicts Poisoned
- **Electric:** Inflicts Electrified
- **Radiation:** Inflicts Radiation Burn
- **Shadow:** Inflicts Shadow Gas
- **Cosmic:** Inflicts -Armor debuff
