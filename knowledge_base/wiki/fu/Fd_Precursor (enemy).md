The precursor enemy is a hostile npc of the precursor race that acts as a hostile guard and will attack the player on sight.

It will always wear the precursor armor set, and can be found using any precursor weapon randomly.

They have a base energy value of 110, and a base protection value of 1.

## Drops
It will drop from the same pool as precursor treasure, meaning any single precursor npc may drop the same as what would spawn in the ruins's chest.

## Fighting
Avoid close range combat in general, as melee types deal a lot of physical damage and ranged types may have high alpha damage radioactive weapons.
