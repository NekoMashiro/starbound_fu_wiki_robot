# [FU] Fd:Strange Maps

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fd:Strange_Maps

---

Strange maps can be found across the galaxy in various dungeons or chests, each pointing to a side mission. Once placed on a wall if a new mission is present, the map will gain a quest marker. Simply interact with the map to obtain the side quest. A bit of warning however they are **non-reusable**, mining the map results in soggy paper so once placed don't move it unless you intend to destroy it.
