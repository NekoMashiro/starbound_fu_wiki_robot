# [FU] Fd:Transwarp Transmitter

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fd:Transwarp_Transmitter

---

The transwarp transmitter is a precursor object uncommonly found in precursor minibiomes, usually the underground ones. If it is activated, when interacted, it can be used to teleport into the precursor ruins mission instance, but this mission will not be saved into your mission interface until you reach a certain point of this dungeon.

If broken, it will not drop as a object, therefore one may lose his chance to enter the dungeon.
