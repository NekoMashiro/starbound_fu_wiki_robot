# [FU] Fear Moth

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fear_Moth

---

**Fear Moth**s are moths that have grown to massive proportions and gained a taste for the flesh of creatures. The once adorable fuzzy creatures who’s biggest threat was destroying clothes has now become a less-capable hunter.

Small brown moth, with a greyish pink abdomen. Possesses a slow methodical flight speed when compared to many other creatures. After attacking a target, the Fear Moth will land for a short time, seemingly drinking in the blood that the creature has spilled. This is shown through the red particle effect during the moth’s landing and the motions the creature makes. Additionally, the Fear Moth is only capable of prolonged flight for about 5 seconds, at which point the creature must land and regain it's stamina.

Relatively harmless, this creature proves to be more of an annoyance than a threat. As a Fear Moth’s low health, relatively low speed, lackluster attacks, and the fact after striking a target 2-3 times it lands and remains stationary for moments at a time, make this creature a poor hunter.
