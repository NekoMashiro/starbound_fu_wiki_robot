# [FU] Feed-Munity Chip

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Feed-Munity_Chip

---

**Feed-Munity Chip** enables you to ignore your diet type, allowing carnivores to eat plants, herbivores to eat meat, omnivores to eat raw meat, non-robots to eat robot food, etc. without experiencing food poisoning or other penalties.

It doesn't allow you to eat pet food.

## Trivia
- In the real life, carnivores aren't actually poisoned by a food solely because it's plant-based. They will eat grass, etc. on occasion. Being a carnivore means that eating plants doesn't provide the body with essential nutrients to sustain it (so a carnivore will still starve if it only eats plant-based food, no matter how much of it).
