# [FU] Firetop

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Firetop

---

Poptops are a unique monster type, found generally in tranquil environments. They wander around aimlessly whistling to themselves, but when the player draws near they get aggressive, attacking with leaping bites and now, ranged highly explosive fireball projectiles. Poptops are astoundingly adaptive, and have shown themselves to be quite resilient when faced with even the most extreme environments. Poptops are native to worlds of forest and tranquil environments, however due to the high demand for these creatures as pets, they have been spread to many worlds and planets they otherwise never would have been introduced too. This introduction of Poptops into different environments has lead to the recorded extinction of 300+ indigenous species, countless other wildlife, and lead to numerous biological adaptations and evolution within the species.

A fiery version of the Poptop, a Firetop can be identified by the sparks of fire and smoke billowing from it’s body. The Firetop launches two large fireball like projectiles that burst into several motes of fire upon impact. Motes of fire created this way continue to burn for several seconds after the initial attack, making dangerous terrain for the inattentive. Anything unfortunate enough to get caught in blast of a Firetop’s fireballs become afflicted with the “Burning” debuff, along with “glow” and are visibly on fire and emitting light for a short time.
