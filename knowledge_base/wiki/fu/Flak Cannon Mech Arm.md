# [FU] Flak Cannon Mech Arm

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flak_Cannon_Mech_Arm

---

The Flak Cannon Mech Arm is a simple mech arm that fires bullets at a slightly slow fire rate.

The cannon fires one projectile at a time at a constant rate when the mouse is held down.
