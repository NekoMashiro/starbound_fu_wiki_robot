# [FU] Flesh Reaper (red)

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Flesh_Reaper_(red)

---

An unusual and deadly eldritch abomination. Whether this creature is a doomed spirit twisted by the unknown, or cursed aberration is unknown. A creature of two realms, the Flesh Reaper shifts between the material plane, and a realm that is dark, cold, and foreign to the memories of the few that have gazed into it and lived. Held together by pulsing materials of unknown origins, the air around this creature seems to shape and form with deadly intent. The energy from which this creature pulls it's power is foreign to all that have attempted to study these apparitions. Preferring to hover close to the atmosphere of a planet, these creatures appear to strike at a target when they are vulnerable.

Capable of quick flight, this creature can track down and strike at targets found in the open in which they float. The projectiles fired from this creature have surprising range, able to hit targets with decent accuracy from a distance. (Gif depicting attacks below) Additionally, these attacks bounce and so fighting in close quarters can prove to be more dangerous than at range. The second primary attack, is a gap-closing slash. This attack pulls the Flesh Reaper closer to a target, has a large hitbox due to it's absurdly long animation, and can be used in quick succession when in close combat. Lastly, this creature can release "clouds of suffering" that deal high damage to nearby targets.

**Flesh Reapers** can be found on Eldritch Field minibiomes, Atropus worlds, Elder Core, Flesh Blobs, Gas GIants along with ***Gas Bag***, and the Delta Freya II mission. They may also be encountered as targets of various NPC quests.
