The **Fool Cook** is a unique Glitch NPC that can be encountered at Burger Fools in space. They can also be encountered as Tenants.

Their stock consists primarily of fast-food themed decorative items, the Plating Station and Cheeseburger. Like some FU-specific merchants, they may also carry Oranges.

## Tips
- The furniture required to attract a Fool Cook is sold at Frogg's Furnishing in The Outpost.
