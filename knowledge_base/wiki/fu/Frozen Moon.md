# [FU] Frozen Moon

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Frozen_Moon

---

**Frozen Moon** is a variant of Moon that has Moderate Cold, but doesn't have an Erchius Ghost.

## Codex Description
- A frozen moon. Watch your footing, and beware dangerous wildlife. Abundant telebrium ore detected. Recommend: Heating EPP II.
- A frigid, frozen moon. Slippery surfaces could prove hazardous. Abundant telebrium ore detected. Recommend: Heating EPP II.

## Notable Features
You can find Crystal Erchius Fuel, Moonstone, Iron Ore, Tungsten Ore, Gold Ore, Telebrium Crystals, Copper Ore, Crystals and Silver Ore on frozen moons.

There are no crops or trees.

1 out of 6 Frozen Moon planets has an abnormally high daylight level, resulting in very high output from Solar Panels (7.4W instead of 4W).
