# [FU] Gaze Lemon

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gaze_Lemon

---

A relative of the Oculemon, the **Gaze Lemon** is an imperishable, consumable food item, with the iris said to be the 'best part'. Consuming one grants +10% Poison resistance and immunity to the Poisoned status for 90 seconds, and causes one's eyes to emit light for 120 seconds.

## Notes
The light emission effect works like a headlamp, where a narrow cone of light is shone from the player's eye level in the direction that they are facing. This cone of light does not track the mouse cursor, so its actual practicality is quite limited especially in underground or indoor areas with variations in elevation. It is easily obstructed by terrain and completely fails to illuminate anything behind the user.
