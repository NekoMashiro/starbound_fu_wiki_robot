# [FU] Ghost Lantern

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ghost_Lantern

---

**Ghost Lantern** is a flashlight that repels Erchius Ghost, but only when you are holding it in your hand.

This item is a random drop. Don't sell it: you need one for a quest.
