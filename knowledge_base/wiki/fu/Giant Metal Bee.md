# [FU] Giant Metal Bee

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Giant_Metal_Bee

---

The Giant Metal Bee is one of many types of flying insectoids, that make up this category of creature, this one being quite uncommon! This variety is noted by it's steely grey body, and relatively large size.

When it charges, the bee swoops at a target surprisingly fast, striking for overall small damage, but leaving the target with a unique debuff called "Sting". This debuff inflicts radiation damage overtime similar to poison, and causes the player to glow brightly for a few seconds.

The **Giant Metal Bee** is a unique monster that can be found in Metal Hive and Metal Jungle biomes, and they are indeed catchable.

## Luna's Lab Notes
~ This monster has a much higher range of tracking when compared to other creatures (a range of 50 compared to the average of 20-30). This meaning that the Infected Queen will pursue players for longer, and from further away.

~ Dealing radiation damage, this creature can inflict a serious wound when in a group and facing a creature not resistant to radiation damage.
