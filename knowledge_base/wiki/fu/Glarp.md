# [FU] Glarp

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Glarp

---

**Glarp** is a crystalline variation to the more common Gleap. More capable in combat than the Gleap, possessing natural regenerative ability and swifter movements. It can also summon a temporary Healing Zone (heals anyone who stands inside, including the player).

Capable of mild contact damage, and small amounts of damage getting pounced on by this creature is nothing to take note of. The leaping radius and jump height of the Glarp is far improved when compared to it's Gleap relative. However, this increase in speed, is not matched by an increase in power or might. The most outstanding quality this creature contains is it's resiliency, and steadfast regenerative capabilities - a will to not die.

**Glarp** is a unique monster found on Crystalline, Mountainous and Bog biomes
