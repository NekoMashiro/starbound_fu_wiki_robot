# [FU] Glump

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Glump

---

**Glump** is a unique and quirky variation to the more common [Gleap](https://starbounder.org/Gleap) More comical in combat than the Gleap, possessing the ability to bounce around frantically, and inflict a similar fate upon the creatures they crash into. Quicker than the Gleap, the Glump uses a unique ability to secrete a hyper rubbery substance that repels like a magnet from any surface it comes into contact with. The substance dries and loses effect in seconds, however the Glump has no shortage in the amount it can produce. This substance is highly prized by alchemists and researches - as it can be used to make several types of noteworthy potions and alchemical products. The most common, is the primary ingrediant in the bouncy morph ball tech, and also bounce potions. These potions have not only saved the lives of many space faring adventureres, but also fetch a fair price on the market for anyone brave enough to contain and harvest these creatures. 

Capable of inflicting the bouncy debuff, combat is less deadly and more fun when encountering this creature. With more health, and slightly more viscous attacks than the common Gleap, the Glump still posses a more dangerous threat than it's relative. Especially when confronted in an enclosed area, or atop a drop-off in which the creature could launch you.
