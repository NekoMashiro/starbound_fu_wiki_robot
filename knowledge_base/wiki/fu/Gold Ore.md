# [FU] Gold Ore

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gold_Ore

---

**Gold Ore** is a tier 1 ore that exists on almost every planet, but very-very deep (almost never near the surface). On all vanilla planets, there is a very high chance to find it in Underground3 layer (directly above the core). Planets that have Gold Ore in Surface layer are Cyber Sphere and Frozen Moon (as well as Bog, Gelatinous and Rainforest, but in much smaller quantities).
