# [FU] Harvester Turret

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Mechanics | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Harvester_Turret

---

**Harvester Turret** will fire at nearby fully grown plants. When hit, these plants will yield their normal harvest items (e.g. Corn and Plant Fibre from a Corn plant). 

Obtained items will remain on the ground. You can use a Grabber if you want to automatically store them in a container.

## List of turrets
- Harvester Turret v1 - range 8 tiles.
- Harvester Turret v2 - range 16 tiles.
- Harvester Turret v3 - range 32 tiles.

## Other mechanics
- The turret needs an unobstructed line of vision to mature plants.
- You can wire something like Iron Lever to the turret's to enable/disable it. By default (when nothing is wired to it) the turret is always enabled.
- of the turret will send "on" signal whenever the turret is actively harvesting.
