# [FU] Heavy Drone

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heavy_Drone

---

Large micro-boss enemies found in space encounters and certain missions. They are not capturable.

In vanilla, they circle around you and fire a burst of 5 plasma rounds out of each arm. In Frackin' Universe, their weaponry is more varied. They may be armed with two of the following:

- 5 burst plasma (vanilla default)
- Homing Missile (single shot, high damage)
- 3 burst flak gun (more damage but take longer to reload and are less accurate)
- Ice thrower (low range)
- Flame Thrower (low range)
- Mini Rocket (5 round burst)
- Spread Bullet (a spread of 3 bullets used by the Omnicannon in a spread config. (single shot)

There is a precursor variant called the Heavy War Drone that uses different weapons.
