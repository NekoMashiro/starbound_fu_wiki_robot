# [FU] Heck Altar

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heck_Altar

---

**Heck Altar** is a storage container with 16 slots.

Despite looking similar to Elder Altar, the Heck Altar doesn't have any hidden functions. It's no different from any other chest.
