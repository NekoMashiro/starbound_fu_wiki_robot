# [FU] Hemogolem

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hemogolem

---

The **Hemogolem** are similar construct creatures to their counterpart the Po Golems. However, rather than fecal matter making up their being, they are instead composed of organic, animated blood and viscera. Arguably less dangerous due to their inability to create minions, and their lack of status infliction and overall damage when compared to their s***ty counterparts. In the case of the Hemogolem it has two attacks; the first is a close ranged attack involving vomiting up waves of gore and blood. The other attack is swinging its arm and releasing a short ranged arc attack in a straight line that reaches only a few feet, but inflicts the -20% Health debuff. This status lasts for a couple minutes and can be quite troublesome in the dangerous environments that they live. More mobile than other Pogolem variants, the Hemogolem is able to jump slightly off the ground.
