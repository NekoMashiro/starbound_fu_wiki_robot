# [FU] High-Caliber Pistol

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/High-Caliber_Pistol

---

**High-Caliber Pistol** is a one-handed procedurally generated ranged weapon model.

It can be common, uncommon, and rare. Common High-Caliber Pistols will shoot bullets, while uncommon and rare ones will shoot elemental plasma shots.

## Base Statistics
- **Base DPS:** 6.5-7.5 health/s (Common), 7.0-8.0 health/s (Uncommon/Rare)
- **Fire Time:** 1.0-2.5 seconds between shots (0.4-1.0 shots/s)
- **Energy Usage:** 19.5-22.5 energy/s (Common), 21.0-24.0 energy/s (Uncommon/Rare)
- **Knockback:** 10-20 (weak to average knockback)
- **Crit Chance:** 2% (Common), 3% (Uncommon), 4% (Rare)
- **Base Crit Damage Bonus:** +4 damage (Common), +5 damage (Uncommon), +6 damage (Rare)

## Elemental Damage
Uncommon and rare High-Caliber Pistols are assigned one of seven elements (non-physical damage types) on generation. Though both uncommon and rare Beam Pistols exhibit elemental visuals, only rare High-Caliber Pistols inflict status effects.

- Fire: Inflicts Burning
- Ice: Inflicts Frost
- Poison: Inflicts Poisoned
- Electric: Inflicts Electrified
- Radiation: Inflicts Radiation Burn
- Shadow: Inflicts Shadow Gas
- Cosmic: Inflicts Armor

## Special Attacks
High-Caliber Pistols, like most one-handed weapon types, do not possess any special attacks.
