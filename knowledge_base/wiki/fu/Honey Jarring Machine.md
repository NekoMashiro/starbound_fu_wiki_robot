# [FU] Honey Jarring Machine

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Honey_Jarring_Machine

---

**Honey Jarring Machine** converts honeycombs (such as Forest Honeycomb) into consumable jars (such as Shell Honey Jar). It takes 1 second to produce 1 jar. It doesn't require Power.

It has the same outputs as less advanced Honey Extractor (see **full list of extractions**), but works 2 times faster.

## About the tooltip
Despite what its tooltip suggests, Honey Jarring Machine has nothing to do with Industrial Centrifuge (there is no reason to "connect" them). These two buildings just look nice when placed next to each other.
