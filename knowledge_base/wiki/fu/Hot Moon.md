# [FU] Hot Moon

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hot_Moon

---

**Hot Moon** is a class of moon, instances of which are found in the scorched and hot orbital regions around most stars. It includes moons of type Chromatic, Strange Sea, Desert Moon, Volcanic Moon, Toxic Moon, Rocky Moon, Desert, Cyber Sphere, Tar Ball, Mountainous, Fungal, Moon, Barren, Infernus, Magma, Volcanic, Sulphuric, Red Desert, and Tropical Ocean.
