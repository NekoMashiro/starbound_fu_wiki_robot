# [FU] Icebobb

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Icebobb

---

The **Icebobb** is an intangible spirit, similar to it's kin the [Wisper](https://starbounder.org/Wisper) both capable of passing through solid blocks. The Icebobb constantly drips glistening hyper-cooled ectoplasm like liquid from it's body. When attacking, they swell up and float in different directions or strait at you after they fire. They shoot balls of ice that are accompanied by clouds of ice upon release. Getting hit by the "snowballs" gives you the [Deadly Chill](https://starbounder.org/Deadly_Chill). status while the body of the Icebobb gives Def -20%

Icebobb is based off of Will-o'-wisps.
