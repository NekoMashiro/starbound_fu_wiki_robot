# [FU] Inspired Painting

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Inspired_Painting

---

**Inspired Painting** is a usable item. It turns into a random *painting* (decorative placeable object), including Madness-giving paintings and random **quest maps**.

A less advanced Painting will only produce normal (purely decorative) paintings.
