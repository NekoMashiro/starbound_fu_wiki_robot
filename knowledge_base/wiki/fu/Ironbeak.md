# [FU] Ironbeak

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Farm Beasts | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ironbeak

---

**Ironbeak** is a Farm Animal that produces Iron Ore. It grows up from Baby Ironbeak, which hatches from Ironbeak Egg.

- Egg: Hatch time: 1000s if placed on ground, 520s in Incubator. Sold by various NPC traders.
- Baby: doesn't produce ore yet. Hunger time: 10.
- Adult: Mate time: 65; Hunger time: 22
