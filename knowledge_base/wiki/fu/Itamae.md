**Itamae** are unique NPCs, artisanal Hylotl chefs that prepare and serve their race's fine cuisine. They can be encountered as Tenants.

Despite appearances, an Itamae's stock is almost entirely decorative. The only consumable Food item they carry is Sushi.

## Tips
- The furniture required to attract an Itamae tenant are sold at Frogg's Furnishing in The Outpost.
