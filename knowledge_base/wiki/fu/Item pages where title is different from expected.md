# [FU] Item pages where title is different from expected

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Pages with problems | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Item_pages_where_title_is_different_from_expected

---

This category is auto-populated with articles about items for which says that the title of the article is incorrect.

This typically happens happens when the item was renamed in-game (e.g. when "Snowberry" was renamed into Snowberry Seed, the page "Snowberry" appeared in this category), in which case the article with old name should be either deleted or redirected to the new article name.

Note to admins: **don't delete this category** even if it's currently empty.
