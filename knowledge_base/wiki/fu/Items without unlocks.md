# [FU] Items without unlocks

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Items_without_unlocks

---

This category is automatically populated with item pages which have , but are NOT unlocked by anything (neither by anything in Research Tree nor by picking up some item).

Note: there are items actually **don't need unlocks**:
- Items which have their blueprints as random drop from monsters,
- Elder/Precursor items,
- Race-specific items,
- Items sold in shops (almost every shop except Hiring Office doesn't require unlocks).

Note to admins: **don't delete this category** even if it's currently empty.
