# [FU] Kill Pod by Kevin-Tek

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kill_Pod_by_Kevin-Tek

---

**Kill Pod by Kevin-Tek** is rewarded by Kevin for completing one of his quests.

When this item is used, player gets hit with 100 points of Cosmic damage for up to 30 seconds. This damage is not reduced by defense stat of your armor.

## Trivia
- Despite what the tooltip says, if you survive this item, you don't get a refund. But no customer has ever complained about it.
