## Extra Properties
Shots from a Kirhosi Blaster will *both* pierce targets *and* inflict knockback. This, combined with the fact that the weapon does ***not*** use the player's energy bar, makes the Kirhosi Blaster a very effective crowd control weapon, *especially* if dual-wielded and upgraded.
