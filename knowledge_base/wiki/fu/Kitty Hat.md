# [FU] Kitty Hat

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kitty_Hat

---

**Kitty Hat** is non-craftable decorative helmet with no bonuses. It can be found behind a [challenge door](https://starbounder.org/Challenge_Door) with "choose what animal to save: the rabbit or the cat" scenario (saving the cat rewards 1 Kitty Hat).

It is a known fact that cats always land on their feet. Theoretically, were this power to be harnessed, infinite energy could be generated...

See also: The Un-Cat Hat.
