# [FU] Lab Directory

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lab_Directory

---

**Lab Directory** is an in-game mini-wiki. It allows you to see extractions, siftings, smeltings, liquid mixings. It doesn't show crafting recipes or unconventional ways of obtaining items (animals, etc.).

For example, Lab Directory can tell you "what can Moonstone be processed into" or "from what can I get Salt". It only shows recipes for machinery that is placed nearby (if you have a Sifter nearby, it will show siftings, etc.).

When you start a new game, you already have one Lab Directory in your shiplocker. There is also a usable Lab Directory at the Science Outpost.

## Font colors of inputs/outputs
- When searching "As input": items listed in **Red** are produced in very small quantities, **Orange** items are less rare, **Yellow** appear even more frequently, and **Green** are quite common.
- When searching "As output": items listed in **Red** are most unlikely to produce material you are looking for, while **Green** will produce a lot of it. If it is light green, it will produce more than if it is dark green.

## Functions
Currently, it can find the extraction recipes of the following extraction devices:

> Extractors
- Quantum Extractor
- Extraction Lab MKII
- Extraction Lab
- Hand Mill
> Gene extractors 
- Advanced Xenolab
- Xeno Research Lab
> Centrifuges
- Gas Centrifuge
- Lab Centrifuge
- Industrial Centrifuge
- Iron Centrifuge
- Wooden Centrifuge
> Sifters
- Powder Sifter
- Sifter
- Rock Crusher
- Rock Breaker
- Precursor Forge
> Smelting machines
- Arc Smelter
- Blast Furnace
- Electric Furnace
> Other machines
- Liquid Mixer
- Autopsy Table

## Note
- Recipes of Sprouting Table won't be shown.
- Any mixing recipes with Essentia Obscura are shown as "??? & ??? = ???". Essentially, they are obscured.
