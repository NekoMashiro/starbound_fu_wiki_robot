# [FU] Large Fusion Reactor

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Large_Fusion_Reactor

---

**Large Fusion Reactor** is an improved of Small Fusion Reactor. It produces more power from the same fuels. 

This building is very large (much larger than Small Fusion Reactor).

Warning: this reactor emits radiation (same type as Fission Reactor).

## Coolant

| Accepted coolant | Heat level reduction (per 1 measure of coolant) |
| --- | --- |
| Water | 30 |
| Salt Water | 20 |
| Liquid Nitrogen | 80 |
| Toxic Waste | 40 |
| Cryonic Extract | 125 |

These are values for a single slot. The Reactor can consume fuel from all four slots at once, producing Power from each.
