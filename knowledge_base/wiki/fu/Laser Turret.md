# [FU] Laser Turret

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Laser_Turret

---

**Laser Turret** is a long-range variant of Defense Turret. It fires very quickly (even the actively dodging enemy can get hit accidentally).

The weakness of this turret is that it doesn't do a lot of damage.
