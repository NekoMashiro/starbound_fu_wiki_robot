# [FU] Lead

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lead

---

**Lead** is an omnipresent tier 1 ore (it exists on almost every planet). It is a necessary ingredient for smelting many high-tier alloys.

Easiest way to obtain it is to sift various soil blocks (Dirt, etc.), especially Sulphuric Dirt.
