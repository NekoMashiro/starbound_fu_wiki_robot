# [FU] Liquid Metallic Hydrogen

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Liquid_Metallic_Hydrogen

---

**Liquid Metallic Hydrogen** is the second strongest fuel for Quantum Reactor (providing 570W power).
It can be extracted from certain late-game materials like Metallic Hydrogen.
