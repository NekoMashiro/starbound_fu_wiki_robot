# [FU] Liquid Mixer

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Liquid_Mixer

---

**Liquid Mixer** is a powered machine that allows the player to mix liquids in a controlled setup, instead of direct contact in the environment. While we could tell you the nitty gritty on the Input/Output system, that would cause this page to be larger than most of the Wiki itself. Rather, you are encouraged to build a Lab Directory and set it up near your equipment.

The machine requires 15J of energy to do one mixing action (e.g. 1 Water + 1 Poison = 2 Poison). It can do 6 mixing actions per second.
