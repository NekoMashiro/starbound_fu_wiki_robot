# [FU] Liquid Network

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Mechanics | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Liquid_Network

---

**Liquid Network** consists of the following objects:
- Liquid Mover Input - sucks the liquid around it, sends it via the wire, 
- Liquid Mover Pump - receives liquid via the wire, then pours the liquid into the world,
- Pressurized Mover Pump - same as Liquid Mover Pump, but doesn't stop if there isn't enough space for liquid (instead it keeps outputting the liquid into the same enclosed space: for example, it can store 40000 liquid in a 5x5 area).

It is primarily used to mix two liquids (such as Poison and Organic Soup) by dumping them into the same lake. It can also store large amounts of liquids by compressing them.

## Unrelated objects
The following objects are NOT a part of the Liquid Network, and can't be connected to it, but can be helpful when dealing with liquids:
- Spout - pours liquids into the world. Part of the Item Network, can receive new liquids only via the Item Transference Device.
- Copper Pump, Iron Pump, Titanium Pump, Densinium Pump - turns liquids in the world into items. Part of the Item Network.
- Liquid Mixer - uses power to mix liquids with each other and with tiles. Part of the Item Network.
- Liquid Collector - produces liquids and pours them into the world, but can't be wired to anything (except power and on/off switch).
