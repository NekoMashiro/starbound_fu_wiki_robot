# [FU] Liquids Acquisition

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Tutorials, Liquids | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Liquids_Acquisition

---

This guide is designed to give players a view at how their access to more liquids and their production will start and develop in a new game and point out some basic strategies. This guide starts at nearly at the beginning of the game and is divided in parts according to your technological level.

You need to know a few basics of liquid interactions: liquids can be mixed by pouring one onto the other. The result in general might not be a liquid and might itself interact with the two liquids themselves, depending upon their quantities. The simplest example is Infinite mixing in world: pouring one drop of Poison in a puddle of Water will turn all the water in Poison. Many liquids do infinitely mix and it is the primary liquid manipulation in the early game. Later, controlled interactions are possible in a Liquid Mixer, but in most of the guide you do not need it.

## Planning research and upgrades
The early game for a long time does not require any Chemistry to gather and manipulate liquids in an effective manner if you have started investing in the Agriculture tree. Since eating is required, Sulphuric acid is optional, it is strongly advised to concentrate first on wells, basic crops and bees, which can provide liquid creation more effectively than the chemistry tree in the early game.

While the first upgrade to your Matter Manipulator that is advisable for the purpose of liquid gathering is indeed liquid collection, you can collect liquids by using pumps, Spouts and liquid movers too, so if you can build a Copper Pump but have not yet gathered enough Manipulator Modules for the upgrade, you can still start.

## Game start
We assume in the very early game you have not gone far in the Power Progression so you have access to tools like Hand Mill or Wooden Centrifuge, but do not yet control powered machines.

In this enviroment, forget chemistry, your first steps are in agriculture: build a well and start beekeping! In particular you are interested in Orchid Queens that are guaranteed to be in a Lush biome on the starting plane: they have a chance to produce Floral Honeycomb which is extracted to Organic Soup. It is among the first liquids you are advised to look for, because it is the simplest fertilizer for trays which will help make the food supply stable in the early game.

### Water and Contaminated Water
A rainy weather or the wells (and upgrades) can provide unlimited Water. Build water sources because they are needed for the rest of the guide.

Water can be centrifuged, e.g. with a Wooden Centrifuge or higher, to obtain **Contaminated Water**, in unlimited quantities for as long as you can produce Water.

### Organic Soup and Poison
Floral Honeycombs have a rare chance to be created, so it might be worth investing in multiple apiaries or upgrading to larger ones. After you got your honeycomb, extract it (e.g. with a Hand Mill) to Organic Soup. You got at least 2. Consider building the following:
1. Start building two relatively large puddles of water (able to contain dozens of units of water),
1. In one puddle of water drop **exactly** one drop of Organic Soup. You will notice that Water and Organic Soup react to create **Poison**. Store some of it in your inventory for later use. Now, as in the vanilla game, for as long as you do not deplete it of poison, now pouring water (eg from the well) in this puddle will create Poison and as such you can pour water, then retrieve Poison unlimitedly.
1. In the other puddle of water, pour one drop of the Poison you stored. this will turn it into Poison. Now pour the remaining Organic Soup, the whole puddle will turn to Organic Soup. Notice now that if you pour some Water in this puddle, on the liquid surface it will briefly turn to poison and then to Organic Soup. Note this type of liquid interaction: a big amount of Organic Soup reacting with Water will turn the contatc surface to poison, hence the whole Water to Poison and Poison to Organic Soup. So, in world, but not in a controlled Liquid Mixer, Water turns unlimitedly to **Organic Soup** with a small loss at the contact surface.

Now you have the best liquid and the best early game fertilzer at the beginning of the game!

One drop of organic soup in water makes poison, a huge amount makes organic Soup: manipulating chain reactions in in-world mixing is key to control liquid mixing.

### Pus and Bio-Ooze
Young queens (e.g. Young Honey Queen or Young Orchid Queen) can be extracted to Baby Larvae, which can be extracted to **Pus**. This is not the easiest source of Pus, but the best the early game can offer. Later you will find Atropus Sea planets with infinite oceans of Pus.

Pus can be centrifuged to obtain **Bio-Ooze**. For the purpose of the early game, it might be worth saving a few units of Bio-Ooze for later, in particular:
- If you find Healing Water in the world, then you can create as much as you want with water, but from Healing Water and a single drop of Bio-Ooze, you can mix unlimited Bio-Ooze! Chain infinite mixing!
- Similarly Bio-Ooze is infinitely mixable in Pus, but at this point, because you have limited quantities, This is likely a last resort,
- If you do not find Healing Water, later you'll have powered machines that will create it, however even if you have no interest in Bio-Ooze, we assume you have access now to at least a few drops, which will grant you access to Irradium and Protocite early later.

### Initial supply of Oil
Oil can be obtained easily but in limited quantities via agriculture: Corn and Automato both are sources for extracting it.

Unlimited quantities are available out of the planet or later technologically in this guide.

### Sulphuric Acid
Like Poison, once one drop is obtained, you can generate infinitely many units of **Sulphuric Acid** mixing with Water. There are two routes in the early game with no powered machines to this first drop:
- Find Sulphur (e.g. find an Egg and extract from it), extract it to Sulphuric Acid, (there can be biomes with puddles of acid)
- Grow Currentcorn and extract it

Once you have found one drop, you can have infinite via mixing with Water. In particular though note that Sulphuric Acid has a lot of interaction tha yields solid blocks, these will be important later once access to powered machines is secured; it is time to move a bit in the power progression to continue.

### Lava
The easiest way at this point is going to the Garden Core Layer and pump unlimited Lava there. You can use it to go further in the Power Progression, see that guide.

### Liquid Iron
While you can now extract Liquid Iron from Iron Ore, it is recommended you save this knowledge for later, as most likely at this point of the game, you need Iron ore to build machinery.

## Early powered machines
We assume you have now a technological level that allows you access to powered Rock Breaker or Sifter. Maybe have access to Tungsten Ore or can leave the planet, but we are assuming:
- you do not have yet technologies requiring ores higher than tier 1,
- no other planet is safe yet,
- your progress in the Chemistry tree is now useful but still optional

### Blood
Once crafted the first unit of Blood, then it is infinitely mixable with Water.

There are a few routes for it:
- if you have enough Bones to create some Bonemeal you can sift both some Oil and the starting Blood
- Cadaver autopsies and the madness route!
- with the Alchemy Table the first unit of Blood is easy, but you might have still other parts of the research tree to spend on with higher priority.

### Dark Water
Now that you have access to a centrifuge like Iron Centrifuge or higher, you get Dark Water if you centrifuge Water too. If you need a huge supply of it, though your best chance is moving to Penumbra planets with a Liquid Collector or on Tidewater planets.

### Healing Water
If you have secured a supply already skip this part. You might also look for it until you find a planet with it.

For a discovery in maximum safety or if you have not still left, you need to produce Gelatinous Stone and crush it. To do so you need 2x Organic Soup and Bio-Ooze to mix:
- Bio-Ooze + Organic Soup -> Slimy Soil
- Organic Soup + Slimy Soil -> Gelatinous Stone
In the in-world mixing, pour 1 drop at a time of Bio-Ooze on Organic Soup for the whole chain reaction to trigger, be careful that you get Patak Crystals if you pour too much at a time!
Alternatively, later with the Liquid Mixer you can mix cleanly.

### Mercury
Sulphuric Acid creates 2 key blocks:
- Sulphuric Dirt comes from mixing with Dirt
- Sulphuric Stone comes from mixing with Lava or Cobblestone; there is infinite Sulphuric Acid from Water and infinite Lava in the core
These substances are sources of Mercury (or Cinnabar, which extracts to it) via crushing or sifting, alongside other useful materials. Via ITD, you can automate the mercury production. There are other ways that do not require power but are at a similar point in the game progression:
- after reasearching, you can produce some at the Chemistry Lab, it will not be as automated as mixing and crushing,
- you can find Cinnabar in other worlds
- you can find liquid Mercury on Cyber Spheres,
depending upon your preparedness to monsters or your research points they might be more appealing.

### Liquid Nitrogen
You have now several sources to supply Liquid Nitrogen via extraction or crushing/sifting, e.g. from Blizzberry, but it will not be long that exploration becomes the chief one. There are moons with Nitrogen Cores and there are Dark Arctic oceans that are half Water (no Salt) and half Liquid Nitrogen, seek those for near infinite easy supply.

### Protocite and Irradium
Now note the following chain reaction:
- Bio-Ooze + Mercury -> Liquid Protocite
- Liquid Protocite + Mercury -> Liquid Irradium
- Irradium and mercury do not react
Hence controlling the quantities, a drop of Bio-Ooze in a puddle of Mercury should stratify the two liquids. Careful with the quantities of Bio-Ooze, because Irradium and Bio-Ooze create Rainbow Sand, dosage is very difficult.

You need only one drop of **Liquid Irradium** in this way, because it is infinitely mixable with Organic Soup.

If you are able to have exactly one unit of Bio-Ooze react with one unit of Mercury (a 1 block puddle, or the liquid mixer), you can also isolate **Liquid Protocite**. It is infinitely mixable with Pus and Black Tar.

Note that, assuming you have Mercury, such setup until now requires no power, so in principle, as early as you can crush a Sulphuric Rock, you can have fission fuel! You can power the ship! This is very useful to build radiation protection in advance of going to irradiated planets! The requirement of controlled reactions it is becoming tougher now, and we cannot ignore the need for Tier 3 tech.

## Liquid mixer time
Now you are more secure in the position on the technological ladder and unambigously have a Liquid Mixer, easy access to Tungsten Ore and access to Tier 3 technology is being reached. You are definetely capable of going on tier 2 and tier 3 planets. If you were not able to isolate Gelatinous Stone or Protocite before, now you can easily in the mixer!

If your goal is to secure more liquids until you complete all the infinitely mixable ones, so that, for instance, then you can have infinite Protocite and Oil from Water (via Blue Grav-Liquid).

### Plasmic Fluid
Even if such fluid has no utility for you now, you need quite a supply (at least 25x) for the others.

Mix Pus and Organic Soup in the Liquid mixer. See Plasmic Fluid for details.

### Essentia Obscura
Only in a liquid mixer: 25x Liquid Protocite + 25x Plasmic Fluid will yield a measure of Essentia Obscura. It is infinitely mixable with Pus, which you might be able to generate arbitrary amounts now, but the point is to have some to start the next step.

### Orange-Grav Liquid and Blue-Grav Liquid
Complete the cycle of infinitely mixable liquids but saving up a source of each:
- Liquid Iron + Essentia Obscura -> Orange Grav-Liquid
- Orange Grav-Liquid + Water -> Blue Grav-Liquid
that allow the next step.

### Infinite Tar, Oil, Protocite
In particular now you can produce infinite Protocite, Black Tar and Oil from Water:
- Water + Blue Grav-Liquid -> Blue Grav-Liquid
- Blue Grav-Liquid + Black Tar -> Black Tar
- Black Tar + Liquid Protocite -> Liquid Protocite
while Oil can be centrifuged from Black Tar.

### Fuel
With infinite Protocite and Oil surely moons with Liquid Erchius Fuel are safe and the various fuels that are not Irradium covered before, reachable. For fuel refining you need to delve in the Chemistry tree now though.

## Technological or exploration side effects
The rest of the liquids are best gathered either via exploring or automatically as fusion/fission byproducts (see the power progression for details) or via Atmospheric Condenser.

Note that while you have now access to enough liquids to set up infinite mixing from Water for most things, still efficiency might dictate to use most suitable planets, especially if you are automating production. For example, if a planet has Liquid Irradium via the Liquid Collector or the Atmospheric Condenser (e.g. on Irradiated) and the goal is some production heavy in liquid Irradium, a chained Liquid Network from a Water Generator as would be required on a Space Station makes little sense.
