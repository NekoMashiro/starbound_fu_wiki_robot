# [FU] List of blocks by durability

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Items by various parameters, Blocks | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/List_of_blocks_by_durability

---

This is the list of blocks sorted by their "durability" (how much **block damage** can they take).

This is important in three situations:
1. when mining: more durable blocks are much slower to mine,
1. when building on a planet with hazardous weather (such as Cosmic Blasts, which can deal 12 damage to the area they fall upon) - blocks stronger than 24 hitpoints are very likely to be safe, while weak blocks will be utterly destroyed,
1. when walking in a Mech (it damages the blocks by stepping on them, and even more so by jumping on them).

Note: extremely weak blocks (less than 4 hitpoints) are not listed here.

## Trivia
- Weakest block in the game is Cloud. It has 0.01 hitpoints.
- You can further increase hitpoints of a block by adding Densinium Ore (+62 hitpoints) with Material Mod Placer tool.
- Damaged blocks will completely regenerate their health after a short delay.
