# [FU] Lobster Trap

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lobster_Trap

---

**Lobster Trap** is a small seafood cage. When placed in the waters of Ocean, Arctic and Tidewater planets, it catches 1 Lobster after 300-1200 seconds of being empty.

If there are other Lobster Traps within 8 blocks, then it will have significantly reduced production. Scan the Lobster Trap to check if it was affected by this.

The land-based alternative is Pest Trap, which produces Pest.

## Tips
- Lobster Traps do not float. They can be placed in platforms under the surface of the water with another platform to walk on over the surface for quick harvesting.
