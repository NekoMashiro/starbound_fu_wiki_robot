# [FU] Lost and Found

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Shops | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lost_and_Found

---

**Lost and Found** is a useful service at the Science Outpost (to the left). Talk to Vel'uuish trader called "Khe" (two levels above Kevin).

Here you can buy any limited or otherwise unobtainable items from missions (like Verdant Ruins), but only after you completed the mission at least once.
- Khe's Note: Beacons are part of an incomplete and probably never to be finished T10 Mission quest chain.
