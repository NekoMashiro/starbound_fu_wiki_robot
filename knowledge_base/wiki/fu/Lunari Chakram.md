# [FU] Lunari Chakram

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lunari_Chakram

---

This weapon will fly out, returning immediately upon reaching the location clicked, reaching its maximum range of 20 tiles, hitting a target, or hitting a solid block, whichever happens first. It deals ice damage to targets. Unlike some other Lunari weapons, the Lunari Chakram will *not* illuminate areas.
