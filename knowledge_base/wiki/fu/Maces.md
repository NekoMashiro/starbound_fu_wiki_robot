# [FU] Maces

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Maces

---

**Mace** is a one handed procedurally generated melee weapon model. They cannot be aimed directionally, and swing in whatever direction the player is facing.

## Base Statistics
- **Base DPS:** 7.0-8.8 health/s (Common), 7.2-9.0 health/s (Uncommon), 7.5-9.2 health/s (Rare)
- **Fire Time:** 0.8-1.3 seconds between 1x-damage attacks (~0.8-1.3 1x-damage attacks/s)
- **Combo Progression:**
1. First Strike: 0.9x damage, 15 default knockback
1. Second Strike: 0.7x damage, 15 default knockback
1. Third Strike: 1.0x damage, 15 default knockback
- **Crit Chance:** 1%
- **Base Crit Damage Bonus:** +1 damage (Common), +2 damage (Uncommon/Rare)

## Elemental Damage
Uncommon and rare Maces are assigned one of seven elements (non-physical damage types) on generation. Neither uncommon nor rare Maces inflict elemental status effects.

- Fire: Inflicts [Burning](https://starbounder.org/Burning)
- Ice: Inflicts [Frost](https://starbounder.org/Frost)
- Poison: Inflicts [Poisoned](https://starbounder.org/Poisoned)
- Electric: Inflicts [Electrified](https://starbounder.org/Electrified)
- Radiation: Inflicts Radiation Burn
- Shadow: Inflicts Shadow Gas
- Cosmic: Inflicts Armor

## Special Attacks
Maces, like most one-handed weapon types, cannot generate with any special attacks.
