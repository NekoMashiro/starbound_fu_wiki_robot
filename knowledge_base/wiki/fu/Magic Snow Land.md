# [FU] Magic Snow Land

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Magic_Snow_Land

---

**Magic Snow Land** is a minor biome that can be found on Snow Planets. It is a biome that include veins of candy canes blocks and several kind of sweets decorating the wilderness, like lollipops or gumdrops, but also wrapped gifts sprayed along the way.

Flying dolls and wrapped mimics are the main ennemies that can be encountered in this biome. Flying dolls can fly through solid blocks in a similar fashion as [Spookits](https://starbounder.org/Spookit) or [Skimbuses](https://starbounder.org/Skimbus), so beware.
