# [FU] Magnet Grip II

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Magnet_Grip_II

---

**Magnet Grip II** is a leg tech available in Frackin' Universe. It is the upgrade of the former Magnet Grip I and as such has amplified versions of it's abilities.

Magnet Grip II has two mid air jumps, increasing from the single jump Magnet Grip I gives when equipped. The player will no longer slide down a wall when attached, allowing the player to stay stuck in place on a wall. Magnet Grip II additionally will give players clung to a wall 10% more defense overall.

Interestingly, the upgrade changes the sounds of the extra jumps.
