# [FU] Mark VI Gasmask

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mark_VI_Gasmask

---

**Mark VI Gasmask** is a helmet that negates the following effects:
- environmental Poison hazards (Mild Poison, Moderate Poison, Extreme Poison, Severe Poison Gas), useful on planets like Toxic or Strange Sea
- Caliginous Gas
- slower movement in Metallic Hydrogen layer (on Gas Giant planets)
- effects of Helium-3 (jumping as if you were in low gravity), these are mostly beneficial
