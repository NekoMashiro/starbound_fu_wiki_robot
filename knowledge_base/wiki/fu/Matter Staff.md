# [FU] Matter Staff

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Matter_Staff

---

**Matter Staff** is a high-tech alternate mining tool. It mines the blocks by targeting them with a controllable flying orb (which follows your cursor for up to 20 seconds, and then disappears).

Left-click creates an orb that can mine foreground blocks, right-click - background blocks.

It mines in a circular area with radius 3 (5x5 square without 3 blocks in each corner) and inflicts many 5 block damage ticks per second. Weak blocks like Dirt will be mined almost instantly.

## Other mechanics
- The orb can "grab" the monsters and drag them around, but won't do any damage to them. That won't cause them to aggro on the player.
- The orb doesn't affect the player.
- The orb doesn't interact with liquids.
- The orb can move items that are lying on the ground (including the blocks that you just mined).
- Matter Staff can be used at significant distance from the blocks that you are mining (far exceeding Mining Laser or Master Manipulator by this parameter).
