# [FU] Metallic Hydrogen

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Metallic_Hydrogen

---

**Metallic Hydrogen** is average fuel for high-tech Quantum Reactor (providing 275 power, while the best fuel, Liquid Deuterium, provides 620). It can be found on some Gas Giant planets in infinite quantities.
