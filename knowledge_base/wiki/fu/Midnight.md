# [FU] Midnight

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Midnight

---

**Midnight** planets are tier 5 vanilla planets. They are very cold and are extremely dark, but (unlike the Lightless planets) they don't have an Unbreachable Darkness hazard (so they can be easily illuminated with Torches, etc.).
