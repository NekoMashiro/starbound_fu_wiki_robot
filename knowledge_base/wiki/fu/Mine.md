# [FU] Mine

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Dungeon | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mine

---

**Mine** is vanilla dungeon on Garden planets. See https://starbounder.org/Mines for details. In FU, Mother Poptop mini-boss was moved from the Mine into the Dark Cavern mission.

Visiting the Mine is very lucrative: there are many Iron Bars, Manipulator Modules and Core Fragments in the crates.
