# [FU] Mmmmm, tasty raw meat!

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Status effects, Buffs | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mmmmm,_tasty_raw_meat!

---

**Mmmmm, tasty raw meat!** - a status effect that is applied when eating raw meat when your race is a **carnivore**. (see also: list of races and list of other status effects)

Icon: 

## Effects
- 1.1x Max Health,
- 1.1x Max Energy,
- slight defense boost.
