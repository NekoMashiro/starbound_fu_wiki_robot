# [FU] Mooshi

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Farm Beasts, Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mooshi

---

There are some changes made to Mooshi in FU. See the [Mooshi](https://starbounder.org/Mooshi) page on Starbounder for info from the vanilla game. They are herbivores and need to be fed from a trough in order to produce.

Mooshi eggs take 300s to hatch in the Incubator or 8000s without.

## Mooshi baby
- Evolve time: 1000 (evolves quicker if fed in trough)
- Max health: 30. Regenerates health
- Hunger time: 20 min.
Capturable & relocatable.

**Monster ID:** fumooshibaby

## Stats
- Has a 10% chance to lay an egg once an hour, or every 40 minutes if happy.
- Diet: herbivore. Hunger time: 20 min.
- Harvest time: every 500-900 sec depending on happiness. Will not produce at all if starving.
- Capturable & relocatable. The captured monster type is a Mooshi baby.
**Monster ID**: fumooshi
