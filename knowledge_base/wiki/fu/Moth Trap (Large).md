# [FU] Moth Trap (Large)

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Moth_Trap_(Large)

---

**Moth Trap (Large)** is an improved Moth Trap. It produces significantly more Silk Fibre, and it works the entire night (instead of only 60% of the night).

Alternative is to grow Bracken plants, especially in trays with good fertilizer.
