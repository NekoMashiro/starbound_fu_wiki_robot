# [FU] Mystery Box

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mystery_Box

---

**Mystery Box** is sold by some NPC merchants. There is something inside.

## Tips
- If you are worried that all your food might spoil, you can carry several of these boxes as an emergency snack. See also: foods that take a long time to spoil.
- In areas that don't have any enemies, Mystery Box can be used to motivate your crew to reapply their buffs (if they haven't already done so in the last 5 minutes).
