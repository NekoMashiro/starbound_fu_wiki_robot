# [FU] Neptunium Ore

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Neptunium_Ore

---

**Neptunium Ore** is a tier 3 radioactive ore. It can be found on Penumbra, Bog, Proto and Aether World planets, as well as underground on Atropus, Fungal, and Volcanic Primeval planets. 

It is the only radioactive ore that can't be found on Irradiated planets.
