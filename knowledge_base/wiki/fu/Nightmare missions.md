# [FU] Nightmare missions

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Nightmare_missions

---

**Nightmare missions** were an idea that was never fully implemented (nobody is working on it anymore): tier 10 revamped/remixed versions of previous missions to provide challenge to experienced players. They were supposed to be started with items like Beacon: Dark Library.

Nothing is known about them; however if the Lovecraft vibe is anything to go by; perhaps Azathoth's Great Dream that we all inhabit is souring into a Nightmare?
