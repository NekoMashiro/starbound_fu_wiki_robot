# [FU] Object Data Sensor

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Object_Data_Sensor

---

**Object Data Sensor** (previously known as "Placement Point Sensor") is a tool that shows optimal points for placing a Storage Bridge near every Chest, Fridge or any other storage. It also shows colony tags of placed objects (furniture, etc.) when you click on them.

## Placement points
You need this if/when you have several chests very close to each other, and you want to ensure that Storage Bridge won't send items into incorrect chest. To do so, place the Storage Bridge as close to the "Placement Point" (highlighted by this tool) as possible.

Placement Points are also used for other purposes. For example, radiation area of Fission Reactor is centered around this point (not around the center of reactor).
