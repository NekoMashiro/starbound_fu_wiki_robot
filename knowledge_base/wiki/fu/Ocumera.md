# [FU] Ocumera

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ocumera

---

**Ocumera** are agile, aggressive hunters. Firing quick snapshot lasers, a group can quickly become a threat due to the persistence of their attacks, and the fact they tend to remain out of range of most melee strikes. Even those with ranged capabilities may find these creatures deadly as their speed and agility may make targeting and landing a hit difficult.

A dark blue flying creature possessing a large eye like appendage at the base of its body. Ocumera tend to fly high above a target, making sure to keep a good distance between attacks. Usually found in groups, the Ocumera attacks through firing a quick charging laser from it’s tail eye. This laser travels in a straight path and can have enough force to destroy low density blocks and tiles.
