# [FU] Orange Grav-Liquid

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Orange_Grav-Liquid

---

**Orange Grav-Liquid**, when poured on the player, makes the player significantly heavier and barely able to jump. It will also increase fall damage.

It rains down from the sky on some planets (Cyber Sphere, Lightless, etc.).

An opposite effect is caused by Blue Grav-Liquid (it makes the player significantly lighter).
