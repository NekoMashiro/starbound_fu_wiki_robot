# [FU] Ore Drone III

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ore_Drone_III

---

Unlike the Mining Drone, which has identical crafting materials, the Ore Drone III has a slightly larger scan radius (about 50-55 blocks instead of ~45), but it doesn't locate caves like the Mining Drone does.
