# [FU] Painting

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Painting

---

**Painting** is a usable item. It turns into a random *painting* (decorative placeable object).

Unlike a more advanced Inspired Painting, a regular Painting doesn't have a chance to produce Madness-giving paintings or random *quest maps*.
