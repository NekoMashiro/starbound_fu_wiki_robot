# [FU] Parasol

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Parasol

---

**Parasol** is a tool that makes you slowly glide down instead of falling like a rock. This can save you from fall damage.

## Upgrades
Once crafted, you can upgrade the Parasol in your Tricorder. Each upgrade adds a better horizontal movement and slower fall.
