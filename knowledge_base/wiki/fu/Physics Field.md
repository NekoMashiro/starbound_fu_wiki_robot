# [FU] Physics Field

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Techs, Disambiguation pages | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Physics_Field

---

**Physics Field** is a head tech (in your Tricorder) that allows you to hover in the air (press Space twice and keep holding it), fly (use WASD buttons while hovering) or to become immune to fall damage (F to enable/disable). It drains energy when active, except when used on your own ship (but not when visiting ships of other players).

There are several levels:
- Physics Field I
- Physics Field II
- Physics Field III
