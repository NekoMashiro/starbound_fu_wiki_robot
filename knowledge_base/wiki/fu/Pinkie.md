# [FU] Pinkie

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Joke weapons | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pinkie

---

**Pinkie** is a joke weapon that doesn't deal any damage. It launches enemies high into the air. They don't suffer fall damage.

While useless for attack, it can allow you to safely pass through outdoor areas filled with enemies (by tossing enemies aside).
