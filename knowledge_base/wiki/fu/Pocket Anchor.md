# [FU] Pocket Anchor

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pocket_Anchor

---

**Pocket Anchor** is a tool that drastically increases your fall speed, and also helps you sink in liquid. This applies as long as you are holding Pocket Anchor in your hands.

Be careful not to die to fall damage (consumables like Ighant Bulb might help). The fall speed is too high to be countered with "double jump" techs like Pulse Jump.

Pocket Anchor can be used to sink in a pool of Blue Grav-Liquid.
