# [FU] Portable Water Pump

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Resource-generating backpacks | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Portable_Water_Pump

---

**Portable Water Pump** is a resource-generating backpack. As long as you wear it in your EPP slot, small amounts of Water will slowly appear in your inventory.
