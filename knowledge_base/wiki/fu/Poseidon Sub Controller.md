# [FU] Poseidon Sub Controller

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poseidon_Sub_Controller

---

The **Poseidon submarine** is an upgraded version of the Triton. Unlike the Triton, which is capable of safely operating only in water, the Poseidon can operate in nearly liquid, with **quicksand, lava, core lava and molten iron (and all gases) as the only exceptions**. As with the Triton, the Poseidon is unarmed, reliant on its speed to escape from predators and enemies rather than fighting them. The Player is provided with an unlimited air supply when piloting the submarine. The Poseidon is a great vehicle for exploring the more hostile ocean worlds of FU.

Poseidon submarines have two seats, pilot in the front and passenger in the back.

**If your submarine won't move, you are in a passenger seat. Exit the sub and reposition yourself to the front seat.**
