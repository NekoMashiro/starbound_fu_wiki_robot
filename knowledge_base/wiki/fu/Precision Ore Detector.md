# [FU] Precision Ore Detector

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precision_Ore_Detector

---

**Precision Ore Detector** is an **upgradable tool** that precisely targets a specific ore or mineral. It has different tiers, in which each tier determines the type of ores it can scan.

To use it, click it to release a pulse to scan any nearby ores. Shift+Click to open its scan interface. An ore or bar can be used to scan a specific type.

Here are the different tiers of the Precision Ore Detector, and the ores it can scan at each tier.

| | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | Tier 4 (Max) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| vanilla | Erchius Crystal | Titanium | Aegisalt | N/A |
| Frackin | Cinnabar | Zerchesium | Lasombrium | Densinium |
| Elithian | N/A | N/A | N/A | Ferroverium |

Alternative tool is Multi-ore Detector, which will detect all ores. This can be a good thing if you need ALL ores, but it can also be a bad thing when you don't want something cheap like Copper Ore to distract you from finding Diamonds or a specific ore that you lack.
