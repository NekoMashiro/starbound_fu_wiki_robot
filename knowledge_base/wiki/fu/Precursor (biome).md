# [FU] Precursor (biome)

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precursor_(biome)

---

This is a surface variant of the Precursor biome. An enormous facility left by the extinct Precursor race, these biomes are a valuable source of Precursor machinery and materials.

Having been picked clean by countless explorers in ages past these surface biomes are much less fruitful than their underground counterparts, but can still be greatly rewarding to explore.
