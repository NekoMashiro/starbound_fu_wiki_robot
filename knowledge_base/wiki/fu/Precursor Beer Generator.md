# [FU] Precursor Beer Generator

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Resource-generating backpacks | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precursor_Beer_Generator

---

**Precursor Beer Generator** is a resource-generating backpack. As long as you wear it in your EPP slot, it will generate Beer at a very high speed. It can be found in the Precursor chests.

The Beer spawning rate is 12 per second (1 per 5 in-game ticks). It will stop generating Beer if you already have 1000 or more in your inventory, or if you stand AFK for a certain period of time. There is a workaround for the 1000 Beer cap - fill the relevant inventory tab with anything except beer and place one or more Grabbers near yourself. The Beer will drop on floor, and the grabber will collect it. You can do nothing about the AFK penalty, however.

Its primary usefulness is as a way to generate cheap beer for fertilizing crops. Beer has a higher starting requirement for seeds than organic soup, but it produces a faster growth rate.

You can also centrifuge beer for pixels. 12 Beer is approximately equal to 3.75 pixels if you use a Gas Centrifuge, so your profits would be 225 pixels per minute and 13500 per hour. It is probably not worth it, as you can use this beer and power to grow crops which will yield much higher profits.
