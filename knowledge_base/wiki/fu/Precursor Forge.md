# [FU] Precursor Forge

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precursor_Forge

---

**Precursor Forge** is a high-tech combination of Powder Sifter and Rock Crusher. It is found in Precursor chests and is relatively rare.

It works approximately 2 times faster than Powder Sifter. It also has approximately 1.5 times higher chance to produce output items than Powder Sifter.

This is a large piece of equipment, 10x16 tiles.
