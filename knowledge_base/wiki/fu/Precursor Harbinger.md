# [FU] Precursor Harbinger

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precursor_Harbinger

---

**Precursor Harbinger** is a Tier 5 shotgun. It is randomly found in precursor chests in the Precursor Ruins mission. Its first attack is glowing shotgun bullets, and its alt ability makes a sound like a thunder crack and jabs a blade forward.
