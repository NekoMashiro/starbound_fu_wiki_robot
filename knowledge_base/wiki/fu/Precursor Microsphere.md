# [FU] Precursor Microsphere

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precursor_Microsphere

---

**Precursor Microsphere** is a combination of Spider Microsphere (climbing walls and fitting into 1x1 space) and Armor Sphere (being able to place mines on left-click).

You find it during the Precursor Ruins mission.
