# [FU] Precursor X-Ray Gun

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precursor_X-Ray_Gun

---

**Precursor X-Ray Gun** is an exploration weapon. Its damage is pathetic, but it fires numerous glowing projectiles that go through blocks, which allows you to see what is inside the far away unilluminated caves, to spot enemies and ores, etc.

It can also be used to aggro Burrowers inside the walls (to make them chase the player in the open).
