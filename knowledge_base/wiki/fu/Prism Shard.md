# [FU] Prism Shard

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prism_Shard

---

**Prism Shard** is a tier 4 ore. It exists on many planets, but Chromatic worlds are exceptionally rich with it. Prism sub-biomes are also a good, if hard-to-find place to find them.
