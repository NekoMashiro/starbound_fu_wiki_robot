# [FU] Prismatic Chakram

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Weapons with Extra Properties | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prismatic_Chakram

---

This Chakram leaves a trail of hearts behind it as flies. This appears to be purely cosmetic. Like the Penumbrite Chakram it bounces off of walls instead of returning when it hits terrain. It illuminates areas, deals cosmic damage, and pierces enemies.
