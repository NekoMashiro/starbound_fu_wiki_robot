# [FU] Prismatic Rocket Launcher

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prismatic_Rocket_Launcher

---

Primary fire is an extremely quick-moving ring of energy that ricochets into several smaller rings when hitting terrain. Alt-Fire creates several small, bouncing orbs of electricity that electrify terrain and can hit foes multiple times.
