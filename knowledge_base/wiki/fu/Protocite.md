# [FU] Protocite

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Protocite

---

**Protocite** is a tier 3 ore found on Proto Worlds and Shadow Proto Worlds in great quantities. It glows in the dark.

It can also be collected from centrifuging Liquid Protocite and silting or crushing Proto-related blocks.
