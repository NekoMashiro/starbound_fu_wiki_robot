# [FU] Quantum Extractor

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quantum_Extractor

---

**Quantum Extractor** is the best extractor in the game. It is significantly more efficient than MKII (produces more items from the same inputs).

Quantum Extractor does 1 extraction every 1.1 seconds.
