# [FU] Quantum Manipulator

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quantum_Manipulator

---

**Quantum Manipulator** is an upgrade of vanilla Matter Manipulator. It starts with 6x6 area, better distance and mining speed.

You can replace existing Matter Manipulator by using **Shift+right click** on the Personal Tricorder and moving it into the Matter Manipulator's slot.

## Upgrades
- It doesn't matter if the previous/vanilla Matter Manipulator had any upgrades e.g. speed or area increases - a newly installed Quantum Manipulator starts from a clean slate (no speed/distance/area upgrades), with the exception of Wiring Mode and Paint Mode, which are retained. Liquid collection is unlocked by default.
- After you install a Quantum Manipulator, you can upgrade it further (with upgrades like "+300% speed"). For example, its mining area can be increased up to 9x9.
