# [FU] Quicksand

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Liquids, Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quicksand

---

> *Not to be confused with Quicksand from Elithian mod, which is a different thing.*
**Quicksand** is unobtainable liquid that only exists in-world: when mined, it turns into Loose Silt items. Loose Silt can't be turned back into the liquid form.

It exists as small puddles/lakes on some desert-like planets, but is relatively rare.

Players who swim in Quicksand are affected by "Quicksand" debuff, which reduces the mobility for a short period of time (similar to Tar).
