# [FU] Quietus Staff

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quietus_Staff

---

The primary fire of the Quietus Staff summons five swords in a circular pattern around the mouse-cursor. After a moment, each sword will fire sequentially in the current direction of the mouse-cursor dealing Physical damage and causing Bleed. The alt-fire of the staff creates a Regeneration Field at the mouse-cursor.
