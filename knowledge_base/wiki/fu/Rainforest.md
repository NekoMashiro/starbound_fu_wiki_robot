# [FU] Rainforest

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rainforest

---

**Rainforests** are handy places to gather Plant Fibre, as well as Jungle Dirt and Jungle Soil tiles which extract a higher ratio than standard dirt will. That means more useful resources for you. They tend to have heavy rainfall, and hurricane season can be nasty. Algae and fossils abound.

## Codex description
- Teeming with plantlife, Rainforests have rich soil, far exceeding the capacity of ordinary dirt.
