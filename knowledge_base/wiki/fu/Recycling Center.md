# [FU] Recycling Center

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Recycling_Center

---

**Recycling Center** obtains useful items that people often throw into garbage (like Paper, Glass, Advanced Plastic, etc.) from nearby Tenants (only those summoned with Colony Deed Mark II, not with the regular Colony Deed) within the distance of 128 blocks.

Having this building slightly increases the happiness of tenants (which increases the rent they will pay to the Colony Core).

Note: There can only be one Recycling Center per colony in range. Any more deactivates the happiness increase and item production for all Recycling Centers.
