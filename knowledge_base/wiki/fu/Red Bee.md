# [FU] Red Bee

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Red_Bee

---

The Red Bee is one of many types of flying insectoids, that make up this category of creature, this one being quite rare. This variety is noted by it's crimson body, black stripes, and it's relatively tiny size.

When it charges, the bee swoops at a target surprisingly fast, striking for overall minimal damage, but leaving the target with a unique debuff called "Sting". This debuff inflicts radiation damage overtime similar to poison, and causes the player to glow brightly for a few seconds.

The **Red Bee**is a unique monster that can be found on ________ planets, spawned by Blood Queen, and they are indeed catchable.

## Luna's Lab Notes
~ Possessing only 1hp may make these creatures seem trivial to deal with, but their increased spawnrate, quick movements, and damage overtime can become troublesome in swarms. Quite befitting of bees really. Interestingly enough, these creatures group together when attacking. Rather than taking different angles of attack, it seems that any attacking Bees will first gather together into a swarm and all dive at once (dealing massive damage depending on the size of the swarm).

~ When caught in a bug net, this creature becomes a "redqueen"

~ This creature seems less likely to stray from it's intended biome as it's movements at first seem sporadic, but upon closer inspection can be seen as keeping the bee quite close to where it was originally spawned. Almost as if it were patrolling the area..

~ The Red Bee used to have another variant in game called the "Tiny Red Bee" similar to how the normal "Bee" has a tiny variant called the "Tiny Bee". This was however removed from the files in game and is no longer accessible, making Red Bee's slightly more rare.
