## Navigation Console
- The majority of this planet's surface is a scorching red desert. Bio-scans reveal interesting flora and fauna. Recommend: Deadly Heat Protection
- Bring extreme heat protection when exploring this red desert as exposure for prolonged periods will prove 100% fatal.
- A red desert stretches across almost the entire circumference of this exceptionally hot world. Recommend: Extreme Heat Protection
