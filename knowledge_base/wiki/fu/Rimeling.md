# [FU] Rimeling

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rimeling

---

Bearing light blue skin, and purple streaks across it's skin, the Rimeling is adept at blending into frozen alien environments. The Rimeling gives off am aura of frost, freezing the air around it with it's unnaturally cold body temperature. To outsiders, or distant observers, the area in which the Rimeling stays will become cloaked in biting thick fog, masking the creatures existence. The the keen and the knowing, the presence of a sudden fog, or small area of thick mist marks the area of a hunting Rimeling. To the unwary, or fool-hearty, such areas of dense frozen cover, only marks the travelers doom.

Rimelings take advantage of their adaptations, and affinity to the cold, using freezing breath attacks and fridged bites to slow the blood of enemies to a crawl and gain pressing advantage in combat. Able to create large areas of freezing fog, it is best to fight the Rimeling at a distance, as the clouds of fog released deal significant damage, and slow a targets movements down significantly. In close range, or within a small room, this creature becomes most deadly - freezing a target before cornering them and tearing the target apart with razor sharp teeth and claws.
