# [FU] Rock Troll

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rock_Troll

---

**Rock Trolls** are horrid carnivores found in all climes, from arctic wastelands to tropical jungles. This variety stalks the barren waste lands of mountainous planets. Most creatures avoid these beasts, since trolls know no fear and attack unceasingly when hungry. Their frame appears bulky and muscular, but trolls possess surprising dexterity. Their arms and legs are long and capable. The legs end in great three-toed feet, the arms in wide, powerful hands with sharpened claws. The Rock Trolls’ rubbery hide is colored a mottled grey, or putrid ash. A writhing hairlike mass grows out of their skulls and is usually a matching color to the creatures skin. Their dull, sunken black eyes possess powerful vision, able to pierce the darkness of night. are capable of incredible strength, able to rip and tear through most of the mountains most brutal predators. Incredibly territorial, once a Rock Troll has claimed an area, it will attack any creatures that dare wander into it's domain. Rock Trolls most commonly claim desolate caves located in high peaks and arid mountaintops. From these caves, the Rock Trolls venture out at night, seeking prey and monsters to hunt. A Rock Troll will search far into the plains of it's region when descend on a hunt, covering many miles before the rays of dawn push the monster back toward their sheltered lairs. All trolls eat anything they can catch and devour, chasing without fear for their natural regenerative abilities make them all but impervious to most threats.

Rock Trolls possess a melee attack which can crush most creatures in a single blow, Also capable of hurling large boulders at distant targets, these rocks are often seen moments too late by unsuspecting. Additionally, the Rock Troll is powerful enough to crush the ground beneath it, smashing many of the toughest blocks and armored hides. Strength enough to pose a threat to heavily armored monsters, many of which roam the ecosystem in which this creature hunts. Additionally, the Rock Troll carries with it many [Crustoise](https://starbounder.org/Crustoise), which it throws or drops at the sight of prey to support in it's hunting - the creatures natural abilities to pierce tough hide, and distract targets assists in the Trolls hunting.
