# [FU] Rocky Moon

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rocky_Moon

---

**Rocky Moon** is a less creepy variant of Moon. They don't have Erchius Ghost, but they do have other monsters.

## Codex Description
- Rocky moons are tough terrain, but otherwise harmless.
- A rocky moon with jagged and dangerous stone formations.

## Notable Features
You can find Crystal Erchius Fuel, Moonstone, Cinnabar, Plutonium Ore, Lunari Crystals, Telebrium Crystals, and Crystals on rocky moons.

There are no crops or trees.
