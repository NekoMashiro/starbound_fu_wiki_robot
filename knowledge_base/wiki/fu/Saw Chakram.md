# [FU] Saw Chakram

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Weapons with Extra Properties | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Saw_Chakram

---

Unlike most Chakrams, this one will stick to floors and walls for approximately 1.5 seconds if it hits them. This allows it to be used in a brief area-of-denial role. It deals physical damage, can pierce targets, and can apply multiple damage pulses to the same target.
