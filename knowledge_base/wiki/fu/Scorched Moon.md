# [FU] Scorched Moon

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scorched_Moon

---

**Scorched Moon** is a class of moon, instances of which are found in the scorched orbital region around most stars. It includes moons of type Tar Ball, Red Desert, Desert, Infernus, Magma, Volcanic, Sulphuric, and Scorched.
