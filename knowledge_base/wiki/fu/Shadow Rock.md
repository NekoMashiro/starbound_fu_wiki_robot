# [FU] Shadow Rock

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shadow_Rock

---

**Shadow Rock** is a block that can only be found on Shadow Moon biomes.

Besides Elder Stone, it is the only reliable source of Raw Nocxium. Depending on the situation, producing it can be slightly more efficient and easier than production of the Elder Stone, because you only need 1 crystal and 30 Essentia Obscura (which can be obtained using Infinite mixing on Atropus Sea planets) for 30 **Shadow Rocks**.
