# [FU] Shadow

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shadow

---

> *This is an article about **planet**. For the species called "Shadow", see Tenebrhae.*

**Shadow** is a planet composed of randomly chosen dark-themed biomes (both on the surface and underground), similarly to other randomized planets (Super Dense and Unknown).

- Outputs of Atmospheric Condenser on this planet are the same as on normal planets (Garden, Eden, etc.), regardless of the primary biome.
- All species of bees have normal production (no bonus/penalty for biome).

## Environmental hazards
Shadow planets have randomly chosen hazards, similarly to Unknown planets.
> === List of all possible hazards ===

Possible hazards are picked from a smaller list than for Unknown planets. For example, a Shadow planet can't have an Erchius Ghost, Pressure or Temporal Instability. It also won't have Lethal hazards (which require 80% resistance to negate them). On the other hand, some "easier" hazards (like Helium-3 or Mild Poison) are also not available for Shadow planets.

These are internal IDs of hazards (e.g. ffbiomecold1 is Mild Cold and ffbiomecold3 is Extreme Cold).
