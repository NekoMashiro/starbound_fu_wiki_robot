# [FU] Shoat

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Disambiguation pages | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shoat

---

**Shoat** is a slime goat. You can use these animals as farm beasts. Their diet is "Special Omnivore".

There are 3 variants:
- Shoat (green) 
- Shoat (slime)
- Shoat (goo)

Opening a Nicely packed Shoat egg gives you an egg of random variant of Shoat (45% green/slime, 10% goo), suitable for incubation. You can also buy Shoat Egg from traders, but their stock will say "Shoat Egg" without any indication of what type of egg this is.
