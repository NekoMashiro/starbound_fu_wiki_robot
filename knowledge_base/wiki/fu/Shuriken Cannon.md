# [FU] Shuriken Cannon

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shuriken_Cannon

---

Primary fire consists of three Shuriken projectiles launched in a fan pattern. Alt-fire launches the same three Shuriken projectiles except they embed themselves in terrain and spin in place for a few seconds; foes that touch them take damage every 1-2 seconds.
