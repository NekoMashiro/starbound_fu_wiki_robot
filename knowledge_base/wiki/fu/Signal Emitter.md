# [FU] Signal Emitter

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Signal_Emitter

---

**Signal Emitter** is a placeable object with 1 wiring output. It always sends ON signal.

You can connect it to a Door to keep it always open (neither you nor NPCs would be able to close that Door), to automatically re-enable Watchers when you approach them, etc.

See also: Broken Signal Emitter (which is always OFF) - it can keep the Door always closed.
