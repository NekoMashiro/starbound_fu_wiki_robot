# [FU] Silver Ore

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Silver_Ore

---

**Silver Ore** is an omnipresent tier 1 ore (it exists on many planets and is not particularly rich on any of them). Demolishing the underground of Garden planet with a well-upgraded Mining Laser might be a good way to get lots of it.
