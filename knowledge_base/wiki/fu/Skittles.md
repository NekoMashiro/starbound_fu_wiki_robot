# [FU] Skittles

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Unique weapons, Joke weapons | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Skittles

---

**Skittles** is a unique tier 4 Grenade Launcher, found in Chromatic world chests. The primary fire launches a medley of various grenade types. Some of these grenades will damage terrain, or cause status ailments and self damage to the user if they're too close

The secondary fire launches Magnorbs of varying number and type.

Both the primary and secondary fire are capable of being fired in a weaker, but faster and more energy efficient, rapid fire mode by holding shift.

Additionally, Skittles has several random features, including:
- Altered item card values (e.g. crit chance is "Taco %," and fire rate is relabeled "pets."
- Various text lines about Greg and Rainbows spouted by the weapon when fired,
- Causing enemies to spout said various text lines.
- Misfiring by saying "Kevin?" and doing nothing.
- Causing a localized music change.
- Granting a long lasting "Party Time" buff characterized by a red balloon to the user causing said music change to be localized on the player

## Fake Skittles
There is a fake version of Skittles that will sometimes drop from monsters spawned by Precursor Spawner. Trying to use it will paralyze and slowly kill whoever tried it.

If you try to fire the fake Skittles in the immediate vicinity of Kevin or Lost and Found merchant, they will confiscate this weapon, possibly giving you something useful in return.
