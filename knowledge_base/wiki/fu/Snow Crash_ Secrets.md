# [FU] Snow Crash: Secrets

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Snow_Crash:_Secrets

---

You can find 2 locked rooms near the exit teleporter in Snow Crash. Those rooms contain a few extra containers but you will need to find 2 levers hidden inside the dungeon.

## First Lever
The first lever is located inside the Sewers part. Near the entrance of the Sewers, you will reach an area with a door that will lock if the security camera sees you. Head in the next room and you will find stairs leading you below. There you will find a small opening under the stairs to the right. Use your Sphere to reach the room at the end of the conduit and you will find the first lever there.

## Second Lever
The second lever is located near the beginning of the path you take when exiting the starting cave through the bottom exit. After blowing up the barrels to open the first door you find, you will reach an area with multiple containers. You can find the lever inside a crane above those containers. There's multiple way to reach it: Spike Sphere, ropes, any way to fly...
