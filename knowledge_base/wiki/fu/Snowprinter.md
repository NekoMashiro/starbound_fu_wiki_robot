# [FU] Snowprinter

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Snowprinter

---

**Snowprinter** is a crafting station for snowpeople parts (purely decorative). You can separately craft Snowhead, Snowbody and Snowbottom of a number of different races (e.g. Glitch Snowbody or Floran Snowhead). They can be placed on top of each other to form a complete snowman.
