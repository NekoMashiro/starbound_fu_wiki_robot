# [FU] Sprouting Table

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sprouting_Table

---

**Sprouting Table** slowly converts seeds and saplings into Genes currency. It doesn't require Power. 

Later in the game it can be replaced with faster Xeno Research Lab, and even faster Advanced Xenolab.

There is a usable Sprouting Table at the Science Outpost.
