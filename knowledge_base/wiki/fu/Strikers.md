# [FU] Strikers

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strikers

---

Strikers or flails are a special class of one handed melee weapon models. The function by releasing an attached projectile to attack enemies. Currently, only a limited selection of strikers exist in-game.

See :Category:Strikers for the list.

## Elemental Damage
Uncommon and rare strikers are assigned one of seven elements (non-physical damage types) on generation, inflicting the appropriate status effect on both primary and special attacks.

- Fire: Inflicts [Burning](https://starbounder.org/Burning)
- Ice: Inflicts [Frost](https://starbounder.org/Frost)
- Poison: Inflicts [Poisoned](https://starbounder.org/Poisoned)
- Electric: Inflicts [Electrified](https://starbounder.org/Electrified)
- Radiation: Inflicts Radiation Burn
- Shadow: Inflicts Shadow Gas
- Cosmic: Inflicts Armor

## Special Attacks
Strikers, like most one-handed weapon types, cannot generate with any special attacks.
