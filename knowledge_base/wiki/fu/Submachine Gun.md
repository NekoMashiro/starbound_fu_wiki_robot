# [FU] Submachine Gun

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Submachine_Gun

---

**Submachine Gun** is a one-handed procedurally generated ranged weapon model.

It can be common, uncommon, and rare. Common Submachine Guns will shoot bullets, while uncommon and rare ones will shoot elemental plasma shots.

## Base Statistics
- **Base DPS:** 8.0-9.0 health/s (Common), 8.5-9.5 health/s (Uncommon/Rare)
- **Fire Time:** 0.08(Uncommon/Rare)/0.09(Common)-0.15 seconds between singly-fired shots (~6.7-11.1(Common)/12.5(Uncommon/Rare) shots/s)
- **Energy Usage:** 24.0-27.0 energy/s (Common), 25.5-28.5 energy/s (Uncommon/Rare)
- **Fire Mode:** Automatic, Burst
- **Bullets per Burst:** 2-4
- **Burst Spacing:** 0.075 seconds between consecutive bullets
- **Knockback:** 4-8 (extremely weak to weak knockback)
- **Crit Chance:** 1%
- **Base Crit Damage Bonus:** +3 damage (Common), +4 damage (Uncommon), +5 damage (Rare)

## Elemental Damage
Uncommon and rare Submachine Guns are assigned one of seven elements (non-physical damage types) on generation. Though both uncommon and rare Submachine Guns exhibit elemental visuals, only rare Submachine Guns inflict status effects.

- Fire: Inflicts Burning
- Ice: Inflicts Frost
- Poison: Inflicts Poisoned
- Electric: Inflicts Electrified
- Radiation: Inflicts Radiation Burn
- Shadow: Inflicts Shadow Gas
- Cosmic: Inflicts Armor

## Special Attacks
Submachine Guns, like most one-handed weapon types, cannot generate with any special attacks.
