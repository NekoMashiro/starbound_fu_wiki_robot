# [FU] Sugar foods

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Food by diet type | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Sugar_foods

---

These foods have a lot of sugar.

None of the FU races or vanilla races care about this (they are not poisoned by foods solely because these foods have sugar, and they don't receive any special bonus from sugary foods). However, some third-party races (from other mods) might have special rules about sugar.
