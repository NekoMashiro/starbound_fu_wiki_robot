# [FU] Swarm Drone Mech Arm

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Swarm_Drone_Mech_Arm

---

The swarm drone mech arm is a drone arm that can deploy several rapid fire drones.

Each drone deployed by the arm will shoot small red laser blasts at the enemy in rapid succession. Quite a lot of projectiles can be fired at the enemy when all drones are deployed.

The weapon can deploy up to 5 drones per arm.
