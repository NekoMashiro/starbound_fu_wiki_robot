# [FU] TC-1 Survival Tent

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/TC-1_Survival_Tent

---

**TC-1 Survival Tent** is a usable furniture that gives numerous resistances and immunities to the player who is hiding inside. It also gives slight healing (similar to beds), but it doesn't stop hunger.

It can be used to recover from hazards that have weakened you (like Proto-Poison or Aetheric Atmosphere) and then continue exploring.

## List of resistances
Player inside the tent has increased resistances:

- +100% Ice
- +100% Fire
- +50% Electric
- +100% Poison
- +100% Radioactive
- +50% Cosmic
- +50% Shadow

This causes some hazards to be completely negated due to having enough resistance (e.g. 20%/40%/60% Ice resistance = immunity to Mild/Moderate/Extreme Cold).

Note that there is no bonus to Physical resistance, so the tent won't help on Sulphuric planets (which need 30% Physical resistance) or against Trophical Humidity (20% Physical resistance).

## List of immunities
Player inside the tent is immune to the following conditions:
- Lack of air
- Poison, Proto-Poison
- Insanity
- Pus, Bio-Ooze
- Bee Stings
