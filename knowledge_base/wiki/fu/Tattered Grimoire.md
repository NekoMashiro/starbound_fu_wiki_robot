# [FU] Tattered Grimoire

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tattered_Grimoire

---

**Tattered Grimoire** is an artifact that you find in Baron's Keep mission. It's hidden in a secret place, so you should search every corner and question everything (even the ground).

Finding it gives you coordinates to Delta Freya II (the final mission).
