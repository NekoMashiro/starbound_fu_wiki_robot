# [FU] Terramart Shipments 3.0

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Terramart_Shipments_3.0

---

**Terramart Shipments 3.0** automatically sells every item that is placed inside. Resulting pixels (30% of item cost) are stored inside.

Unlike vanilla Terramart, Terramart Shipments 3.0 can sell anything, not only plants, etc.

## Limitations
- Terramart Shipments 3.0 requires clear space above it (no blocks directly above it, in all area that is currently loaded). Lore-wise, it physically launches a rocket with shipments into space. More advanced Terramart Shipments 3.5 doesn't require that (it teleports the shipments, through solid rock if necessary).
- It takes 5-6 seconds for items to be shipped (all 40 slots will be shipped at once, slot that contains pixels will be ignored). If you have a large factory and are selling more than 39 item types, it may become a bottleneck.

## Tips
- Terramart Shipments 3.0 can be an alternative to Mass Incinerator: you can ship items that you don't need, such as bees. Since the cost of bees is 0, you don't get any pixels from this.
