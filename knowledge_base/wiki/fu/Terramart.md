# [FU] Terramart

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Terramart

---

**Terramart** is an agriculture-related shop in the vanilla Outpost.

It sells eggs of Farm Beasts, as well as two shops that you can place on your base: 1) furniture shop Prop Pack Truck, 2) "sell only" shop Terramart Shipments (in which you can sell crops and foods for 30% of their price instead of normal 20%).
