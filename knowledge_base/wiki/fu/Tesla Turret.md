# [FU] Tesla Turret

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tesla_Turret

---

**Tesla Turret** is an incredibly strong variant of Defense Turret that fires explosive electric projectiles. It has long range. Its chances of hitting a moving target are average (it's not as bad as Rocket Turret, but it's not stellar either).

The weakness of this turret is that its projectiles are a threat to player-built structures (they can damage the blocks), so it might not be a good choice for protecting an NPC town (unless it's built from durable blocks).
