# [FU] The Negablade

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Negablade

---

**The Negablade** is a weapon wielded by Greg crew member. This sword looks gigantic. Aside from its main Poison damage, it also inflicts Radiation Burn debuff upon the enemies.

It is likely a reference to a Castlevania enemy called Great Armor, yet by its design it looks like the one from Castlevania: Aria of Sorrow.
