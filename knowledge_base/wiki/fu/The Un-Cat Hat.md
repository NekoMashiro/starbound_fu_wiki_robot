# [FU] The Un-Cat Hat

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Un-Cat_Hat

---

**The Un-Cat Hat** is non-craftable decorative helmet with no bonuses. It is found in some treasure chests, as well as dropped by Hobo NPCs from the "deal with bandits" tenant quests.

It is a known fact that cats always land on their feet. Theoretically, were this power to be harnessed, infinite energy could be generated...

See also: Kitty Hat.
