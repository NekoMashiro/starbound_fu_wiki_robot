# [FU] Thorny Plant

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thorny_Plant

---

**Thorny Plant** is a perennial crop that only exists in the wild. You can find it on Savannah planets.

You can harvest Thorn Fruit from it as many times as you want. But it's impossible to obtain/move the plant itself (to grow it elsewhere) - if you destroy it with your MM, it won't give you the seed, and the plant will be lost.
