# [FU] Titanium Chakram

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Titanium_Chakram

---

This weapon will fly to the location clicked (if within range) or out to its maximum range of 18 tiles in that direction, hover in place for approximately 1 second (if it didn't hit anything), and then return. It will return immediately upon hitting any target or solid block. It deals physical damage.
