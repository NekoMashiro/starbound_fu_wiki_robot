# [FU] Toxic Waste

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Toxic_Waste

---

**Toxic Waste** is a cheap source of Uranium Ore. It is produced by both Fission and Fusion reactors in great quantities: 1 Toxic Waste appears every time when 1 fuel is consumed.
