# [FU] Translocator

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Translocator

---

**Translocator** is a tool that fires a "teleporter disk". You can then Right-click to teleport yourself to the disk.

The disk is affected by gravity, so it requires certain skill to use it. After fire, you have two minutes to use it again to teleport yourself to the disk.

You can craft a Translocator yourself, but there is also a Radar Dish dungeon (which you can randomly find while exploring) that will give you a Translocator for free.

## Upgrading
- When upgraded, the Translocator uses less energy to fire/teleport.
- When upgrading Translocator IV to Transwarpator (the last upgrade), the speed of projectile (launched teleporter disk) will become twice as fast.

## Alternatives
- Randomly generated bows with "Warp-Point Arrow" right-click ability.
- Void-Warp Locus (very high-tech and convenient, but doesn't work in missions).
