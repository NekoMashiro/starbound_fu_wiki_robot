# [FU] Tropical Ocean

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tropical_Ocean

---

**Ocean** planets are tier 3 vanilla planets. They feature an infinite ocean of Salt Water and are a very nice and hospitable environment (no hazards or bad weather).

However, they are also a home to a plethora of annoying monsters like Crabcano, Snuffish or Monopus. Be careful.
