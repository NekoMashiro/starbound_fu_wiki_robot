# [FU] Universal Frame

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Universal_Frame

---

**Universal Frame** prevents bees from dying due to hazardous environments (too hot, too cold, too radioactive, too damaging physically, etc.).

However, it won't help with the following:
1. It won't allow the bees to survive on Gas Giants (except few bees that already can).
1. It won't help corrupt/evil bees like Xenodaemonae to survive in non-dangerous biomes like Garden/Eden.
