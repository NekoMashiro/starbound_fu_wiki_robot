# [FU] Uranium Ore

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Uranium_Ore

---

**Uranium Ore** is a tier 3 radioactive ore. It is abundant on Irradiated and Chromatic planets, as well as underground on Penumbra, Fungal and Bog planets. It can also be found on Moons and Crystalline planets in smaller quantities.
