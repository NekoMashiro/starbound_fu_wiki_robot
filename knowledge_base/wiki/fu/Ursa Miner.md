# [FU] Ursa Miner

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ursa_Miner

---

**Ursa Miner** is a bear trader. He has a shop at vanilla Outpost.

He sells pickaxes and vanilla bars (up to Solarium Star), but not all of them are available at the beginning of the game. "What items can be bought" depends on how many vanilla main quests (given by Esther Bright) have been completed.
