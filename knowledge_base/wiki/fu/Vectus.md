# [FU] Vectus

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vectus

---

Vectus are dark blue Cactus monsters with colorful leaves atop their head, resembling typical background plants. However, due to the vectus giving off a constant purple gas akin to that of an Erchius ghost, the camouflage doesn't work as well as it could. Incredibly quick and surprisingly agile, once the Vectus senses prey within striking range, it swiftly springs to the attack.

Vectus are very fast, and capable of teleportation. They rush towards the player and do quite a bit of damage, so it is best to avoid them where possible. The Vectus is also capable of launching blasts of corruption, that inflict the Darkwater Poison debuff on a successful hit. This corrupt poison is the most deadly aspect of this creature, and it uses it frequently.

**Vectus** can be found on Desert Moon, Desert planets, Irradiated planets, and often found alongside glowing sand.
