# [FU] Vehicles

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Lists, Mechanics | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Vehicles

---

**Vehicle** is a car-like machine which you can ride. It gives you enhanced protection, movement speed and armaments, but is more bulky than a player, so it works best in open spaces (not in caves).

There two types of vehicles:
> **Mech** : Mech is a combat vehicle. It's used in space encounters (such as boarding a pirate ship), but you can also deploy it on the planet.
> It can be equipped with modular weapons, can fly, is well-armored, but consumes fuel. You can also equip it with huge Mining Drills or Mining Lasers. It also damages the blocks when you jump on them. It can mine big chunks of the terrain if you fly it up and then drop it onto the ground.
> **Mobility vehicles**: Flyers, motorbikes and submarines. They are not as durable as mechs, but they are extremely fast. They are good for scouting and moving between same-planet locations.

## Mech
Requires the same research as STL drives to craft basic mech parts.

Highly customizable, can be configured to fight, resist harsh environments, move around quickly, mine, stomp, or all at once.
