# [FU] Void-Warp Locus

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Void-Warp_Locus

---

**Void-Warp Locus** is an extremely high-tech staff that allows player to Teleport to the cursor, ignoring all walls and other obstacles. This consumes 100% of available energy with nearly all armor loadouts, so "teleport and immediately attack" is not a viable strategy.

Unlike a less advanced Translocator, this staff's Teleport ability doesn't work within instanced worlds (including friendly maps like the Science Outpost or Heaven).

If you have Precursor Data-Key in your inventory, it will work in the Science Outpost.
