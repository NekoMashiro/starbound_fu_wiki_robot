# [FU] Volcanic Moon

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Volcanic_Moon

---

**Volcanic Moon** resembles the more threatening Volcanic planets from vanilla Starbound, albeit without any atmosphere.

## Codex Description
- A volcanic moon flowing with lava and severe heat. Take care when exploring.

## Notable Features
You can find Crystal Erchius Fuel, Moonstone, Cinnabar, Plutonium Ore, Lunari Crystals, Telebrium Crystals, and Crystals on volcanic moons.

Like other Frackin' Universe moons, Volcanic Moons do not spawn Erchius Ghosts, meaning you can harvest as much Erchius fuel as you want without worrying about them.

Chili plants and volcanic-themed trees can be found and harvested on the surface.
