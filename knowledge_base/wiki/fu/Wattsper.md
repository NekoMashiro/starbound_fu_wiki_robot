# [FU] Wattsper

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wattsper

---

The **Wattsper** is an intangible spirit, similar to it's kin the [Wisper](https://starbounder.org/Wisper) both capable of passing through solid blocks. The Wattsper seems to be surrounded by a field of electricity, this dissipates and becomes concentrated into bolts of lightning when spotting prey. When attacking, they swell up and float in different directions or strait at you after they fire. They shoot lightning bolts that "burn" on the ground for about 6 seconds. Getting hit by the "fire" gives you the [Electrified](https://starbounder.org/Electrified) status while the bolts and the body of the Wattsper give [Light](https://starbounder.org/Light) and Levitation.

Wattsper are based off of Will-o'-wisps.
