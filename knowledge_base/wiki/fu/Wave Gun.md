# [FU] Wave Gun

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wave_Gun

---

**Wave Gun** is a high-tech gun that fires through blocks. It is extremely strong when fighting in complex buildings, etc.

However, its projectiles move in a wave-like pattern, and its damage isn't very high, so it's not as good in open combat.
