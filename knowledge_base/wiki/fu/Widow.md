# [FU] Widow

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Widow

---

**Widow** is a small hostile spider. They have a radioactive variant. Can also spawn Spider Silk background walls, which can be destroyed to produce the Spider Silk item, which in turn could potentially be exploited if you can make an area big enough to avoid them while mining the silk.
