# [FU] Wooden Water Tower

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wooden_Water_Tower

---

**Wooden Water Tower** is an improved Well. It slowly creates water over time.

Later in the game it can be replaced by Water Generator, which is better and can generate Methanol and Algae as byproducts.

If there are other wells (Well, Wooden Water Tower, Water Generator) within 24 blocks, then Wooden Water Tower will have significantly reduced production. Scan the Wooden Water Tower to check if it was affected by this.
