# [FU] Wretchel Fruit

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wretchel_Fruit

---

**Wretchel Fruit** creates a damage-absorbing barrier around you. Amount of damage it can absorb equals to your health (after that the barrier disappears).

If you eat this fruit again within the minute, it makes you sick (not negated by resistances/immunities to Poison), so while spamming it non-stop will protect you from external damage, the fruit itself can kill you.
