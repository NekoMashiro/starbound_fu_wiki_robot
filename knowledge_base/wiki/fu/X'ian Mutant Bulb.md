# [FU] X'ian Mutant Bulb

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/X'ian_Mutant_Bulb

---

**Mutated Seed Bulb** is a better version of X'ian Seed Bulb. 

Mutated Seed Bulb has a 19% chance to appear instead of X'ian Seed Bulb. If player is hungry (the hunger bar is below 10 food), this chance decreases to 9%.

Mutated Seed Bulb applies its heal/food bonus every second (while normal X'ian Seed Bulb needs 2 seconds).
