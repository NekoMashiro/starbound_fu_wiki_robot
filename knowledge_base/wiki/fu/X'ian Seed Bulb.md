# [FU] X'ian Seed Bulb

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/X'ian_Seed_Bulb

---

**X'ian Seed Bulb** appears when X'i player uses "Seed Bulb" racial tech. By toggling the racial ability with [F] and then crouching for about 8-10 seconds, this normally creates 1-3 X'ian Seed Bulbs, but it can instead create 1-3 X'ian Mutant Bulbs (a better version of Seed Bulb) with 19% chance (9% when Hungry), or it can instead create 1 X'ian Alpha Bulb (extremely good version of Seed Bulb) with 1% chance.

Standing near the X'ian Seed Bulb slowly replenishes health and food of X'i, Novakid or Thelusian. Small amounts are added to these stats every 2 seconds.
