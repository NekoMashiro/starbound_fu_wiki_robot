# [FU] Xeno Research Lab

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Xeno_Research_Lab

---

**Xeno Research Lab** is a faster version of Sprouting Table. It converts seeds and saplings into Genes currency. It doesn't require Power. 

Later in the game it can be replaced with even faster Advanced Xenolab.
