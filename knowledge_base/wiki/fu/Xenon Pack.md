# [FU] Xenon Pack

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Xenon_Pack

---

**Xenon Pack** is (depending on your preferences) best or second best (after Diamond Lantern) light-emitting backpack in the game. It provides light with maximum range, but this is a point light, and it's barely capable of penetrating solid blocks.
