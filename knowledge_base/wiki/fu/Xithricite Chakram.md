# [FU] Xithricite Chakram

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Weapons with Extra Properties | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Xithricite_Chakram

---

When thrown, these Chakrams will release a spray of rebounding, seeking bullets that deal cosmic damage. These bullets are the same type as used by the Hail Strike. These chakrams pierce enemies, and both the chakrams and their bullets will briefly illuminate the areas that they fly through.
