# [FU] Zerchesium Chakram

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zerchesium_Chakram

---

This weapon will fly to the location clicked (if within range) or out to its maximum range of 40 tiles in that direction, hover in place for a fraction of a second (if it didn't hit anything), and then return. It will return immediately upon hitting any target or solid block. It deals ice damage.
