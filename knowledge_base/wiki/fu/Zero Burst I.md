# [FU] Zero Burst I

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Zero_Burst_I

---

This tech will cause you to launch upwards to roughly the top of the current screen with a minor explosion. This maneuver consumes a *lot* of energy, but you do receive a Safe Fall buff for several seconds afterwards, eliminating fall damage.
