Crafting Stations / Academician's Table

## Overview
The Academician's Table is crafted by upgrading the Magician's Table.

### Requires
10 Arcanium Ore 
1 [Battery](https://starbounder.org/Battery)
4 [Refined Aegisalt](https://starbounder.org/Refined_Aegisalt)
4 [Refined Ferozium](https://starbounder.org/Refined%20Ferozium)
4 [Refined Violium](https://starbounder.org/Refined_Violium)

### Upgrade to next tier - Cyberneticist's Table
10 Electrolite Superstone 

1 Enhanced Battery
1 Jupiter Cell
