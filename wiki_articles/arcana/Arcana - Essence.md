## Overview
Arcana - Essence is a book that can be obtained in a number of ways:
- A new character starts with this item in their inventory
- Crafted directly from [C] for 1 pixel and 1 sheet of [paper](https://starbounder.org/Paper)
- Crafted at the Magician's Table for 1 pixel and 1 sheet of [paper](https://starbounder.org/Paper)
- Purchased from the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
Arcana - Essence

The most common forms of essence that you will encounter are arcane, illuminated and superstorm essence. Most essence can be extracted by materials and liquids. For example, arcane water, found in Shining Seas, can be extracted for arcane essence.
