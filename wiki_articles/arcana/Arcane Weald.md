# [Arcana] Arcane Weald

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Arcane_Weald

---

In current versions this planet's name is "Arcane", however Arcane Weald is it's planned new official name.

**Arcane Weald** is a primary biome type characterized by a abundance of arcane magic and large amount of trees. Arcane Weald planets have a 'inconceivable' threat, geared toward tier 6 in progression.

Arcane Weald planets can be found orbiting around Arcane Stars. Unless players have a Arcane EPP equipped their energy regeneration will be greatly diminished.
