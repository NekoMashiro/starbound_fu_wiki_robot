Ores / Aspirite Ore

## Overview
Aspirite Ore is an ore that can be mined on Planets orbiting Tripolar Stars. It is always found in the first underground layer, in medium sized chunks. Intima Essence can be found in the ore chunks as well.

## Usages
Aspirite ore can be refined at an Auric Smelter using 2 ore and 1 Intima Essence. It can be used for crafting one set of armor, a few augments, and Nullatus Bar.
