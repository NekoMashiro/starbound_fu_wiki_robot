Ores / Atla Gold Ore

## Overview
Atla Gold is an ore found on Shining Sea and Empyrean Planets.

## Usages
Atla Gold Ore can be refined into Atla Gold Bars at an Arcanium Furnace and extracted for Chryso Essence at an upgraded Essence Extractor. It is an ingredient for the following: 

Atla Gold Bar 

Chryso Essence
