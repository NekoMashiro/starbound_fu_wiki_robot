Aurea Overview is a codex.

## Contents
Originally known as the Aurea Collective, the Aurea is long known as an universal artifact safekeeping order, founded by the Gilten. At its head is the Artificer, who oversees preservation of artifacts as well as the development of Gilten technology.

Day after day, the Aurea's Excavators and Inquisitors travel to many distant planets, in search of lost artifacts from the Ancient Ages.

The Artificer has ruled that the Gilten must preserve and protect these artifacts from evil's reach, so that peace may prosper within the universe.

## Trivia
- Despite the Codex saying the Aurea was "Originally known as the Aurea Collective" it is still referred to with that name in recent text, even by characters within the organization.
