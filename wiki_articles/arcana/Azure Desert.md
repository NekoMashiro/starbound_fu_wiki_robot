Planets / Azure Desert

## Overview
 Azure Desert is characterized by rather dry and windy climates its surfaces is comprised of mostly sand, Azure sand and cobblestone. Azure Desert planets are of a "Unreal+" threat level, geared towards tier 8 progression wise. Their appearance is similar to Desert and Savanna planets with "oasis" pools scattered along the surface. It is imperative that any explorer that intends to traverse this planet equips a Trinity EPP.

 Azure Desert planets can be found orbiting Tripolar Stars.
