## Overview
Bosses are very powerful enemies that require skill and strategy to defeat. They are unique to other enemies in that they have a much more advanced AI, and that they cannot be afflicted with any Debuff, but at the same time possess no elemental resistance of any sort. Defeating bosses is a necessary step in upgrading your crafting stations, as they require certain crafting materials that can be obtained via each boss.
