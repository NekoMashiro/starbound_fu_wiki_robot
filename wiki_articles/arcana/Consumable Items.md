# [Arcana] Consumable Items

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Consumable_Items

---

Consumables are items that can be found or crafted. They can add helpful status effects, remove negative ones, buff stats, restore health and energy, or summon bosses. A small number of consumables also have negative effects.
