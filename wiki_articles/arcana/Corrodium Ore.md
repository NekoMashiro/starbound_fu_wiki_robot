Ores / Corrodium Ore

## Overview
Corrodium Ore is an ore that can be mined on Viridescent Planets orbiting Superstorm Stars. It can be found in small clusters close to the surface, often near acid pools. Because corrodium ore blends in well with the colors of the blocks around it, it can be tricky to spot.

## Usages
Corrodium Ore can be refined at a Modern Smelter. It is an ingredient for the following: 

 Corrodium Bar
