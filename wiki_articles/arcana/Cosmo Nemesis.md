# [Arcana] Cosmo Nemesis

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **分类**: Bosses | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Cosmo_Nemesis

---

**Cosmo Nemesis** is the second progression essential boss of the Arcana Mod. It can be encountered randomly on arenas found on Animus planets

## Attacks
Cosmo Nemisis will shoot long trails of fire at the player, along with a larger fireball.

Cosmo nemesis can spawn small copies of itself to attack the player, these copies can only shoot a singular, small fireball.

Cosmo Nemesis will attempt to slam down on the player to damage them.

In it's second stage cosmo nemesis's large fireball attack will become even larger and gain a large increase in speed
