B. Academician’s table tier

• Crafting materials

1. Enhanced battery (x10 Superstorm essence + x10 Neon water + x1 Battery)

2. x2 Holosium star (x1 Holosium bar + x1 Illuminated essence + x1 Solarium star)

3. Jupiter cell (x30 Arcane essence + x6 Electrolite superstone)

4. x10 Magiwood log (x10 Wooden log + x1 Arcane essence)

5. Regulus cell (x30 superstorm essence + x6 Electrolite superstone)

6. Renatine crystal (x2 Renatine crystal + x6 Arcane water)

• Sigils and boss spawns

1. Evo abomination (x60 Evoaquatic mud + x60 Evoaquatic dirt + x10 Illuminated essence) 

2. Sakura tribute (x60 Ketsu rock + x60 Animus brick + x10 Illuminated essence

Note : 

1. Consider mining about in the Illuminated planets, these white waters are abundant among the planet’s caves.

2. If your device doesn’t have strong specs, consider fighting Sakura tribute first. Most of Evo abomination boss’ attacks produce many lag inducing particles

C. Cyberneticist’s table tier

• Crafting materials

1. Dustrium connector (x1 Dustrium plate + x1 Gold bar + x1 Copper wire)

2. x2 Evernight crystals (x2 Magifire cyrtal + x2 Superstorm essence + 2x Necronium bar)

3. Solar core (x2 Solar core fragment + x8 Solar Essence + x6 Solite crystal Block)

• Sigils and boss spawns

1. Terminal rune (x1000 Ancient essence + x1 Tripolar essence)

Note : 

1. The terminal rune is used for Artificer tier weaponry and gear

2. The tripolar essence are found exclusive among the golems, shrine vases and bosses found on the tripolar star planets

3. The ancient essence are found exclusively in vanilla Ancient vaults

D. Artificer’s table tier

• Crafting materials

1. x10 Kuromatsu wood (10x Wooden log + x1 Lunar essence)

• Sigils and boss spawns

1. Aeon sigil, Siorraidh (x1 Aeon leaf + 10 Raw diathrylium + 20 Raw dustrium + 40 Aeon essence)

2. Azure sigil, Astecrois (x1 lapis shard + x10 Oceanite + x20 Azarium bar + 40 Azure essence)

3. Lunar sigil, Imihealach (x1 lunar insignia + x10 Lunar clematis + 20 Luminarium bar + 40 Lunar essence)

4. Poison sigil, Venetox (x1 Verdant chakram + x1 Verdant boomerang + x20 Corrodium bar + x40 Poison essence)

5. Vermilion sigil, Lowenherz (x4 Nanoweave + x10 Iron bar + x20 Rufescite + 40 Vermillion essence)
