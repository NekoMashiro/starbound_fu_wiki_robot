# [Arcana] Crew

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Crew

---

Crew (or Crewmembers) are NPCs that reside on the player's ship and provide passive benefits. (Although with Arcana's Crew Roles, they provide benefits as a buff with a timer instead of Vanilla's permanent benefits.)

Up to two crew members at a time can follow the player.
