Ores / Electrolite Ore

## Overview
Electrolite Ore is an ore that can be mined from Superstorm Expanse Planets orbiting Superstorm Stars. It can be found in clusters both near the surface and further underground, emitting a bright pink glow.

## Usages
Electrolite Ore can be smelted into Electrolite Superstone at an Arcanium Furnace. It is an ingredient for the following: 

Electrolite Superstone
