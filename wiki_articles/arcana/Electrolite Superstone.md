Bars / Electrolite Superstone

## Overview
Electrolite Superstone is a material that is smelted at an Arcanium Furnace (or higher) from 2 Electrolite Ore.

## Usages
Electrolite Superstones are used as ingredients in the following recipes: 

Aureola Bar

Galvasteel Bar
