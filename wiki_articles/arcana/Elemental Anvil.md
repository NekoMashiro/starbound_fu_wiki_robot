Crafting Stations / Elemental Anvil

## Overview
The Elemental Anvil is crafted from the Magician's Table.

### Requires
2 [Copper Bar](https://starbounder.org/Copper_Bar)
2 [Silver Bar](https://starbounder.org/Silver_Bar)
2 [Gold Bar](https://starbounder.org/Gold_Bar)
1 [Empty Bottle](https://starbounder.org/Empty_Bottle)

### Upgrade to next tier - Arcanium Anvil
18 Arcanium Bar
8 [Durasteel Bar](https://starbounder.org/Durasteel_bar)
4 [Gold Bar](https://starbounder.org/Gold_bar)
