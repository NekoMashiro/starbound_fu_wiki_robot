Crafting Materials / Enhanced Battery

## Overview
Enhanced Battery is a crafting material that is crafted at an Academician's Table(or higher)

### Requires
10 Electrolite Superstone
10 Electric essence
1 [Battery](https://starbounder.org/Battery)

## Usages
Enhanced Battery are used as ingredients in the following recipes:

Cyberneticist's Table

Modern Smelter

Orion Fabricator

Orion Essence Extractor

Orion Workstation

Exousia Armory

Exousia Workstation
