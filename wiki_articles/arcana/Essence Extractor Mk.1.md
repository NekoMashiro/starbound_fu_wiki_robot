# [Arcana] Essence Extractor Mk.1

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Essence_Extractor_Mk.1

---

Crafting Stations / Essence Extractor Mk.1

The Essence Extractor Mk.1 is crafted from the Magician's Table. It takes various liquids and automatically extracts them into Essence and other materials.
Water, slime, and oil cannot be extracted.

The Essence Extractor Mk.2 is not a direct upgrade from this item, but rather is crafted at at a Basic Tech Station (or higher).

### Requires
20 [Iron Bar](https://starbounder.org/Iron_Bar)
8 [Copper Bar](https://starbounder.org/Copper_Bar)
