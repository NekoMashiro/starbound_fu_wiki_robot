## Overview
Evolapis (codex) is a book dropped by the Evolapis boss upon defeat.

## Contents
As Ecolife's research effort grows, they set their sight on the development of new species. Their countless experiments gave birth to a myriad of lifeforms, one of which evolved into the giant golem known as the Evolapis.
