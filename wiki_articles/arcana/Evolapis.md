# [Arcana] Evolapis

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **分类**: Bosses | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Evolapis

---

Evolapis is the first progression essential boss. It can be found in an arena on Evoaquatic Planets.

### Attack
In its first stage, Evolapis alternates between spawning acid rain clouds and shooting the player with homing poison balls. The player can easily use the double jump technology to dodge the boss during its acid cloud stage, but the boss should be avoided when it starts shooting the player with poison balls. Melee weapons do well during this stage of the fight due to its habit of chasing the player around.

At half health, Evolapis switches to its second stage. At the beginning of this stage the boss flies off screen, giving the player time to snipe it with a bow or other ranged weapon. Evolapis adds three more attacks to its arsenal during the second stage: slamming into the ground, spawning giant acid clouds that rain down lightning, and firing bolts of poison at the player.

Around twenty-percent health, Evolapis begins teleporting around the player and shooting poison bolts until its death.
