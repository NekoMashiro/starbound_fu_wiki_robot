# [Arcana] Galactic Map

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Galactic_Map

---

## Overview
The Galactic Map is an item that allows the player to access various locations unique to Arcana. It is purchased/crafted at a Magician's Table for 1 pixel.

## Usages
As of Version 1.2, the Map allows the player to access the following locations:

Lighthaven 

Nova Station 

Zhushi Pond 

Astral Observatory
