Ores / Galvasteel Ore

## Overview
Galvasteel Ore is an ore that can be mined underground on Automated Planets orbiting Superstorm Stars, specifically in underground Geoelectra Caves.

## Usages
Galvasteel Ore can be refined at a Modern Smelter. It is an ingredient for the following: 

 Galvasteel Bar
