## Getting Started (Codex):
 * **Welcome to Arcana!** This mod adds a large quantity of planets and items, and most are made for late gameplay. To start your journey as a mage, you will first need to craft a Magician’s Table at an Inventor’s Table. Afterwards, you will be able to craft another essential part of Arcana, an Essence Extractor.*

## Essence (Codex):
 *The most common forms of essence that you will encounter are arcane, illuminated and superstorm essence. Most essence can be extracted by materials and liquids. For example, Arcane Water, found in Shining Seas, can be extracted for Arcane Essence.*

## Progression (Codex):
[T1-T6] Magician’s Table, Arcane Stars

[T7] Academician’s Table, Illuminated Stars

[T8] Cyberneticist’s Table, Superstorm Stars

[T9] Artificer’s Table, Tripolar Stars

## Game Mode
----

### Character Creation
At the select character screen, click create character to create a new character or an existing character to advance directly to the game.
The character creation window provides a choice between race, gender, and appearance. 
- Your race choice here will determine the appearance of the ship you’ll start with, the ship’s pet and how your character will describe objects you inspect. 
- Although most racial options are almost purely cosmetic, each race comes with its own distinct racial abilities.

## Beginning Gameplay
----

### Welcome to your Ship
After selecting a character (or completing the optional intro mission), players will appear on a ship floating over a planet. A quest window begins the game’s story (Note: The forementioned quest is purely vanilla unless otherwise altered).
- Your race choice will determine the appearance of your ship.

### Building Your First Arcana Workbench
Once you’ve obtained a decent amount of wood, cobblestone, and iron bars, you can start your journey to becoming a powerful mage by crafting the Magician’s Table. It can be made in the Inventor’s Table, or from the [C] menu.

After crafting the table, it will appear in the hotbar if there are empty spaces, and the player’s object inventory [I] if not.
