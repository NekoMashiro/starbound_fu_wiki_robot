# [Arcana] Gilten

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Gilten

---

The **Gilten** are one of the two playable Arcana races.

A race of high-tech dragonic humanoids whose culture revolves around the worship of the Lux Aureo, also known as **the Light**. The Gilten are led by a Patron Divine Dragon, who are said to be blessed by the Light itself.

Due to possessing affinities harnessed from the Light the Gilten are naturally resilient against basic elements, but weak from physical strikes.
Their cultural style is reminiscent of Greco-Roman aesthetics.

## Gilten as NPCs
Gilten are quite rare, residing mainly in the sky-bound arks located on Empyrean planets and Lighthaven
