Lore / Codex / Gray Radiance

## Overview
Gray Radiance is a book that can be purchased at the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
The Protectorate made attempts to stop the Colorless, but it was already far too late. When the Gray Radiance rose, countless planets were submerged in the Light. Those who were hit were either illuminated by the Light or desolated. With this, was also the creation of the Lumen and the Abyss.

'The forgotten lights of justice and judgement have been awoken once more', the Colorless said to the witnesses. The armored figure then disappeared, and no one has seen him in more than hundreds of years.
