Ores / Holosium Fragment

## Overview
Holosium Fragment is an ore findable on Illuminated Planets orbiting Illuminated Stars.

## Usages
Holosium Fragment can be refined into Holosium Bars at an Arcanium Furnace. It is not currently an ingredient in any other recipes.
