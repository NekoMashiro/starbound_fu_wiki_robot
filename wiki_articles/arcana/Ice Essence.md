## Overview
Ice Essence is a crafting material that is extracted at an Essence Extractor Mk.1(or higher)

Input

1 Illuminated Water 

Output

1 [Water](https://starbounder.org/water)

40% Darklight Resin

20% Ice Essence

20% Holosium Fragment

Input

1 [Liquid Erchius Fuel](https://starbounder.org/Liquid_Erchius_Fuel)

Output

1 [Water](https://starbounder.org/water)

80% Electric Essence

20% Ice Essence

20% [Crystal Erchius Fuel](https://starbounder.org/Crystal_Erchius_Fuel)

Input

Ruinous Water

Output

1 [Water](https://starbounder.org/water)

60% Darklight Resin

20% Ice Essence

20% necroium Fragment
