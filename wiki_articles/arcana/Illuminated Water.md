# [Arcana] Illuminated Water

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Illuminated_Water

---

**Illuminated Water** can be obtain in Illuminated cave biome of Illuminated planets with varying amount. Usually found on the top layer of the cavern, going deeper does not guarantee bigger pool of Illuminated Water especially if it start to transition to other cave biome.
