Lore / Codex / Land of No Color

## Overview
Land of No Color is a book that can be purchased at the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
The illuminated plains are well known for their lack of any distinct or colorful features. Their entire environment seems to be largely composed of gray landscapes, and ruins of pale palaces.

It is currently unknown who used to reside in these plains, as most records found in the ruins are largely indecipherable, but many have come to a general consensus that a large kingdom of sorts was once created there, judging by the architectural remains.
