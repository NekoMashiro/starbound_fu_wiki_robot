Ores / Luminarium Ore

## Overview
Luminarium Ore is an ore that can be mined on Eminsnow Planets orbiting Tripolar Stars. It can be found in small to medium clusters close to the surface only.

## Usages
Luminarium ore can be refined at an Auric Smelter using 2 ore and 2 crystal erchius. It can be used for crafting weapons and armor, and Nullatus Bar.
