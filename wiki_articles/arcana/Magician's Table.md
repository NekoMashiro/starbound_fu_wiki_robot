Crafting Stations / Magician's Table

## Overview
The Magician's Table is tier 1 of the main crafting station of Arcana. It is crafted from the [C] menu or from the [Inventor's Table](https://starbounder.org/Inventor%27s_Table).

### Requires
4 [Iron Bar](https://starbounder.org/Iron_Bar)
10 [Timber](https://starbounder.org/Timber)
10 [Cobblestone](https://starbounder.org/Cobblestone)

### Upgrade to next tier - Academician's Table
10 Arcanium Ore 
1 [Battery](https://starbounder.org/Battery)
4 [Refined Aegisalt](https://starbounder.org/Refined_Aegisalt)
4 [Refined Ferozium](https://starbounder.org/Refined%20Ferozium)
4 [Refined Violium](https://starbounder.org/Refined_Violium)
