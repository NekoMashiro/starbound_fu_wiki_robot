## Overview
Nullatus Bar is a material that is smelted at an Auric Smelter from the following ingredients:

- 1 Atumium Bar
- 1 Luminarium Bar
- 1 Azurium Bar
- 1 Solar Medallion
- 1 Lunar Insignia
- 1 Aeon Leaf
