Ores / Pink Aquamarine

## Overview
Pink Aquamarine is an ore that can be found on Turbulent Planets.

## Usages
Pink Aquamarine, although unable to be refined at an Arcanium Furnace, is an ingredient for the following: 

Aquamarine Dagger 

Seatide 

Aquamarine Eye 

Illuminated EPP
