# [Arcana] Plague Doctor

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **分类**: Bosses | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Plague_Doctor

---

The Plague Doctor is a boss found on Nightmist Planets.

### Lore
"In his eagerness to rid the disease plaguing his land, the doctor discovered something far greater: the cure to death itself and the potential to harness souls. From the spring of life, he grants immortality to all who have perished, giving rise to an unending surge of the undead."
