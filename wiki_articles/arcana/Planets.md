## Planets
Arcana adds many new planets, with most of them featuring unique loots, dungeons and encounters. Each of the planets can be found within the 5 new stars, ranging from a threat level from 1 to 8+. New explorers are encouraged to start at Arcane Stars before venturing into other stars, and are familiar with vanilla progression before beginning Arcana's mid to late-game content.

## General Planet Dungeons
Some dungeons are common and can be found in multiple Arcana planets.
