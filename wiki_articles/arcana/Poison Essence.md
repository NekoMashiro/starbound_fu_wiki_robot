Essence / Poison Essence

## Overview
Poison Essence is a crafting material that is extracted at an Essence Extractor Mk.1(or higher)

Input

1 [Poison](https://starbounder.org/Poison)

Output

1 Poison Essence

1 [Water](https://starbounder.org/water)

20% [Bio Sample](https://starbounder.org/Bio_Sample)

Mining Noxion Ore

60% Noxion Ore

40% Poison Essence

Poison Sigil

Refined Noxion
