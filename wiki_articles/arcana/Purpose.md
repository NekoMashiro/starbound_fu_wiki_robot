## Overview
Purpose is a book that can be purchased at the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
Taking apart machines, trash, and bodies was my only way to survive in the Badlands. I had no purpose except to live. And to live as a Scavenger wasn't much at all. You kill or be killed, that is the rule of the Badlands. There was no moral in our ways of living, I feared to be scavenged as much as the people who I scavenge.

When I was on the brink of ending my meaningless life, he appeared. 'Join me', he said. With that, I was given safety and much more. If it weren't for him, I would have never found my purpose. That is why I will follow the Knight, my savior, till my dying breath.
