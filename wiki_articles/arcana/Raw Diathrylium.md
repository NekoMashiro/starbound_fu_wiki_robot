Ores / Raw Diathrylium

## Overview
Raw Diathrylium is an ore that can be mined from Timeless Planets orbiting Superstorm Stars. Deposits of it can be found near the subbiome's surface and deeper underground. The ores emit a deeper, darker blue glow than the blocks around them. 

## Usages
Raw Diathrylium cannot be further refined at an Arcanium Furnace or any of its upgraded versions. It is an ingredient for the following: 

Aeon Sigil 

Acuity 

Sagacity 

Aeon Shortbow 

Cerueon Wings
