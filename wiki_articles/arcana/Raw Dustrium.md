Ores / Raw Dustrium

## Overview
Raw Dustrium is an ore that can be mined on Automated Planets orbiting Superstorm Stars. It can be found in small clusters close to the surface or bigger deposits underground, embedded in the metallic bedrock of the planet.

## Usages
Raw Dustrium can be refined at a Modern Smelter. It is an ingredient for the following: 

 Frame 

 Dustrium Bar
