Lore / Codex / Sanctuary's Archivists

## Overview
Sanctuary's Archivists is a book that can be purchased at the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
Many educated Arcanians are either scholars, historians or librarians of the Great Library. While literature is pursued by many, the preservation and study of history has always been the core focus of the Arcanian society.

Above those are the Archivists, who oversee Arcanian law and order with the assistance of an elite unit of soldiers, known as the Paladins. Among the Archivists, one is selected as the Grand Archivist, who diplomatically represents the whole of the Arcanians and act as a figurehead.
