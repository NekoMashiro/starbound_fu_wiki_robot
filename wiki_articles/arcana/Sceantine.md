Ores / Sceantine

## Overview
Sceantine is an ore that can be mined on most Tripolar Stars planets deep underground. The best place to obtain it is from Draco Ridge sub biome commonly found on Solarized Planets in clusters on the surface.

## Usages
Sceantine can not be refined into bar. It is an ingredient for the following: 

 Borealite Bar
