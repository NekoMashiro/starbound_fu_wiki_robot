Bars / Slipstream Composite

## Overview
Slipstream Composite is a material that is smelted at a Modern Smelter (or higher) from the following ingredients:

- 2 Ursaplate
- 1 Regulus Cell
- 2 Evernight Crystal

## Usages
Slipstream Composite is used as an ingredient in the following recipes: 

Oceanite Composite
