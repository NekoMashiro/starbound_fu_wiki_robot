# [Arcana] Status Effects

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Status_Effects

---

Status effects are conditions applied from various sources that can have both beneficial and damaging effects on the player. Status effects can be gained through item use, contact with certain blocks, liquids, traps, monsters, weapons, and the environment itself. 

## Hazards
Higher tier planets have hazardous biomes that will afflict players and mechs with harmful status effects from the moment they beam to the planet until they beam off of it. Players can be protected against some of these hazards with an appropriate EPP. The EPPs do not need to be swapped out; as players upgrade their EPP, it will protect against previous hazards.
