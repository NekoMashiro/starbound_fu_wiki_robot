Lore / Codex / Structure of The Workshop

## Overview
Structure of The Workshop is a book that can be purchased at the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
The Workshop of Gears is composed of 8 Planet sections and a singular Sun section overseeing the rest. Each Planet section focuses on one type of production, such as ammunition production for section 7 and firearm production for section 8. The Sun section, on the other hand, controls the flow and trading of the goods produced. The sections also tend to operate in close proximity of each other to facilitate the speed of transportation.
