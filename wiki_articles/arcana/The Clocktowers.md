Lore / Codex / The Clocktowers

## Overview
The Clocktowers is a book that can be bought from the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
The clocktowers located in the timeless planets are, as expected, also frozen like the planets themselves. Some have suspected that the Clocktowers are what controls the movement of time in the Timeless planets. The Aeon Timepiece, a piece of clockwork that somehow came loose, can be found at the top of the towers.
