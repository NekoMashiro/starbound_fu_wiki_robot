Lore / Codex / The Harvester (codex)

## Overview
The Harvester (codex) is a book dropped by the The Harvester boss upon defeat.

## Contents
A small fragment of an ancient being that which old scriptures have described as the Reaper of Stars. When a star falls, the Harvester will emerge to return its eons of memories to the universe's embrace.
