Lore / Codex / The Imperial Army

## Overview
The Imperial Army is a book that can be purchased from the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
A typical division within the Imperial Army is classified into one of the following groups.

Frontline Divisions, Schwarzritters 
Incendiary Divisions, Nashorns 
Stealth Divisions, Nordwolves 
Special Support Divisions 
Engineering Divisions 
Science Divisions 
Behemoths (Armored, Terrestrial) 
Leviathans (Armored, Celestial) 

Large scale military confrontation are typically handled by the infantry and armored divisions, while the engineering and science divisions handle maintenance, production and research. The special support divisions generally handle medical tasks, but sometimes work with science divisions to enforce chemical and cryogenic warfare.
