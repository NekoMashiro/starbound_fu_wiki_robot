**The Navigator** is a boss found in the Sealed Ark dungeon on Shining Sea planets

### Lore
The Navigator was a A.I that functioned as part of the navigational systems for the Starstriders ships. A long time ago the arks were sealed beneath the waters of the Shining Seas, along with the navigator. It finds no purpose in preserving the vessel, and instead seeks to guide those who hold potential
