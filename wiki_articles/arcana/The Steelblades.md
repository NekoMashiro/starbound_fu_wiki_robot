Lore / Codex / The Steelblades

## Overview
The Steelblades is a book that can be purchased at the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
Residing primarily in the Badlands, the Steelblades appear to be just another group of mercenaries. Perhaps a bit more chivalrous than most, they also seem to follow a strict set of moral codes. While they're small in size, their weaponry is highly capable and surpasses the average mercenaries.

Still, they cannot compete with the higher leagues with firepower alone. What classified them as a Titan-Class threat is their leader, the Gray Knight. There isn't much information on their activities, but they're believed to be associated with the Ironsides, and possibly even the once purged Gray Radiance. High alert is advised if you ever encounter this individual.
