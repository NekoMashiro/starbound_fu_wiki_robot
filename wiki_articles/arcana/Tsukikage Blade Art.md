Lore / Codex / Tsukikage Blade Art

## Overview
Tsukikage Blade Art is a book that can be purchased at the Bookstore on Nova Station.

## Contents
Many hylotl swordsman who reside in the Eminsnow practice the Tsukikage blade arts, and yearn to perfect the Full Moon One Cleave, a technique that once mastered would allow the practitioner to cut even rivers and mountains at ease.
