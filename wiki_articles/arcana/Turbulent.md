# [Arcana] Turbulent

**来源**: Arcana Wiki | **分类**: Planets | **链接**: https://starboundarcana.miraheze.org/wiki/Turbulent

---

Turbulent is a primary biome type characterized by deep dark oceans and a gloomy atmosphere. Turblent planets are of a ‘risky’ threat level, geared toward tier 3 in progression. Their appearance is similar to ocean planets. There are no environmental hazards in turbulent biomes which require use of an EPP aside from deep oceans which can drown players not using a Breathing EPP.

Turbulent planets can be found orbiting arcane (green) star types. Lilodon, Monopus, Miasmop, Snaggler, Crabcano, and Snuffish have a high chance of being found in turbulent biomes, and Hylotl settlements have a higher chance to appear as well.
