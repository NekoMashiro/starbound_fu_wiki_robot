Ores / Verrostin Ore

## Overview
Verrostin Ore is an ore that can be mined on Stahlern Badlands Planets orbiting Superstorm Stars. Deposits of it can be found near the subbiome's surface, emitting a faint golden glow. This effect becomes far easier to spot at night. 

## Usages
Verrostin Ore can be refined into Haken Verrostin at a Modern Smelter. It is an ingredient for the following: 

 Haken Verrostin
