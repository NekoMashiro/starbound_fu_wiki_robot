# [FU] Aeroponic Tubing

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Aeroponic_Tubing

---

**Aeroponic Tubing** is a soil that is always watered. You can plant seeds (such as Corn Seed) on this tile, and you won't need to water the crops.
As with any other soil, you still need to till it with Hoe, etc. before planting the seeds.

It does exactly the same thing as Hydroponic Tubing (the only difference is "how it looks").
