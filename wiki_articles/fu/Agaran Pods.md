# [FU] Agaran Pods

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Agaran_Pods

---

Agaran Pods are large pods made out of plant matter and water.
These microdungeons are very different than Fungal Den dungeons.

Four Agaran soldiers defend the pod while a Floran is submerged in the water.

These are usually found on Forest and Snowy planets. However, there are more variants that can be found on more hostile worlds.
