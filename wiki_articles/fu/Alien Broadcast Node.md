# [FU] Alien Broadcast Node

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Alien_Broadcast_Node

---

An artifact given to the player at the end of the Evernight Jungle quest. 

Can be researched in a microscope for Protheon. Can be given to the owner of Lorewalker's Shop in a quest after the bee tutorial in exchange for some rare bee queens. 
It has no other use.
