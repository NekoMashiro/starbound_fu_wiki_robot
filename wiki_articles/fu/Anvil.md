# [FU] Anvil

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Anvil

---

**Anvil** is a vanilla crafting station for weapons/armors. **You don't need Anvil for weapons/armors from FU+vanilla**, only for weapons/armors from other mods.

All weapons/armors from FU+vanilla can be crafted at the Armorworks instead.
