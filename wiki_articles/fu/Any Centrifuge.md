# [FU] Any Centrifuge

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Any_Centrifuge

---

**Any Centrifuge** means that this recipe works with any of the following:
- Iron Centrifuge
- Industrial Centrifuge
- Lab Centrifuge
- Gas Centrifuge

Note that low-tech Wooden Centrifuge is not included. It can only handle Honeycombs, Water, Contaminated Water and Milk.

Gas Centrifuge is more advanced than others, so it has additional recipes (see list of Gas Centrifuge-only recipes).
