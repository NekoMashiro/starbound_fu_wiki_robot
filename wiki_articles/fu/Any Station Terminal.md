# [FU] Any Station Terminal

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Any_Station_Terminal

---

**Any Station Terminal** means that 8-11 of these items will be sold as "general stock" by any of the following shops:
- Medical Station Terminal
- Military Station Terminal
- Trading Station Terminal

These shops are NOT related to player-controlled stations.

See also: Trade goods.
