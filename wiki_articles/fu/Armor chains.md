- This table shows armor sets that are used in the creation of other armor sets.
- It only includes armor sets created at the Armorworks/Assembly Line/Armory.
- (It does include some armor sets made elsewhere, but only if they build into something at the armor station)
- There are many armor sets made at the same station that are not listed here; this table shows only armors involved with other armors.
- Tier info is based on where the recipe is stored in the mod; sometimes this is inaccurate (Type-3 recon armor should be tier 6, not 5)
