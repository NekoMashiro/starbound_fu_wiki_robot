# [FU] Away In A Longship: Secrets

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Away_In_A_Longship:_Secrets

---

Away in a Longship hides a few extra chests along the way. You will also need to find 3 switches hidden inside the dungeon to unlock the 3 doors near the exit teleporter.

## Hidden chests
### Hidden chest under the bridge
If you head to the left of the lake under the blown up bridge, you will find an explosive barrel in front of a wall. Attack the barrel to destroy the wall and reach a small cave with a chest. Approaching the chest will release 4 Batongs near the entrance of the cave.

### Hidden chest at the beginning of the underground ruins
Soon after entering the underground ruins, you will reach a room with a pressure plate that drop bombs on you. To the right of this room, you will find a smaller room with a lever on a desk. Pulling that lever open a hatch further, that will let you reach an additional chest. Climb down the stairs near that room and head through the door to your left. Cross that bedroom then climb down the stairs where you will find a pond of swamp water. Dive in it and you will find a small opening to your left that you can enter with the Sphere tech. A chest awaits you inside. After exiting the swamp water pond, you can either go back the way you came from or cross the room to your right. This room has several pressure plate that activate poison traps, and Swarpions will also spawn near the end of the room (and those Swarpions also trigger the traps, but they are immune to it).

## Locked rooms
### First locked room
To unlock the first locked room, head to the very beginning of the dungeon. Just before exiting the monastery, you will see 3 armor display. The one on the left is actually a hidden switch, opening a passage in a room in the first floor of the monastery. Follow this passage and you will find a proximity sensor. Trigger it by walking near it and the first door in the exit room will open.

### Second locked room
If you cross the bridge before it blows up and get through the Alpacas forces, you will reach a building with 4 massive doors in the background where several Alpacas attack you. Climb the first tower to the left of this building to reach the next tower. This one goes higher than the others and you will find a lever at its top that open the second locked room near the exit. Be warned that pulling this lever will also summon 4 Alpacas at the bottom of the tower.

### Third locked room
Near the end of the underground ruins, you will reach a first stairs with boulders rolling down. You will then reach a room, with the exit to the right of this room. Head to the left of the room instead. Cross the first room and you will reach a small hallway with a few chest on the far left. In the middle of the hallway, a pressure plate will open the floor under you and you will fall into a small pit with 4 Swarpions. Don't panic. The wall to the left and the right of the pit can be interacted with to reveal secret doors. First head to the left of the pit and you will reach a room with several stone platforms. Some of those platforms are rigged with a pressure plate that will make the trap on the walls fire an arrow at you. In the top left of the room, you will find a wall switch that open the last locked room near the exit. After activating this switch, head back in the pit and open the walls to the right. You will find 2 pixels dispensers and a lever that open a way out of the pit.
