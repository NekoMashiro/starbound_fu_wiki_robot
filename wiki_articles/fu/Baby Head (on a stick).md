# [FU] Baby Head (on a stick)

**Baby Head (on a stick)** is a creepy Floran food. Don't worry, these are not from actual babies (I think).

Codex "Classified document. 11 - 2 - XX" (link contains spoilers) reveals the origin of this item type (and the rest of the stock of Sstabbed-Thingss Emporium).

Used in one of Kevin's quests at The Science Outpost (he wants to test how many of these heads fit in the fridge).
