# [FU] Bamboo Forest

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bamboo_Forest

---

**Bamboo Forest** sub-biome can be found on Rainforest worlds. It is the source of Bamboo, which can be crafted into furniture.

These subbiomes are largely flat, grassy biomes of clay, dirt, and mud. Stands of bamboo grow here.
