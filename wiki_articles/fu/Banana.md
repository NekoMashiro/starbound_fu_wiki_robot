# [FU] Banana

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Banana

---

**Banana** is an Apex-produced substitute for the real bananas (as existed on Earth before Ruin ate it). Unlike genuine bananas, this "Banana" never spoils.

There is a codex that mentions it being invented and tested. Apparently it's made from Coralcreep (an underwater plant, possibly a species of algae).

## Theories
Bananas harvested from the wild trees are indistinguishable from these artificial bananas, so for all we know, the trees themselves could have been artificially produced.
