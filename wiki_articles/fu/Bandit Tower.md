# [FU] Bandit Tower

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bandit_Tower

---

Bandit Towers are not like Bandit Tents. These spawn only on Ice Waste and Super Dense planets.

Up to three bandits spawn along with the tower, one in the tower and 0-2 outside. A weapon chest spawns but does not always contain loot.

The bandit in the tower is guaranteed to spawn, and carries either a rocket launcher or a sniper rifle. They also have a much longer engagement range than most normal NPCs. A second or even third bandit may or may not spawn on the bottom with a randomized weapon.

There is no difference between Bandit towers except the Bandits and their weapons.
