# [FU] Baroshk Nyano

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Baroshk_Nyano

---

**Baroshk Nyano** is a unique monster found on Proto, Gelatinous an Chromatic planets.

Highly advanced alien race, this species has achieved space flight, and advanced weaponry development. These weapons, are typically used to devious means - killing the inhabitants of settlements, and abducting survivors to serve as food. This race has a strong focus on physiological augmentation and experimentation. Other races are often abducted for horrific experiments that make even the worst of the Apex labs look like favorable in comparison. These creatures greatest downfall, is their overconfidence in their superiority to other races - cold and calculating, they are unable to form alliances with other races, or consider them worth anything more than meat or prospective testing materials.

Possessing several deadly methods of attack, the most prominent is the plasma cannon held by a few of it's many tendrils. This weapon electrifies a target, and can be fired at range with deft proficiency. Upon contact with a creature or surface, the plasma shot explodes into an electrifying field, making even near misses hit their mark. Additionally, the viscous alien possesses a poisonous knife, in which it is more than capable of dispatching a foe in brutal close quarters combat. Able to leap great distances with the swift dispelling of shadow gas from an area in it's back, the Baroshk Nyano can leap in and out of combat, disorienting any nearby enemies and sending them reeling from the forceful expulsion of the dangerous gasses. Lastly, the Baroshk Nyano possesses a plasma generator, capable of making a shield upon being damaged - this shield also serves to damage, and melt any creature within close proximity to the Baroshk Nyano.
