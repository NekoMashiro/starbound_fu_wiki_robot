# [FU] Bio Rain

**[Article is incomplete]**

Bio Rain is a weather event where Organic Soup rains from the sky and onto the planet. The soup piles up quickly so if you are there to farm the soup it is advised to dig a pit and standby to harvest the liquid.

It also allows the growth of Algae on Bog planets.
