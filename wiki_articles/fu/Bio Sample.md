# [FU] Bio Sample

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bio_Sample

---

**Bio Sample** exists as ore on Rainforest planets (looks like bright flowers inside the block). It can also be found by breaking bushes in many other biomes that are overgrown with plants, such as Primeval, Proto World, Bog, Penumbra, etc.

## Decorative value
In decorative builds, Material Mod Placer can be used to add Bio Sample to blocks ("Rainforest Flowers" effect).
