# [FU] Biome chests

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Mechanics | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Biome_chests

---

**Biome chests** are containers like Weapon Chest that are found in many biomes. Their contents are randomized (determined by some treasure pool).

Note that dungeons/microdungeons can have other chests (different from what you normally find in that biome), and can have other loot in them.
