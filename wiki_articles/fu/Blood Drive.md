# [FU] Blood Drive

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Blood_Drive

---

**Blood Drive** obtains various biomaterials from nearby Tenants (only those summoned with Colony Deed Mark II, not with the regular Colony Deed) within the distance of 128 blocks.

Having this building slightly decreases the happiness of tenants (which decreases the rent they will pay to the Colony Core).
