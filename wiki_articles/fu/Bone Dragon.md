# [FU] Bone Dragon

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bone_Dragon

---

The Bone dragon is a Boss introduced in vanilla, but modified in Frackin' Universe. It is more resilient, has elemental resistances and weaknesses, and some more attacks.
It can be found as a boss at the end of the Baron's Keep.

[See the Starbounder article for more information.](https://starbounder.org/Bone_Dragon)
