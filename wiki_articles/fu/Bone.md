# [FU] Bone

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bone

---

**Bone** is found as ore on Garden and Primeval planets. It can be hand-crafted into tier 1 weapons, including an incredibly powerful Bone Wand.

You also get several Bone from accidentally breaking Fossils with Matter Manipulator or from their unsuccessful excavation (while successful excavation yields both fossil as an item and additional rewards).
