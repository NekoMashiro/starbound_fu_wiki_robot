# [FU] Boom Cannon

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Boom_Cannon

---

**Boom Cannon** fires a medium-range rocket with a mining charge (it is very effective at mining blocks). The rocket detonates after 3 seconds or when it hits something. It harms enemies and sets them on fire.

This weapon's Alt ability causes an even bigger explosion **very close to you**. Alt ability doesn't harm enemies directly, but it will set them on fire.

## Advantages
Unlike a Mining Laser, Boom Cannon can hit background blocks (because the timed detonation happens even if the rocket didn't hit anything in the foreground layer). Mining Laser fires a constant stream of projectiles, and they cause "explosions" only if they hit something.
