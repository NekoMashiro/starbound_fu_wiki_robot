# [FU] Broken Master Manipulator

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Six vanilla artifacts | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Broken_Master_Manipulator

---

**Broken Master Manipulator** is an EPP Augment. You receive it from the Lost and Found merchant (Khe) after defeating The Ruin (final vanilla boss). In vanilla lore, this is an artifact that was gifted to Humans by the Cultivator.

It can be crafted into Master Manipulator.
