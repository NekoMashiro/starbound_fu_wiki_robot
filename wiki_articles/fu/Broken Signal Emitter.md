# [FU] Broken Signal Emitter

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Broken_Signal_Emitter

---

**Broken Signal Emitter** is a placeable object with 1 wiring output. It never sends ON signal.

You can connect it to a Door to keep it always closed (neither you nor NPCs would be able to open that Door).

See also: Signal Emitter (which is always ON) - it can keep the Door always opened.
