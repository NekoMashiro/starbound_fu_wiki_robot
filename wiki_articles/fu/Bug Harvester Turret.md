# [FU] Bug Harvester Turret

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Bug_Harvester_Turret

---

**Bug Harvester Turret** catches any insects (including bees) within 12 blocks. These insects are targeted with a special non-damaging projectile; being hit by this projectile is equal to being hit by a Bug Net.

Captured insects will drop to the ground as items. You can use a Grabber if you want to automatically store them in a container.
