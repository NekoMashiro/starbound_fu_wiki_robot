# [FU] CROC Arm

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/CROC_Arm

---

The CROC Arm is a very powerful melee weapon that appears as a chainsaw and fires short range crescents where aimed.

The chainsaw will quickly spin up and then swing wherever the mouse is aimed. During this, it will spawn a shortrange crescent that deals around 222 electric damage. 

If the player clicks right after the chainsaw is swung, it will fire an additional crescent. This must be done through clicking a second time, and cannot be done by holding the mouse down. This brings the total damage per attack cycle to around 444 per arm.
