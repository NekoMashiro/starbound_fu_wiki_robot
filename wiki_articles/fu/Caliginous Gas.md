# [FU] Caliginous Gas

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Caliginous_Gas

---

**Caliginous Gas** is a hazardous gas. It can be found on some Gas Giant planets in infinite quantities.

On contact, the gas inflicts a weak damage over time debuff and restricts air mobility. Immunity to it can be gained with either Shadow immunity (from armors, etc.), or with Caliginous Gas immunity, or with 50% (or more) Shadow Resistance.
