# [FU] Campfire

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Campfire

---

**Campfire** is used to cook basic foods (like Cooked Steak or Boiled Rice). More complex foods are cooked at Kitchen Counter.
