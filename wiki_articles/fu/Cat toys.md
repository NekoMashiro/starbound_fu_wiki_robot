# [FU] Cat toys

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cat_toys

---

**Cat Toys** is a quest at the scientist outpost given by Khe (the cat person near the entrance in an officer-like uniform). You will need an Accordion and a Ghost Lantern (both of which are random drops) to complete the quest.
