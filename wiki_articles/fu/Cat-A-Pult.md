# [FU] Cat-A-Pult

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cat-A-Pult

---

This weapon deals ice, physical, and cosmic damage, with a tiny amount of shadow damage. The alt fire also applies a weak and slow poison damage-over-time. It plays cat sound effects when fired.

This weapon is probably a reference to the card game "Exploding Kittens" by Elan Lee, Matthew Inman (of "The Oatmeal"), and Shane Small which is a temporal paradox as this mod and the weapon were created before the publishing date of the card game.
