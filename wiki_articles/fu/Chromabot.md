# [FU] Chromabot

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Chromabot

---

The **Chromabot** are similar construct creatures to their counterpart the Bobot. However their metal frame is a deep purple chroma color. Arguably more dangerous due to their increased speed and vexing abilities when compared to their basic metal counterparts. When aggroed on a creature, their eyes and markings will change from purple to brilliant green. They fight by charging up and then releasing pockets of shadow gas and green orbs of anti-matter. Upon touching the green orbs that will spiral and dance sporadically upon release, a creature is affected with the Light status and "Shadow Gas". Both of these combined, make for one exhilarating ride! The black pockets of shadow gas will launch creatures in random directions, and hitting the green orbs will send a creature flying even faster. In a small room, expect to hit a wall a minimum of 37 times in rapid succession.
