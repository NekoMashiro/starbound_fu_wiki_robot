# [FU] Chromingo

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Chromingo

---

The **Chromingo** are similar to their kin the [Toumingo](https://starbounder.org/Toumingo) both being brightly colored birds. The difference is that Chromingos have technicolor feathers and shoot psychic waves at the player. Additionally, one might want to give this bird plenty of room as they seem quite clumsy and upon falling, cause a small prismatic explosion that can deal significant damage to anything nearby. Not the best technique for fishing one would imagine.

## Luna's Lab Notes
~ This monster quite fast when compared to many other monsters, sprinting and diving at high speeds. Additionally, the damage dealt by the prismatic explosion after contact with the ground upon a leap deals seems to deal Cosmic damage which can be useful against most creatures as many lack resistance to this damage type.
