# [FU] Cleaning Bot

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cleaning_Bot

---

The Cleaning Bot is a small silver and red robots that barely comes up to a humanoid's shins and resided in [USCM Bunkers](https://starbounder.org/USCM_Bunker).

While fragile and easy to destroy, they possessed a weak electrical attack that they would use on enemies if they were hit and somehow survived the blow.

.
**Cleaning Bot **is a unique monster found on any kind of **Cyber Sphere** world. It is capturable.

They seems to have a variant.
