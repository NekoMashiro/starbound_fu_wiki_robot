# [FU] Cloning Lab

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Saplings | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cloning_Lab

---

**Cloning Lab** can combine two saplings (one with foliage A, another with stem B) into two saplings, both of which have foliage A and stem B.

This is used to improve drops from trees, and also for decorative purposes (resulting trees can look very unique and/or weird). Foliage that drops Geode is not accepted by the Cloning Lab.

There is a working Cloning Lab at the Science Outpost.
