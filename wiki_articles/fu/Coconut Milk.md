# [FU] Coconut Milk

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Coconut_Milk

---

**Coconut Milk** is a vanilla liquid that spills on the ground when you throw a Coconut. It can be collected as any other liquid. It can't be found as naturally occurring puddles/lakes anywhere.

Coconut Milk is infinitely mixable.
