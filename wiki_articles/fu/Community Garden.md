# [FU] Community Garden

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Community_Garden

---

**Community Garden** is a Tenant-based machine for growing/harvesting plants. 

Its efficiency greatly depends on the presence of tenants (only those summoned with Colony Deed Mark II, not with the regular Colony Deed) within the distance of 128 blocks around the Colony Core. When there is only one tenant nearby, it will not be any better than the basic Growing Tray. However, each tenant will increase the yield (how many items are obtained on each harvest). For a large town this Tray can become incredibly effective.

It consumes 3 Water per harvest (same as the basic Growing Tray, while the advanced Hydro Tray consumes only 2).
