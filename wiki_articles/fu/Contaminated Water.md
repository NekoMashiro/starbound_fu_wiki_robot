# [FU] Contaminated Water

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Contaminated_Water

---

**Contaminated Water** is a byproduct of centrifuging other liquids. It has some use in chemistry (including the creation of Unrefined Fuel, which is a good fuel for mechs).

Contaminated Water is infinitely mixable.
