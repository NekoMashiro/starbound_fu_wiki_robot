# [FU] Cottonbop

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cottonbop

---

**Cottonbop** is a somewhat peaceful monster: it will cause touch damage, but won't pursue you unless you attack it.

You can grow your own Cottonbops from Cottonbop Bulb (dropped by wild Cottonbops).
