# [FU] Cultist Altar (Microdungeon)

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cultist_Altar_(Microdungeon)

---

A Cultist Altar is a random spawn consisting of three Occassus cult members and a Cultist Altar. No Cultist Scandroids are present. There is no loot to be found except the Altar, which provides a small healing boost when used.
