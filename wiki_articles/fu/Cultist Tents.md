# [FU] Cultist Tents

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Cultist_Tents

---

Cultist Tents are a rare variation of the Bandit Tents. All information of Bandit Tents applies to the Cultist Tents.
The main difference is the replacement of the bandits with Occassus cult members. The green tents found in some Bandit camps are replaced by the Occassus purple tents. 

Usually, one cultist will have a capture pod with a Cultist Scandroid.
