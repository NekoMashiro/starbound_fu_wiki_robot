# [FU] Damaged Pistol

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Damaged_Pistol

---

The **Damaged Pistol** is one of the weapons the player starts the game with. It is located within the ship locker when a character is first created and every character receives one. It can be upgraded to the Protectorate Pistol which will improve it slightly as well as visually repairing the pistol.

The Damaged Pistol is an intentionally bad weapon as one of the first weapons the player is given to defend themself with along with the Broken Broadsword, a new weapon should be pursued as quickly as possible.

While it is weak it is worth holding on to the Damaged Pistol (and the Broken Broadsword) for later in the game as it can be upgraded once the player has acquired more materials.
