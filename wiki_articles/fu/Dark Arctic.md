# [FU] Dark Arctic

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Arctic

---

**Dark Arctic** is a planet similar to Arctic ocean, but it orbits a Black Star that doesn't give any light.

50% of these planets have Water, while other 50% have Salt Water. There is also a 50% chance that the bottom of this ocean will be a layer of infinite Liquid Nitrogen.

These planets are very beautiful at night.
