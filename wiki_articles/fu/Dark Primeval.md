# [FU] Dark Primeval

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Primeval

---

**Dark Primeval** is a very spooky primeval world, holding both swamp monsters and dinosaurs, a bad combination for someone even late-game. Bring your toughest gear.

## Codex Description
- There is little light penetrating the tree canopies here on this dark primeval forest planet. Bring a light-source.
- Sunlight rarely touches this dark primeval world, yet somehow the plant life grows thick and lush.
- Darkness covers the dense woodlands of this dark primeval planet
