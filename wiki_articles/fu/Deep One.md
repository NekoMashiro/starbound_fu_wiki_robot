**Deep Ones** are a race of undersea-dwelling humanoids whose preferred habitat is deep in the ocean (hence their name). However, despite being primarily marine creatures, they can come to the surface, and can survive on land for extended periods of time. All Deep Ones are immortal; none die except by accident or violence, and can grow to massive size. The Deep Ones are humanoid beings with fish, human and amphibian-like traits, they are described as having grey-ish green, glossy and slippery skin with white stomachs. They have scaled, ridged backs, as well as webbed hands and gilled necks. Notably, they have a head similar to that of a fish, with eyes incapable of blinking.

They move about in an inhuman fashion, by hopping oddly, and occasionally moving on all fours. Deep Ones possess an abhorrent, croaking voices that speaks a language unknown to any but their race. Additionally, it is said that the race of Deep Ones are so horrid that their mere appearance can cause loss of consciousness for viewers. 

Deep Ones attack by charging prey and use their massive claws and powerful maw to tear most things apart with ease. Upon a kill, the Deep One will distend it's jaw in preparation to devour any creature unfortunate to encounter them. Additionally, the Deep one possess the ability to retch and vomit a host of large partially digested fish with such force that contact with the foul attack melts and bludgeons most creatures to death within moments.

**Deep One **is a unique monster found in the Delta Freya II mission, Elder Core, Eldritch Field minibiomes. It is not captureable.
