# [FU] Defense Matrix

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Techs, Disambiguation pages | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Defense_Matrix

---

**Defense Matrix** is a body tech (in your Tricorder) that grants passive bonus to Protection if this tech is currently equipped.

There are several levels:
- Defense Matrix I - gives +5 to Protection.
- Defense Matrix II - gives +10 to Protection.
- Defense Matrix III - gives +15 to Protection.
