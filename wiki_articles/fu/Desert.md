# [FU] Desert

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Desert

---

**Desert** planets are tier 2 vanilla planets. Aside from being uncomfortably warm and having Sandstorms, they are no more dangerous than a Forest planet.

You always have a Desert planet in your starting system. It's a good source of early Tungsten Ore (which you need for your first FTL Drive).

## Desert Heat
Desert Heat is a minor hazard that can be safely ignored (it deals slight damage over time, but can be easily outhealed). It's completely negated at night, underground or when submerged in liquid.
