# [FU] Devastator Mech Arm

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Devastator_Mech_Arm

---

The Devastator Mech Arm is a large, heavy minigun that rapidly fires a spray of bullets.

When the player holds down their mouse, the gun will 'spin up' for around half a second, and then will begin firing. 

The bullets have a spread that's decent, but not wide enough to require getting too close to enemies.

The gun fires the bullets fast enough that the bullets can actually mine through tiles, but the tiles will not drop anything, so it can't be used to drill ores.

This gun is very noisy especially when you have two of them. It can drown out other sound effects easily.
