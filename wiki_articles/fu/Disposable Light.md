# [FU] Disposable Light

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Disposable_Light

---

**Disposable Light** is a consumable that makes you glow for 20 minutes after using it.

It is significantly less bright than light-emitting backpacks (Plasma Light Pack, etc.), but using it keeps your EPP slot and augment slot free.
