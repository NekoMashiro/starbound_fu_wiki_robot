# [FU] Drops from trees

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Mechanics | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Drops_from_trees

---

**Drops from trees** are items that you get when chopping trees.

## How are drops determined
Drops are defined by **Foliage** (leafy part of the tree) and **Stem** (woody trunk of the tree). Each Sapling is a combination of 1 Stem and 1 Foliage.
> For example, protoleaves foliage gives 1 Protocite and 1 Sapling, and prototree stem gives 3 Wooden Logs. By cutting a "protoleaves + prototree" tree, you get both.
> Furthermore, you get way more than just 3 Wooden Logs, because this number is further increased by the height of the tree.

Some types of trees don't drop a Sapling and can't be replanted. This includes vines (which grow upside down and usually yield Plant Fibre). Parts like penumbriteroot are only used for vines, not for regular trees.

## Making custom trees
If you have two saplings: one with foliage A, another with stem B, then you can use the Cloning Lab to create an A+B sapling.

For example, you can add Red Apples to a giant tree with Redwood Bark wood, or add Coconuts to Kelp. Some of these trees will look epic, others will be an abomination.

## How to chop trees
With your Matter Manipulator (slow), with Chainsaw (fast, produces funny sounds) or with Harvester Beamgun (instant, attempts to automatically replant the Saplings).
