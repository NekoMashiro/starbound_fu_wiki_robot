# [FU] Effigiflora

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Effigiflora

---

**Effigiflora** is a unique evolution of the rare [Mandraflora](https://starbounder.org/Mandraflora) that has a hard-to-spell name. This variety is noted by it's pitch black body, and ominous glowing red eyes. When spotted by an Effigiflora, it will act the same as a Mandraflora.

When it charges the spin attack, it zips across the screen at an unreal speed. Touching an Effigiflora does a good chunk of damage to you, which makes this a fairly dangerous enemy if unprepared or careless.

When killed, it spawns a sparkling portal that lasts for five seconds. This explains the "Phases dimensions when murdered" part of its description. Touching the portal will deal damage, so keep clear.

The **Effigiflora**is a unique monster that can be found on Penumbra planets, and they are indeed catchable.
