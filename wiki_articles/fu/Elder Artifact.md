# [FU] Elder Artifact

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Elder_Artifact

---

**Elder Artifact** is a very rare Elder crafting component. It is found in Delta Freya II mission and nowhere else. It's not a random drop, this mission has a chest that always has it.

It's used in many combination recipes of Elder Altar (read Elder codexes, as they will often tell you what these combinations are).
