# [FU] Energy Lobber

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Lobber

---

Primary fire launches tiny, bouncy orbs that that ricochet around for 4 seconds. Alt-fire is a missile that explodes on contact and creates an "energy net" barrier that cannot be crossed. If the alt-fire missile hits a foe, they will be trapped in the energy net, immune to damage, but still able to perform ranged attacks.
