# [FU] Erchius Converter

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Erchius_Converter

---

**Erchius Converter** can be found in underground Precursor biomes (inside the large, well-illuminated rooms). It turns many ship fuels into superior Quantum Fluid. Be aware that there is also a Faulty version that produces Bags of Farts instead of Quantum Fluid.

It doesn't require power, but processing takes some time.
