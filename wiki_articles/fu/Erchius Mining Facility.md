The **Erchius Mining Facility** is an **optional** tier 3 mission that you can undertake for Penguin Pete.

## Vanilla
In Giraffe, this was an optional mission. In post-1.0 vanilla, it is required to do this mission in order for your ship to be repaired, allowing you to travel to other planets and systems. This essentially makes it a "beginner's dungeon" that is necessary to progress through the game. It is also possible to skip a large part of the quest simply by taking the upper route at a certain point, and there is little to no point to taking the path of most resistance.

On top of that, the vanilla enemies consists entirely of moontants with 3 infected miners thrown in at the bottom. Some of which could be cheesed via shooting rockets or other AOE items at shafts containing '[monster closets](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/MonsterCloset)'.

At the end of the dungeon, you would face off against the Erchius Horror, which is immune to all attacks other than the mining laser, making it purely a puzzle boss.

## Frackin Universe
In Frackin Universe however, the (The Dark Cavern, or "Poptop Cavern") is the first dungeon the player encounters, which the player must complete in order to leave the starter world. Upon completing this quest, Esther has Penguin Pete repair your ship right away (if using vanilla ship. If using BYOS, it instead starts a miniature BYOS tutorial questline. However, this questline does not in any way necessitate the completion of Erchius mine.) 

Because of this, the Erchius Mine quest no longer has any relevance to the main quest and no longer affects progression in any way (much like how it was back in Giraffe). Despite this oldfound optional-status, the quest itself has gotten a major facelift. The entire dungeon has been essentially remade from the ground up (remade in Tiled, whereas the vanilla version still used the png format.)

### Changes
- Tier has been restored to tier 3 to accommodate its optional status.

- There are now many alternate routes and secrets that require the microsphere to reach, and the matter manipulator to uncover. These alternate routes contain treasures.

- No more "monster closets". Instead, these enemies spawn out of holes in the wall, removing the cheese in these encounters.

- More enemies. Not just more moontants, but more enemy variety. Berserk robots, wattspers, and purple moontants with a larger ranged attack join the obstacles that bar your way.

- Hallways that were once devoid of enemies (specifically, the long shaft preceding the Erchius Horror) are now filled with enemies.

- The upper route is barred off. It is no longer possible to skip a fourth of the whole level via that route.

- This also means you *must* have a sphere tech of some sort (since the aforementioned Dark Caverns provides the basic one, this should not be a problem.)

- Erchius Horror can be destroyed by means other than the mining laser (however it is very heavily armored, so this might be more trouble than it's worth.)

 - Defeating it without the laser will reveal a hidden room at the end of the map with some loot, including access to a Erchius Buddy (an item normally only obtainable through the Metaphysics Research Tree.)
