# [FU] Exploration Diary (codex)

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Exploration_Diary_(codex)

---

**Exploration Diary** is a codex found in Delta Freya II. It is found off the body of an old corpse located just before the cavern requiring a grappling hook or some form of gravity-defying tech to progress properly. It is dated as an exploration conducted sometime in the year 2004.
