# [FU] Extraction Lab MKII

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extraction_Lab_MKII

---

**Extraction Lab MKII** is a faster variant of Extraction Lab.

Extraction Lab MKII does 1 extraction every 2.45 seconds. Later in the game it gets replaced by Quantum Extractor, which produces significantly more items from the same inputs.
