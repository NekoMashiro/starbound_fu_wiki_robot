# [FU] Extraction

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Mechanics | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Extraction

---

**Extraction** is a way to convert basic (sometimes unneeded) materials into useful resources.

You can place a Lab Directory near your equipment to view possible extractions in-game. You can also see them in "How to obtain" and "Used for" sections of this wiki.

## Advantages of extraction
1. Turning inventory clutter into useful resources
1. Obtaining new resources
1. Getting more bars out of the respective ore and some byproduct at the cost of electricity
1. Alternate way to obtain Sand, Oil and ores, to name a few.
