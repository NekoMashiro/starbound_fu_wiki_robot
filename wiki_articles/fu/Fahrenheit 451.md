# [FU] Fahrenheit 451

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fahrenheit_451

---

In-game behavior and stats are much different than the tooltip on this page.

This weapon requires you to "charge" the attack before releasing. At the lowest level of charge, two small fireballs are released in a fairly flat trajectory. At maximum charge, there are 5 fireballs released. There is no alt-fire for this weapon. Going by the in-game tooltip alone, a maximum-charged shot deals 6.5*5 at the cost of 200 energy, making this weapon among the worst in the game.
