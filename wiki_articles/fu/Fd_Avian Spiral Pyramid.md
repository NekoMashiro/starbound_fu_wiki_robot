Avian Spiral Pyramids are a dungeon found on the surface level of Proto World planets. The dungeon consists of a twisting tunnel, bordered by lava and shaped inside a square The structure appears to be some type of pyramid, and made out of Temple Blocks. The pyramid is inhabited by (skeletal birds?) and decorated with avian temple furniture. On either side of the pyramid are small wooden shelters that protect little graveyards.

Avian Spiral Pyramids don't feature any sort of tile protection, and so can be broken down for resources.

Loot that can be found in the chests includes:
- Phoenix Circlet
- Throwing Kunai
- Pixels
One interesting thing that can be found in the Avian Spiral Pyramids are Human Sarcophagus's with live humans trapped inside. By interacting with the piece of furniture several times in a row, you are able to free the human inside, who then offers to join your crew in thanks for rescuing them.
