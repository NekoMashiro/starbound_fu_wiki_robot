---

**Black Stars** are an anomalous star type with a very apt name. One of the greatest unanswered questions in science is why these stars give life and warmth, despite throwing off next to no light.

Life on the planets orbiting these stars have evolved to the absurdly harsh conditions; therefore, they are extremely dangerous to mess with. Even the planets themselves are a threat.

It is extremely recommended to equip high tier gears, as the threat level on these planets are extremely high.
