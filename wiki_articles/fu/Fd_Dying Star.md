# [FU] Fd:Dying Star

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fd:Dying_Star

---

Dying Stars are greeny-blue coloured stars usually containing mid-tiered and high tiered planets. They are one of the less common stars, but are easy to spot due to their unique green light emittance on the map. They rarely contains any of the most dangerous and rarest planets, such as Dark Atropus worlds. They are perhaps the second most dangerous Star system, behind Black Stars.
