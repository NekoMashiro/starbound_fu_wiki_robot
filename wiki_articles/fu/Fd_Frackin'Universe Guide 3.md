### Badlands
You won't find much in terms of plant life here, but you will find ore and minerals. There are most common in deserts.

### Blisterbush Field
Well, this is one way to stock up on blister pods on the fly. Useful. Most common on Proto Worlds.

### Bloodstone Field
Much like the planet type, Bloodstone Fields are downright strange.

### Caliche Field
A great place to stock up on Iodine and Saltpeter by way of Caliche.

### Hellfire Field
You'll find tons of Hellfire plants in these small regions. Oh, and lava.

### Irradiated Waste
Essentially the same as their planet-size selves.

### Lava Lake
Lava. That's...pretty much it.

### Meadow
Flowers. Lots of flowers.

### Wasteland
A dead expanse of nothing fun or happy. Don't smile.

### Pitch
Pitch regions are full of heat, tar and bones. The upside is that they provide abundant oil.

### Primeval Forest
Ancient forests loaded with massive trees. A very useful place to stock up on wood.

### ... and several dozen more!
There are quite literally dozens of other micro biomes to be discovered, loaded with useful resources for your studies. Keep alert and explore!
