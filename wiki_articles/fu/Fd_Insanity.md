## Summary:
Insanity is a Frackin' Universe Status Effect. It is primarily found on Atropus worlds. However, this can be occasionally gained on other worlds as well. You can tell when this occurs when you will suddenly start hearing a strange voice chanting in the background. This is also the unfortunate effect of coming into contact with Essentia Obscura, certain enemy attacks, or eating Elder Rotcombs (the product of Elder Bees).

## Effects
When insanity is gained on an Atropus world, you will be greeted with two Insanity debuffs which slows down and inverts your movement, if an Atropus Ghost comes too close to you, your energy will rapidly decrease while your hunger gradually decreases.

You will constantly be greeted with messages of your own thoughts stating that you need to eat raw flesh and the messages gradually become worse when your hunger drops more.

In addition, you will begin to take a very minor amount of damage, and your armor rating will begin to fall.

On non-Atropus planets, the effects are far less dramatic, and are restricted to the aforementioned damage/armor rating loss. This restriction also applies to NPCs.

## Codex Description:
**Insanity:** A constant degradation of your mental state or your brain not being able to transfer what's happening, insanity saps your ability to protect yourself over a sustained period by draining your Protection.

Additionally, it gives a mild bit of damage per tick.

Augments and armor exist to combat this nasty effect. You'll find this effect on Atropus biomes.
