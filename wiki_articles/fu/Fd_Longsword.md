**Longsword **is a one-handed procedurally generated melee weapon model. It can be common, uncommon, and rare.

## Base Statistics
- **Base DPS: **7.5-8.5 health/s (Common), 7.6-8.75 health/s (Uncommon), 7.7-9.0 (Rare)
- **Fire Time: **0.8-1.0 seconds between 1x-damage attacks (~1.0-1.3 1x-damage attacks/s)
- **Combo Progression:**
 - **First Strike: **1.0x damage, 15 default knockback
 - **Second Strike: **0.7x damage, 10 default knockback
 - **Third Strike: **0.8x damage, 15 default knockback
- **Crit Chance: **1%
- **Base Crit Damage Bonus: **+5 damage

## Elemental Damage
Uncommon and rare Longswords are assigned one of seven elements (non-physical damage types) on generation. Both uncommon and rare Longswords will inflict the appropriate elemental status effect with the primary attack.
- **Fire:** Inflicts Burning
- **Ice:** Inflicts Frost
- **Poison:** Inflicts Poisoned
- **Electric:** Inflicts Electrified
- **Radiation:** Inflicts Radiation Burn
- **Shadow:** Inflicts Shadow Gas
- **Cosmic:** Inflicts -Armor debuff

## Special Attacks
Longswords, like most one-handed weapon types, cannot generate with any special attacks.
