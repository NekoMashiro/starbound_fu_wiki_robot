# [FU] Fd:Machine Pistol

**Machine Pistol **is a one-handed procedurally generated ranged weapon model.

It can be common, uncommon, and rare. Common Machine Pistols will shoot bullets, while uncommon and rare ones will shoot elemental plasma shots.

Note: though Machine Pistols are in vanilla *Starbound*, their characteristics are modified significantly in Frackin' Universe. For the original characteristics, consult the official *Starbound* wiki, Starbounder (http://starbounder.org/).

## Base Statistics
- **Base DPS: **6.0-6.5 health/s (Common), 6.5-7.0 health/s (Uncommon/Rare)
- **Fire Time: **0.06/0.05/0.04-0.12/0.18 seconds between singly-fired shots (Common/Uncommon/Rare-unRare/Rare) (~5.6/8.3-16.7/20.0/25.0 shots/s) (unRare/Rare-Common/Uncommon/Rare)
- **Energy Usage: **18.0-19.5 energy/s (Common), 19.5-21.0 energy/s (Uncommon/Rare)
- **Fire Mode:** Automatic, Burst
- **Shots per Burst:** 2-6
- **Burst Spacing:** 0.05/0.04/0.035 seconds between consecutive shots (Common/Uncommon/Rare)
- **Knockback: **2-4 (extremely weak to very weak knockback)
- **Crit Chance: **1% (Common/Uncommon), 2% (Rare)
- **Base Crit Damage Bonus: **+1 damage (Common), +3 damage (Uncommon/Rare)

## Elemental Damage
Uncommon and rare Machine Pistols are assigned one of seven elements (non-physical damage types) on generation. For all elements, only rare Machine Pistols will inflict the appropriate elemental status effect in addition to dealing elemental damage.
- **Fire:** Inflicts Burning
- **Ice:** Inflicts Frost
- **Poison:** Inflicts Poisoned
- **Electric:** Inflicts Electrified
- **Radiation:** Inflicts Radiation Burn
- **Shadow:** Inflicts Shadow Gas
- **Cosmic:** Inflicts -Armor debuff

## Special Attacks
Machine Pistols, like most one-handed weapon types, cannot generate with special attacks.
