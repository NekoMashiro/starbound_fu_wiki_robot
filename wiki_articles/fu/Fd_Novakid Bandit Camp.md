# [FU] Fd:Novakid Bandit Camp

---

Novakid Bandit Camps are randomly generated structures. They may be found on any planet type (however are much more common and likely to spawn on planets in a star with a more dangerous rating) and contain hostile Novakids.

Camps include tents, housing facilities, complex underground mineshafts, saloons, Mooshi, cooking stations, and smelting stations. The two entrances (and exits) to the camps are guarded by tower-like structures, on which a hostile Novakid will be positioned and attempt to attack the player on sight. The camp is protected by Shield Generators which can be subsequently destroyed when found by a damage-dealing tool or the Matter Manipulator's primary mode.
