**Rapier **is a one-handed procedurally generated melee weapon model. It can be common, uncommon, and rare.

## Base Statistics
- **Base DPS: **5.0-5.5 health/s
- **Fire Time: **0.4-0.7 seconds between 1x-damage attacks (~1.4-2.5 1x-damage attacks/s)
- **Combo Progression:**
 - **First Strike: **1.0x damage, 10 default knockback
 - **Second Strike: **0.6x damage, 8 default knockback
 - **Third Strike: **0.8x damage, 9 default knockback
 - **Fourth Strike: **0.7x damage, 8 default knockback
 - **Fifth Strike: **0.4x damage, 9 default knockback
 - **Sixth Strike: **1.2x damage, 12 default knockback
- **Crit Chance: **1% (Common), 2% (Uncommon), 3% (Rare)
- **Base Crit Damage Bonus: **+2 damage (Common), +3 damage (Uncommon), +4 damage (Rare)

## Elemental Damage
Uncommon and rare Rapiers are assigned one of seven elements (non-physical damage types) on generation. Neither uncommon nor rare Rapiers inflict elemental status effects.
- **Fire:** Inflicts Burning
- **Ice:** Inflicts Frost
- **Poison:** Inflicts Poisoned
- **Electric:** Inflicts Electrified
- **Radiation:** Inflicts Radiation Burn
- **Shadow:** Inflicts Shadow Gas
- **Cosmic:** Inflicts -Armor debuff

## Special Attacks
Rapiers, like most one-handed weapon types, cannot generate with any special attacks.
