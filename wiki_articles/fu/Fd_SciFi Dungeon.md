# [FU] Fd:SciFi Dungeon

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fd:SciFi_Dungeon

---

Sci Fi dungeon is a [cut Chucklefish surface dungeon](http://starbounder.org/Scifi_Dungeon) that is restored for Frackin Universe use.

Its layouts are relatively the same, however its purpose is different: rather than being a stargazer base, it instead plays host to Miniknog scientists and soldiers who wield Frackin weapons and armors. It sports one Apex Helghast miniboss where the matter dispenser used to be.

Unlike the original, the gravitational pull is *not* weakened. Also, the Sci-Fi Dungeon does *not* have block-protection of any kind.
