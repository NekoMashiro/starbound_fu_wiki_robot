# [FU] Fd:Science Outpost mission quests

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fd:Science_Outpost_mission_quests

---

Certain NPCs will ask you to find specific objects. The objects are found at the end of missions. These missions are found, by chance, from a map as you are exploring. The maps can be located on space stations or on planets. Look for maps with a yellow exclamation point above it.

Lucky Coin is a quest you get at the science outpost. It is found in the mission Takeshi's Castle. The princes gives it to you as a reward.

Ancient Remote is requested by the penumbra merchant. It is found at the end of Brine Star

Crystaline Trophy is requested by the Eld'uukhar merchant. It is found at the Ancient Temple

Alien Broadcast Node is found at the Evernight Jungle

Greenfinger Medal is requested by the floran merchant. It is found in the Verdant Ruins.

The missing Friend mission asks you to find Wagner's ID card, this mission is unlocked by finding a secret area in Baron's Keep mission.
