**Semi-Automatic Pistol **is a one-handed procedurally generated ranged weapon model.

It can be common, uncommon, and rare. Common Semi-Automatic Pistols will shoot bullets, while uncommon and rare ones will shoot elemental plasma shots.

Note: though Pistols (re-named to Semi-Automatic Pistols in Frackin' Universe) are in vanilla *Starbound*, their characteristics are modified significantly in Frackin' Universe. For the original characteristics, consult the official *Starbound* wiki, Starbounder (http://starbounder.org/).

## Base Statistics
- **Base DPS: **5.5-6.0 health/s (Common), 6.0-6.5 health/s (Uncommon/Rare)
- **Fire Time: **0.25-0.75 seconds between shots (~1.3-4.0 shots/s)
- **Energy Usage: **16.5-18.0 energy/s (Common), 18.0-19.5 energy/s (Uncommon/Rare)
- **Knockback: **4-8 (very weak to weak knockback)
- **Crit Chance: **3%
- **Base Crit Damage Bonus: **+1 damage (Common), +2 damage (Uncommon), +3 damage (Rare)

## Elemental Damage
Uncommon and rare Semi-Automatic Pistols are assigned one of seven elements (non-physical damage types) on generation. For all elements, only rare Semi-Automatic Pistols will inflict the appropriate elemental status effect in addition to dealing elemental damage.
- **Fire:** Inflicts Burning
- **Ice:** Inflicts Frost
- **Poison:** Inflicts Poisoned
- **Electric:** Inflicts Electrified
- **Radiation:** Inflicts Radiation Burn
- **Shadow:** Inflicts Shadow Gas
- **Cosmic:** Inflicts -Armor debuff

## Special Attacks
Semi-Automatic Pistols, like most one-handed weapon types, cannot generate with special attacks.
