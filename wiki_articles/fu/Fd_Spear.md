**Spear **is a two-handed procedurally generated melee weapon model. It can be common, uncommon, and rare.

Note: though Spears are part of vanilla *Starbound*, their characteristics are modified by Frackin' Universe. For the unmodified characteristics, consult the official *Starbound* wiki, Starbounder (http://starbounder.org/).

## Base Statistics
- **Base DPS: **9.0-10.0 health/s (Common), 9.5-10.5 health/s (Uncommon/Rare)
- **Held-Attack Multiplier: **x0.1 damage
- **Fire Time: **0.83-1.11 seconds between 1x-damage attacks (~0.9-1.2 1x-damage attacks/s)
- **Knockback: **25-35 (strong knockback)
- **Crit Chance: **4%
- **Base Crit Damage Bonus: **+5 damage (Common), +7 damage (Uncommon), +9 damage (Rare)

## Elemental Damage
Uncommon and rare Spears are assigned one of seven elements (non-physical damage types) on generation. Both uncommon and rare variants will inflict status effects with special attacks. For all elements, only rare Spears will inflict the appropriate elemental status effect with the primary attack.
- **Fire:** Inflicts Burning
- **Ice:** Inflicts Frost
- **Poison:** Inflicts Poisoned
- **Electric:** Inflicts Electrified
- **Radiation:** Inflicts Radiation Burn
- **Shadow:** Inflicts Shadow Gas
- **Cosmic:** Inflicts -Armor debuff
