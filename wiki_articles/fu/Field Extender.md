# [FU] Field Extender

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Field_Extender

---

**Field Extender** helps to connect Field Generator and Field Marker if they are very far away. 

## Appearance
Once placed in the world, the extender can be interacted with. It supports 3 modes Field Generator does, however it doesn't mimic appearance of wired Field Generator and have to be toggled manually.
