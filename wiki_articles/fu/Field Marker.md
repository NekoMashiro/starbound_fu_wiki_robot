# [FU] Field Marker

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Field_Marker

---

**Field Marker** is used with Field Generator to select "which area of the map to protect". 

## Appearance
Once placed in the world, the marker can be interacted with. It supports 3 modes Field Generator does, however it doesn't mimic appearance of wired Field Generator and have to be toggled manually.
