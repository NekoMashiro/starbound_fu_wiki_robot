# [FU] Fish foods

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Food by diet type | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Fish_foods

---

Fish (whether it is cooked or not) can be eaten by Omnivores and Carnivores. Even if the fish was raw, Carnivores don't receive a buff that they would receive from raw meat.

 Herbivores are poisoned by eating these foods.
