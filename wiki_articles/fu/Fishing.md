# [FU] Fishing

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Mechanics | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Fishing

---

**Fishing** is vanilla mechanic (see https://starbounder.org/Fishing). FU doesn't change much about fishing, aside from slightly increased loot (when fish is caught) and ability to catch fish in ocean-like biomes of FU, such as Tidewater or Nitrogen Sea.
