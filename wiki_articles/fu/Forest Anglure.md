# [FU] Forest Anglure

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Forest_Anglure

---

The **Forest Anglure** is a medium sized creature akin to it's relative the [Anglure](https://starbounder.org/Anglure). Adapted to environments of deep forestry and thick undergrowth, this creature also hunts through deception using it's bio-luminescent stalk atop its head to lure in prey. The Forest Anglure has developed a green hide to help it blend in with it's environment. More-so, the Forest Anglure has also developed a tail that assists it in balancing and climbing the scatter terrain and fallen trees of it's home. There are not many lights in the areas these creatures live, and so the presence of their bio-luminescence attracts all sorts of creatures to them - easy hunting. Be wary of a glowing light in the middle of a dead of night for it might just be your end rather than your salvation.

The Forest Anglure attracts prey by moving in a slow and methodical fashion across terrain, pulsing it's bio-luminescent angler to attract interest. When close enough to prey, the Forest Anglure will fire a glob of exploding toxic slime, coating an area in sticky mucus to slow prey while it closes in sharp teeth bared. While not the most dangerous of attacks, it is when you are attacked at night, and at your most desperate when these creatures tend to strike. This is what makes an Anglure so deadly.
