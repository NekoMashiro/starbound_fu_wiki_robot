# [FU] Forest of Fae: Secrets

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Forest_of_Fae:_Secrets

---

There's only one secret in the Forest of Fae. To find it, you will need to climb the tree in the starting area. You will find a switch hidden in the branches.

After activating this switch, make your way further in the dungeon. At the first crossroad, cross the small water pond that turn into poison midway through it. You will find the chest in the ground to the right of the pond.
