# [FU] Forest

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Forest

---

**Forest** planets are tier 2 vanilla planets. They are green, happy and devoid of any environmental hazards. They are similar to Garden in that regard.

You always have a Forest planet in your starting system. It's a good source of early Tungsten Ore (which you need for your first FTL Drive).
