# [FU] Frostlit

The **Frostlit** is an icy blue variant of the [Peblit](https://starbounder.org/Peblit). A more mysterious, and aggressive variation of the creature, the Frostlit seeks to confront unsuspecting creatures within it's domain. Finely polished, the Frostlit's body causes little friction on the surfaces it traverses. As such, when the creature needs to escape a threat, pursue prey, or simply move across long distances, the Frostlit will spin itself rapidly and launch in the desired direction with surprising speed and force. Created with many of the same frozen elements residing on this creatures home, the body of a Frostlit is highly unstable to warm temperatures, and unable to preform physical activities for extended or prolonged duration. 

## Behavior
The Frostlit is capable of creating biting cold clouds in which any creature without proper protection from the elements will find themselves frozen and slowed. Spinning quickly at targets, the Frostlit uses it's own rock hard body as a weapon. When launching itself at another creature, the Frostlit leaves behind a trail of super-chilled air. This is likely the result of the forced reactions happening within the creatures body being suddenly expelled. 

Its cold vapour attacks can still damage its foes even while it is retracted in its shell. Melee users should put some distance while waiting for their chance to strike when it is doing so. Its vapour attacks also deal rapid ticks when being blocked.
