# [FU] Gas Giant

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gas_Giant

---

**Gas Giant** planets are seas of liquid gases, containing several floating islands. Air is only present in the upper layer of the planet. Just below rests a sea of random liquid gas. The core of a gas ball is usually a sea of a random liquid, anything from Lava to Healing Water can be found there. The core may or may not be minable.

They are the only places where one can acquire Metallic Hydrogen outside of a lab, and in large quantities.

**Do not attempt to explore until properly equipped!**

## Exploration
Although, with the exception of its Surface layer, the Gas Giant has no dangerous creatures, a gas ball is quite dangerous to navigate on:

- Firstly, it applies the **Extreme Pressure debuff**, which requires some mid to late-game armor to resist (such as Star-Killer, or Spacefarer mk2, or Valkyrie, or Phase armor, search "Pressure" on the page List of armor sets for the full list).
- Then, it applies the **Hunted debuff**, which spawns in ghosts that operate similarly to the Erchius Ghosts found on moons, but are capable of draining both health and energy when in range (50 blocks from the player).
- Then, as its only solid ground are medium sized **floating islands** (similar to [vanilla asteroids](http://starbounder.org/Asteroid_Field)), you should bring a way to fly around safely to avoid falling to the extremely hard core of the gas planet. Such as one of FU vehicles, a Spear with the Rocket Spear ability (such as Flightfire or Irradium Spear), or a Staff with the Repulsion Zone ability. A grappling hook can be useful for the extremely high gravity. The Gravity Wand will work wonders here. An Escape Teleporter is almost certainly a must-have if you are playing on survival or hardcore. Consumables that grant Water Walking (such as Blue Melon) allow you to stand on gas. Consumables that negate fall damage (such as Ighant Bulb or Xax Wing) are useful here.
- Some of the gas layers have surprising (and fatal) qualities. For example, Metallic Hydrogen significantly increases your gravity (and fall damage) such that Rocket Spear abilities will no longer provide much flight. This will pose an issue if you do not have another means of escaping the layer/planet. Double jump techs (such as Quad Jump) don't work in most gases.
- # You don't necessarily need Gas immunity (which negates these debuffs), because gases (except Caliginous Gas and gaseous Bio-Ooze) don't deal damage over time. Nevertheless, there are armors like Leviathan that provide such an immunity.
- # Layer of Bio-Ooze won't damage you if you have Gas Immunity, or Poison Immunity, or 45% Poison Resistance.
- # Damaging and energy-draining Shadow Gas is not countered by "immunity to Gas" and might require Shadow resistance, or Shadow Barrier, or immunity-granting consumables.
- # If you are exploring Gas Giant by swimming in gases (which works in layers of Hydrogen, etc.), note that it's not possible to swim in Helium-3 (when you enter this layer, you drop like a rock).
- Monsters are usually not a concern on Gas Giants. The biggest and scariest of them (Gas Bag and Sky Whale) are not hostile at all: they won't pursue you unless you damage them first. Beware that passive Gas Bag still deals huge contact damage (while Sky Whale is 100% safe). If you attack them, they are incredibly strong. Gas Bag is capable of destroying blocks and violently self-destructs when defeated.

If you are ready, though, it is a true treasure hoard of infinite gases, some of which are not found anywhere else!

## Resources
> Minerals :These planets contain small patches of variety of resources: Trianglium Ore, Irradium Ore, Telebrium Crystal, Densinium Ore, Prism Shard, Solarium Ore, Crystal Erchius Fuel, Effigium Ore
> Gases : Gases/liquids are also found in plentiful amounts, as there are usually 3 to 4 layers of different randomly generated layers of gases, such as: Helium-3, Caliginous Gas, Bio-Ooze, Metallic Hydrogen, Nitrogen, Hydrogen.
> Core: The core might have an infinite ocean of Lava, or Healing Water, or (very rarely) Liquid Erchius Fuel.

## Terraforming
It's possible to terraform the Gas Giants into another planet type (at least 50% of the planet must be converted to change its type).

This won't add any solid blocks, and it won't remove the gases, but it will remove Pressure and the ghost.

## Variants
Gas Giants have 5 variants: normal (see below), Proto Giant (with additional Proto-Poison hazard), Flame Giant (with Extreme Heat), Ice Giant (with Extreme Cold) and Toxic Giant (with Mercury Poison). They are very similar (and the navigation console will show them all as "Gas Giant"), but their floating rocks are composed of different blocks.
