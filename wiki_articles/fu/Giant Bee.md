# [FU] Giant Bee

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Giant_Bee

---

**Giant Bee** is one of many types of flying insectoids, that make up this category of creature, this one being uncommon. This variety is noted by it's yellow body and black stripes similar to a wasp, if that wasp was the size of a small dog.
> ***Warning:** this is not a farmable bee, and it can't be caught with a Bug Net.*

When it charges, the bee swoops at a target surprisingly fast, striking for overall small damage, but leaving the target with a unique debuff called "Sting". This debuff inflicts radiation damage overtime similar to poison, and causes the player to glow brightly for a few seconds.

## Luna's Lab Notes
~ Akin to many types of bees in real life, this monster will not pursue a player into liquids like other creatures might. In fact, this monster cannot see through liquids and so the moment a player submerges themselves, the bee(s) will eventually lose interest.

~ This monster has a much higher range of tracking when compared to other creatures (a range of 50 compared to the average of 20-30). This meaning that the Giant Bee will pursue players for longer, and from further away.
