# [FU] Giant Worm

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Giant_Worm

---

Sends forth a worm in a boomerang-like trajectory. In addition to regular damage, foes appear to be poisoned. Note that the worm is incredibly slow on returning, and combined with the low knockback value, makes this more of a novelty weapon.
