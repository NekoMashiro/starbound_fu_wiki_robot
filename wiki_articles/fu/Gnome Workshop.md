# [FU] Gnome Workshop

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gnome_Workshop

---

**Gnome Workshop** is an early-game version of Manufacturing Station. It crafts items very slowly (6 seconds + square root of usual crafting time of item) and is not refrigerated, but it doesn't require Power.
