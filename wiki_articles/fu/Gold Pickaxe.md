# [FU] Gold Pickaxe

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gold_Pickaxe

---

**Gold Pickaxe** is a Pickaxe. It deals 2.5 tile damage (per hit) to 3x3 area. It has 800 durability. 

It can be crafted at tier 1 and is extremely practical to use when exploring the depths of the starting Garden planet. Later in the game it becomes outclassed by a much more durable Carbon Pickaxe.
