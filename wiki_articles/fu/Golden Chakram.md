# [FU] Golden Chakram

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Golden_Chakram

---

This weapon will return immediately if it hits any target or solid block. Otherwise, it will fly to the location clicked (if within range) or out to its maximum range in that direction, hover in place for a split second, and then return.
