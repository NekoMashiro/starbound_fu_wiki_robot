# [FU] Gorgoraptor

---

The **Gorgoraptor** is one of the many raptor types that inhabit Primeval Forests. With greenish copper red hide and two hooked horns atop it's dense head, this raptor has adapted well to hunting in dense forest and jungle regions. Unlike it's less evolved kin, the Gorgoraptor, this creature possesses both

running speed and long distance pounces. The Gorgoraptor is the apex of raptor kind, and shows it with it's rash personality. Less patient and perceptive, the Gorgoraptor deftly weaves through environmental hazards as it closes the distance to it's prey. When close, the Gorgoraptor will kick off of the ground, trees, or other environmental obstacles, propelling itself at high speeds toward it's prey. By the time you notice this creature deftly weaving through the jungle toward you, you likely won't have enough time to escape before it's upon you.

This creature is capable of a moderate close range dash, able to close distance to most prey. Gorgoraptors are persistent hunters, choosing to pursue prey no matter the size, and not giving up easily.

All raptors are found on any form of Primeval Forest planets.
