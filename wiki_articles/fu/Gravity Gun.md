# [FU] Gravity Gun

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gravity_Gun

---

The **Gravity Gun** fires a piercing flamethrower-like burst that inflicts tremendous knockback upon enemies. It is very effective at damaging and pushing back Burrowers, even the dreaded Doomwyrm variant. Against most other enemies, it is fairly weak, as the damage stats suggest.

Alt ability creates a small gravity vortex similar to that of a Neutron Bomb, but it pushes the enemies away (instead of dragging them into the center, as a Neutron Bomb would do). This attack will break most blocks easily after a few seconds. The initial hit deals 3x the damage as the primary fire, but enemies will not take much damage from the vortex even if they continue to touch it.

## Other uses
- This gun may be useful on the Moon, because its Alt ability can mine weak blocks (like Moondust), approximately 100 blocks per shot, and it will push Erchius Ghost with such force that it will fly offscreen.
