# [FU] Gravity Normalizer

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Gravity_Normalizer

---

**Gravity Normalizer** increases/decreases gravity to be closer to 80 (which is the gravity of the starting Garden planet). It will select the new gravity automatically (player can't configure it). It doesn't require Power (its wiring is an on/off switch, it can be connected to Iron Lever, etc.).

It doesn't work on Asteroid fields (and in other zero-gravity environments). You need Field Generator to add gravity to them.

Area with modified gravity has a radius of 30 blocks and is centered around the Gravity Normalizer.

## See also
- Field Generator - more advanced, can apply gravity to large areas, works in space, allows player to choose the gravity level.
