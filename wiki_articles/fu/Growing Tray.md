# [FU] Growing Tray

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Growing_Tray

---

**Growing Tray** is an early-game machine for growing/harvesting plants. 

This tray consumes 3 Water per harvest and doesn't require power.

Later in the game it can be replaced by Heatlamp Tray and Hydro Tray, which can be powered to increase the growing rate of plants.
> Another improved tray is Community Garden (which can be fast even without power). However, you would need a town with tenants for it to be useful, and only one Community Garden can exist in each town.
