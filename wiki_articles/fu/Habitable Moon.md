# [FU] Habitable Moon

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Habitable_Moon

---

**Habitable Moon** is a class of moon, instances of which are found in the habitable orbital region of most stars. It includes moons of type Crystalline, Strange Sea, Eden, Desert Moon, Volcanic Moon, Toxic Moon, Rocky Moon, Forest, Cyber Sphere, Mountainous, Tidewater, Fungal, Moon, Irradiated, Bog, Primeval, Volcanic Primeval, Atropus, Red Desert, Proto World, Savannah, Jungle, Alien, Wasteland, Tropical Ocean, Toxic Moon, Atropus Sea, Gelatinous, Rainforest, and Tabula Rasa.
