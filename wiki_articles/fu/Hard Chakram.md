# [FU] Hard Chakram

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Weapons with Extra Properties | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hard_Chakram

---

This weapon pierces targets, though this is difficult to take advantage of due to its very short range of 6 tiles. It will attempt to fly to its maximum range in the direction clicked, returning immediately upon either hitting a solid block or reaching maximum range.
