# [FU] Harvester Beamgun

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Harvester_Beamgun

---

**Harvester Beamgun** fires a 5 blocks long beam that instantly harvests any plant it hits (including trees). It will attempt (not always successfully) to automatically replant Saplings and seeds of non-perennial plants.

Right-clicking on tillable soil blocks (such as Dirt) makes them tilled, as if they were hit with a Hoe or Hand Hoe.
