# [FU] Health

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Mechanics | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Health

---

**Health** is your overall well-being; by maintaining your health your character stays alive. If your health ever drops to, or below zero, your character dies.

Most races start with 100 HP (Health Points), however differences in race, equipment worn, and augments attached can affect the maximum amount of health you have.

## Restoring health
You are able to restore lost health in a variety of ways; from Salves to Bandages, to even just eating well enough, these are but a few ways you can restore your health either gradually or instantly. Below is a list of objects that can restore/heal you.

- Salve: Restores 50 HP over time.
- Medical Kit: Restores 100 HP over 10 seconds. (Also clears most status effects.)
- : See also: Medical Kit II (125 HP), Medical Kit III (150 HP), Medical Kit IV (200 HP), Perfect Medical Kit (250 HP).
- Bandage: Restores HP instantly. (Cooldown before you can use another.)
- Full Belly: Eat enough to fill your Hunger meter, and you will gain limited regeneration until the Hunger meter starts dropping again. (Survival and Hardcore Mode only.)
- Healing Water: Immersing yourself in Healing Water will recover your HP slowly.
- Organic Soup: Immersing yourself in Organic Soup will randomly restore your HP (and hunger).
- Healing plants: many plants in the game (e.g. Miracle Grass Root) have healing properties.

## Increasing maximum health
- Races: Certain races have a larger HP bonus than others. Further, certain environments they travel in can also boost HP.
- Armor: Equipping higher tier armor, or even acquiring an entire armor set can increase overall HP.
- EPP/Augments: Certain EPPs and Augments can increase your overall HP.
