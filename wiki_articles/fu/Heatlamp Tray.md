# [FU] Heatlamp Tray

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Heatlamp_Tray

---

**Heatlamp Tray** is a machine for growing/harvesting plants. 

It consumes 4 Water per harvest (less advanced Growing Tray needed 3 Water). If you wire the Heatlamp Tray to 1W of power, it will grow plants 3 times as fast as it would without power. If you leave it unpowered, plants will grow at the same rate as in Growing Tray.

Later in the game it can be replaced with a more powerful Hydro Tray, which grows plants 2 times faster and consumes 2 times less Water.
