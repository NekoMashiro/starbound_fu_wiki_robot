# [FU] Helium-3

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Helium-3

---

**Helium-3** is a non-toxic gas. Touching it increases your jump height for a short time. It can be found on some Gas Giant planets in infinite quantities.

**Warning**: you can't swim in Helium-3 (unlike in many other gases). If you are exploring the Gas Giants by swimming, then entering a layer of Helium-3 will cause you to rapidly fall down. This can be very deadly (if you don't have anything that grants fall damage immunity - Parasol, Ighant Bulb, Oddball Sphere tech, etc.)
