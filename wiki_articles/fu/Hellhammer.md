# [FU] Hellhammer

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hellhammer

---

**Hellhammer** is the strongest Pickaxe in the game. It deals 6 tile damage (per hit) to 4x4 area. It has 8000 durability. 

Despite its impressive stats, this pickaxe is somewhat outclassed for its tier, because it requires Pyreite Bar, and Morphite Manipulator (which deals 12 tile damage) can be obtained earlier.
