# [FU] Hobo Contract

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hobo_Contract

---

Gifted by Kevin after completing one of his quests.

This crew member inflicts Intoxication debuff (blurry vision) every time you beam down. You can stop this by firing this crew member.

Medical buff Immunization (from medical space stations) gives complete immunity to this debuff.
