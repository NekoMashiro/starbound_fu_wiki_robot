# [FU] Honey Extractor

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Honey_Extractor

---

**Honey Extractor** converts honeycombs (such as Forest Honeycomb) into consumable jars (such as Shell Honey Jar). It takes 2 seconds to produce 1 jar. It doesn't require Power.

More advanced version of this building is Honey Jarring Machine. It produces exactly the same, but works 2 times faster.
