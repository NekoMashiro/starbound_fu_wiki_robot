# [FU] Hopper

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hopper

---

**Hopper** is a unique bipedal, over-sized rabbit made of gelatinous, rubbery flesh. The creature sings and giggles in a high pitch at all times. When attacked, the *Hopper *hides underneath the ground for a few seconds and is immune for this time. Overly enthusiastic about hugging, this creature will launch itself at the nearest sight of another humanoid. 

The Hopper possesses some quirky abilities, mostly it's ability to hop and jump at high speeds, dealing contact damage from the force of hurling itself at a creature. Additionally, the Hopper is capable of blowing a small bubble at a target, dealing smol amounts of damage upon contact. Lastly, the Hopper will periodically spawn areas of energy regeneration fields. This is useful, as standing within the field will rapidly regenerate ANY targets energy - meaning you can regain lost or spent energy from combat quite quickly within this field. As such, these creatures make great pets to snipers, and others who rely heavily on energy stores. If one can get passed the incessant whistling that is.
