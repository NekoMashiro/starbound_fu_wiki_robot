# [FU] Hunter Pteropod

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Hunter_Pteropod

---

The Hunter Pteropod is a small flying creature akin to it’s relative the [Pteropod](https://starbounder.org/Pteropod). Adapted to unforgiving environments, this creature stores precious and volatile liquids in the swinging sack beneath it’s torso. While it’s kin the Pteropod stores digestive juices and poisonous concoctions in its pouch, the Hunter Pteropod has adapted even more complex and viscous biological evolution. This creature contains boiling plasma inside of it's tiny sac. Firing from afar, the Hunter Pteropod zigzags and fires exploding plasma bolts in burst fire, with enough force and explosive results to damage blocks.

This creature is capable of quick flight, and combined with it’s small size, it can be a challenge to hit at range. More aggressive and quick than the common Pteropod, this creature is most likely to fire barrages of superheated explosive plasma toward prey. The Hunter Pteropod is also known for not giving up a chase once it has it's eyes set on a target.
