# [FU] ITD Currency Handler

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/ITD_Currency_Handler

---

**ITD Currency Handler** can be placed into Filter slot of Item Transference Device (it requires "Type" filter). This will instruct the ITD to move pseudo-items like Pixels, Madness, Ancient Essence, Research, Genes, etc.
