# [FU] ITD Unhandled Handler

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/ITD_Unhandled_Handler

---

Warning: you don't need this item in 99,9% of situations.

**ITD Unhandled Handler** can be placed into Filter slot of Item Transference Device to select "items that are not supported by item transference network". In practice, though, only **weirdly implemented items from other mods** will match this filter. If you want to transfer FU items or vanilla items, this filter won't match anything.
