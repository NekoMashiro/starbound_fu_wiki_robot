# [FU] Industrial Centrifuge

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Industrial_Centrifuge

---

**Industrial Centrifuge** processes 1 liquid every 0.2 seconds. It is faster than any other centrifuge, but its conversion rates are not that great. **See the page Any Centrifuge for the list of recipes.**

## Strategy
This centrifuge is more practical when the input liquid is infinite (e.g. Lava from a core ocean), because in this situation conversion rates (which are a weakness of Industrial Centrifuge) are not particularly important (you can just process more Lava to compensate).

If the input liquid is limited, precious or slow to obtain, you might want to use some other centrifuge (with better conversion rate).
