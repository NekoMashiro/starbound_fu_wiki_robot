# [FU] Infernus

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Infernus

---

**Infernus** planets - redder, magma-y-er , and burnier. They are hot and deadly. The atmosphere itself tries to kill you, it rains lava and fire AND there are strange creatures wandering around just waiting to murder you. Without good protection (and some sort of hazard suit or immunizing plant) you won't last long on such world. They are an excellent source of many minerals, Cinnabar being chief among them.

Infernus worlds (and their variant Dark Infernus) are the sole source of Pyreite Ore.

## Exploration
- This world applies the "Extreme Heat" debuff, which requires an EPP or armor with heat resistance/immunity to make exploring possible without dying quickly
- There are many monsters that can deal serious damage and even [burn](https://starbounder.org/Burning) or [melt](https://starbounder.org/Melting) you if you do not have sufficient protection
- Above ground, there are many harmful weather effects that can deal severe or even fatal damage and apply many debuffs which can be a pain to counter
- This world lacks a breathable atmosphere, requiring the use of a breathing EPP or armor with Oxygen/Breath immunity.
