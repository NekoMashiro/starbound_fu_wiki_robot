# [FU] Iron Centrifuge

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Iron_Centrifuge

---

**Iron Centrifuge** processes 1 liquid every 0.85 seconds. **See the page Any Centrifuge for the list of recipes.**

## Troubleshooting
- If you are using Alternator Generator (produces 4W) to power Iron Centrifuge (uses 4W), and it doesn't work, add a battery (such as Simple Battery). Having a battery evens out any irregularities in power distribution.
