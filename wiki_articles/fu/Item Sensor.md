# [FU] Item Sensor

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Item_Sensor

---

**Item Sensor** is a placeable object with 1 wiring output. It sends ON signal if there is a nearby item that is lying on the ground (e.g. was just dropped by a defeated monster).

## See also
- Grabber - can take the items from the ground.
