# [FU] Jallaraptor

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jallaraptor

---

The Jallaraptor is one of the many raptor types that inhabit Primeval Forests. Hunting rocky cliffs and mountainous outlands, these raptors are built larger and tougher than other adult raptors. With thickly reinforced skulls, the Jallaraptor charges targets at high speeds, using it's head like a ram with enough force to shatter large boulders. Upon impact with a wall, the Jallaraptor will be stunned for a brief period of time, reeling and dazed from the forceful impact. Decent impact damage, this creature can become trouble in a flat environment. However, this creature doesn't possess very good climbing skills, and is slowed significantly in hilly terrain. Interestingly enough, the Jallaraptor is the only raptor type to care for not only it's own young, but also the young of other raptor species. Looking after, and caring for the offspring of other raptor species, and fiercely attacking adults on sight.

All raptors are found on any form of Primeval Forest planets.

## Luna's Lab Notes
~ The Jallaraptor is a monster found in the game *[Keroro RPG: Kishi to Musha to Densetsu no Kaizoku](https://www.spriters-resource.com/ds_dsi/kerorpg/).* (Ripped by Maverick-PK) [enemy 24]

~ This creature does not do well in environments with many obsticles, or tight spaces. When charging, if even the tail of this raptor is in contact with a block, it is sent into it's stunned animation as if colliding with a wall. This can happen when it's large hitbox of a tail touches a slightly raised piece of terrain, closely walled room, etc.
