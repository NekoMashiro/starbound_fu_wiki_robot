# [FU] Jungle

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Jungle

---

**Jungle** planets are tier 4 vanilla planets. They are radioactive and uncomfortably humid.

## Tropical Humidity
Tropical Humidity hazard deals very little damage (even if you aren't immune to it, it's possible to ignore it). Its main impact is reduced jump height and movement speed.
