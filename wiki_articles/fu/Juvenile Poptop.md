# [FU] Juvenile Poptop

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Juvenile_Poptop

---

**Juvenile Poptops** are a younger variation of the adult potops. This monster is a weaker version of the Adult Poptop that players encounter similar biomes. They fight by charging towards players and NPCs. Having a higher base health than most monsters. The player should be well equipped to fight it. The juvenile poptop is smaller in size than the adult poptop, boasting smaller arms, range of attack, and health pool. While still growing into it's adult body, the juvenile poptop will voraciously seek out prey to devour, requiring large amounts of food to aid in it's development. The creatures metabolism pushes it to seek targets without sleep while growing.

The Juvenile Poptop is less powerful compared to it's adult counterpart, and less coordinated than it's younger nimble poptop kin. Able to swing it's growing claws only once every few seconds, the power behind these attacks are still something to be wary of. Most fond of using it's small developing legs to get into biting range, dealing contact damage similar to the claw attacks.
