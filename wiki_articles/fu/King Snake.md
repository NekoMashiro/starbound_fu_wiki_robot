# [FU] King Snake

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/King_Snake

---

> *This monster is planned to be removed from the game, it no longer spawns anywhere.*

The **King Snake** is a large gray, and deep forest green snake with an armored skull, and hood, which is primarily used to make the King Snake appear bigger. The snakes good contains loose skin which it can inflate with air from its lungs, expanding the movable ribs. Having grown large in size, a customary trait of creatures found on the dense, dangerous rainforest worlds. The King Snake is capable of holding itself own against many of the smaller hunting predators of these worlds, though to the more dangerous beasts and monsters that roam these mysterious worlds, they are but a small snake waiting to be snatched up and devoured.

Capable of lashing bites that inflict poison damage, this creatures often feared by adventurers as even a glancing nip from the creatures powerful fanged mouth can bring down an adult poptop. Additionally, the King Snake is capable of spitting it's venomous spittle at distant targets with exceptional range, and deadly precision. One should be prepared to neutralize venom when approaching a fight with one of these creatures.

## Luna's Lab Notes
~ This creature's assets are pulled from the game *Keroro RPG: Kishi to Musha to Densetsu no Kaizoku* - which the snake like creature appears as a monster called "Enemy 7". The original sprite-sheet used can be found here: https://www.spriters-resource.com/ds_dsi/kerorpg/sheet/46703/

~ The venomous spit of this creature has a long range, and is highly accurate, making ranged combat just as dangerous as melee.

~ This creature has immunity to pus, and madness gain. A quirky stipulation given that madness is often unused by hostile monsters (the likes of which would be allied with the King Snake regardless).

~ Ironically, this creature is categorized as a "walking" monster type.
