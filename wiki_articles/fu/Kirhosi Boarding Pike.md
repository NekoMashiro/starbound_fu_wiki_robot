# [FU] Kirhosi Boarding Pike

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Kirhosi_Boarding_Pike

---

Contrary to what the description says, this weapon can NOT break blocks. The "Pain Emitter" ability is simply a Rocket Spear ability, albeit strong enough to lift the player in 90 gravity (but not 107 gravity) and lights up the surrounding area briefly.
