# [FU] Large Apiary

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Large_Apiary

---

**Large Apiary** is an improved version of Apiary. It allows you to breed bees. **See the page Bees for details.**

You can place 1 queen bee into the Large Apiary. It also has 2 slots for drones (up to 1000 of them in each slot), 2 slots for frames (up to 64 of them in each slot) and 20 slots for produced materials.
