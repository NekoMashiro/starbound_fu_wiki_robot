# [FU] Large Toxic Fly

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Large_Toxic_Fly

---

The Large Toxic Fly is the result of a Toxic Fly living and growing for decades. Bearing the same appearance of a green body with glowing iridescent blue eyes, with the only difference being a massive bloated body. The Large Toxic Fly has control of other younger and smaller Toxic Flies and uses them the attack.

No longer easy to destroy, the Large Toxic fly can be quite troublesome with its constant spawning of drones, and overall high damage. It spawns 5 little flies at a time. Most of the time, the large fly will hover in midair to spawn and "watch" its smaller flies swarm you, but dont underestimate the bigger fly. When it *does* decide to touch you, it will deal significant damage. 

.**Large Toxic Fly **is a unique monster found on any kind of irradiated planets, and is capturable.

## Luna's Lab Notes
