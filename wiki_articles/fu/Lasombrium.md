# [FU] Lasombrium

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lasombrium

---

**Lasombrium** is a tier 4 ore. Unlike other ores, it doesn't have a specific planet: instead, it appears in various corruption-themed subbiomes, such as Eldritch Forest, Wasteland Mini Biome, Corrupt (biome), Corrupt Asteroids, Corrupted Moon.

A good planet to look for such biomes and therefore, the ore itself, would be a Tidewater, since it often has a corrupt subbiome on it, which is also pretty easily accessible thanks to the large bodies of liquid.

This ore slightly glows in the dark.
