The **Lesser Sentry** (also known as "Precursor Sentry") is an ancient drone-like precursor enemy.

## Fighting
The precursor sentry is in a way, a complete pushover, neither in contact nor it's ranged attacks deal enough damage to consider it a threat, so when in a fight, deal with them last.

It is advised to use electric weaponry against it, as it will deal 30% extra damage.
