# [FU] Lightless Moon

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightless_Moon

---

**Lightless Moon** is a class of moon, instances of which are found around Black Stars. It includes moons of type Shadow Moon, Dark Sulphur, Dark Ice Waste, Dark Primeval, Nightmare Atropus, Shadow, Frozen Moon, Cyber Sphere, and Unknown.
