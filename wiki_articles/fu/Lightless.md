# [FU] Lightless

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Lightless

---

**Lightless Sphere** planets are stark, nigh-impregnably dark and full of danger if you don't bring ample light.

These worlds are the sole source of Effigium Ore. They have a stronger Erchius Ghost, who will follow the players if they mined any Erchius or Effigium Ore.

They contain numerous valuable resources, but are never a bright and cheery place.

## Unbreachable Darkness
Being on the Lightless planet inflicts "Unbreachable Darkness" debuff (you can only see a few blocks away, no matter how many Torches you place). You will need some form of immunity to it, such as Darklight Blossom or Darkness Shield.

## Navigation Console
- These unusual worlds are ancient and something prevents any sun from reaching their surfaces. There may be some useful things there, however. Bring lots of lights!
- This strange lightless sphere reflects all sunlight. Reason unknown. Effigium Ore detected.
- The atmosphere of this lightless sphere blocks any sort of light from penetrating. Scans inconclusive.
