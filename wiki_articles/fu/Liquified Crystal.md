# [FU] Liquified Crystal

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Liquified_Crystal

---

**Liquified Crystal** is a very good **fertilizer** for Trays (equal to Advanced Fertilizer). Note that Trays can't use it as liquid.

It very rarely occurs in nature (in tiny underground lakes), but can be crafted.
