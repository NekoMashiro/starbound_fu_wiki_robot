**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Everything | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/List_of_biome_music

> What is the music that plays on Aether Worlds?
> — Frequently asked question
See below for ALL music tracks for all biomes, dungeons and instances. Use the browser Find tool (Ctrl+F) to find the biome or dungeon by name. If you don't know the name of a biome, you can determine it by its blocks.

- If the music is from FU, there is a link to download it.
- If the music doesn't have a link, then it's a vanilla song. It can probably be found somewhere on YouTube.
- This list doesn't include tracks from [Frackin' Music](https://github.com/sayterdarkwynd/FrackinMusic/tree/master/music) (a separate mod) or from any other mods (only from FU and vanilla).
