# [FU] List of mech weapons

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Mechanics, Items by various parameters | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/List_of_mech_weapons

---

Frackin' Universe adds a large array of new mech weapons to the game (marked by the FU label on the item icons), and changes their recipes too. There are new weapons to play with made from Iron Bar, Aetherium Alloy, and everything in between.

## About this list
Mech weapons do not follow a tier system like weapons the player wields. Because of this, weapons have had their tier on this list determined by what kind of metals are involved in crafting them, and linking the metal to the Armor and Weapons tree. 

For example, if a mech weapon requires Durasteel Bar, it is labelled as tier 4 on the Armor and Weapons tree, and therefore is labelled as tier 4 on this list.

## Additional tips and info
- Some Space enemies are immune to at least Poison and Cosmic damage. It's a good idea to have a second weapon of different elemental type to minimize the risk of an enemy being immune to your damage.

- Offensive drones (Sniper Drone, etc.) do not have smart targeting (they do not shoot where an enemy will be, and instead fire directly at them). In space battles, if you get the enemy to chase you and practice in matching your movement to the enemy movement, these drones will become significantly more effective.

- Mining weapons can be used as offensive weapons. They deal much less damage compared to other weapons, but are still viable.
