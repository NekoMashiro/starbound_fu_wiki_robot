# [FU] List of tenants

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/List_of_tenants

---

These are all tenants from FU and vanilla.
> "Requirements" are colony tags of objects that must be inside the house for Colony Deed to summon this tenant. If you are using Colony Deed Mark II, then all tenants additionally require a bed.
> Numbers in the brackets (e.g. 8 in "cooking (8)") mean that "cooking" objects must occupy at least 8 tiles of the house.
