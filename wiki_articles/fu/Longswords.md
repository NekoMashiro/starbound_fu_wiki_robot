# [FU] Longswords

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Longswords

---

Longsword is a one handed procedurally generated melee weapon model. It functions as a one-handed counterpart to vanilla [broadswords](https://starbounder.org/Broadswords), and as such, function in similar fashion; they cannot be aimed directionally, outside swinging in whatever direction the player is facing. It can be common, uncommon, and rare.

## Base Statistics
- **Base DPS:** 7.5-8.5 health/s (Common), 7.6-8.75 health/s (Uncommon), 7.7-9.0 (Rare)
- **Fire Time:** 0.8-1.0 seconds between 1x-damage attacks (~1.0-1.3 1x-damage attacks/s)
- **Combo Progression:**
1. **First Strike:** 1.0x damage, 15 default knockback
1. **Second Strike:** 0.7x damage, 10 default knockback
1. **Third Strike:** 0.8x damage, 15 default knockback
- **Crit Chance:** 1%
- **Base Crit Damage Bonus:** +5 damage

## Elemental Damage
Uncommon and rare longswords are assigned one of seven elements (non-physical damage types) on generation. Neither uncommon nor rare longswords inflict elemental status effects.

- Fire: Inflicts [Burning](https://starbounder.org/Burning)
- Ice: Inflicts [Frost](https://starbounder.org/Frost)
- Poison: Inflicts [Poisoned](https://starbounder.org/Poisoned)
- Electric: Inflicts [Electrified](https://starbounder.org/Electrified)
- Radiation: Inflicts Radiation Burn
- Shadow: Inflicts Shadow Gas
- Cosmic: Inflicts Armor

## Special Attacks
Longswords, like most one-handed weapon types, cannot generate with any special attacks.
