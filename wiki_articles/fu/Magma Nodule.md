# [FU] Magma Nodule

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Magma_Nodule

---

A growable, far deadlier Molotov. Unlike it or the Ignus Chillis, this does not release fire when it hits something. Instead, it generates Lava (unless the area is tile-protected).
