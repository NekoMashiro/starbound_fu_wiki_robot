# [FU] Meatbag

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Meatbag

---

The **Meatbag** has a thin pink fleshy outer layer of skin unlike it's relative bulbops that possess an outer shell, and a tear soaked face. Additionally, the Meatbag has similar behavior as the more common Bulbop, hopping around the terrain and jumping towards the player to deliver a body-blow attack, however the Meatbags movements are more speratic, moving both toward and away from a target without much reason. Additionally, after landing from a jump, a Meatbag will splatter the area with blood, akin to a certain Super Meat Boy.

Lacking in combat, this creature truly lives up to its status as meatbag, and makes a great target for the particularly cruel. However, be warned that even though it's damage is reduced, this creature can still inflict insanity... This likely has no effect on creatures however and as such can be safely ignored. ## Luna's Lab Notes
~ The Meatbag is similar in design to [Super Meat Boy](http://supermeatboy.com/) in the way that its flesh is stripped bare and it makes meaty splashy noises and effects after hopping.. Additionally the tragic tear stricken face reminds me somewhat of [The Binding of Isaac](http://bindingofisaac.com/) (Capture one of these to creep others out or show off to Grimdark fans).

 ~ This creatures animations and programming has left it with some interesting abilities that likely are bugs rather than features. The most prominent is that after moving forward, if the Meatbag moves off a slight incline, it will begin floating up at an angle as it moves - for as long as it continues its movement in the same direction. This means that the creature can stroll on into the sun if it desired just on a whim. This also means that the player may be attacked suddenly from the sky by a fleshy Meatbag plopping on them. The aggro of this creature is sporadic, and the creatures movements give no indication of hostility or targeting the player even after a combat que (such as change of music or the blep sound upon initiating combat). The Meatbag can also see through walls and with enough persistence, float through single block barriers.
