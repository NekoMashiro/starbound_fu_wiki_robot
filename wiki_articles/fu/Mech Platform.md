# [FU] Mech Platform

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mech_Platform

---

**Mech Platform** is a placeable object that (when interacted with) will summon your Mech.

It works on planets, asteroid belts, player space stations, etc.

It usually won't work in SAIL missions (some missions have areas without tile protection, thus allowing you to place a Mech Platform there, but it will usually fail to spawn a mech when used inside the mission).
