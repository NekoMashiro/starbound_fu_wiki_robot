# [FU] Medical Bay

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Medical_Bay

---

**Medical Bay** is a bed that heals 100% of your health over 30 seconds (basic beds like Sleeping Bag need 60 seconds for that, and there are some beds in between). As with any other bed, your hunger meter won't deplete while sleeping in it.
