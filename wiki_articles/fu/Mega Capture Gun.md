# [FU] Mega Capture Gun

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mega_Capture_Gun

---

> *Not to be confused with Capture Gun, which is much weaker.*

**Mega Capture Gun** stuns the monster, reduces its health by percentage and (if the target's health is low enough) captures it, producing a Capture Pod with the monster inside.

It's generally much better than using Capture Pods, except in a very rare case: some high-tier creatures (such as Gas Bag) are immune to Mega Capture Gun (because possibility to stun them would be too overpowered), but are still capturable by a regular Capture Pod.

Monsters marked as non-capturable (those that are completely impossible to capture with a Capture Pod) won't be capturable by the Mega Capture Gun either.

## Advantages over Capture Gun
Unlike much cheaper Capture Gun (which can also capture monsters):
- Mega Capture Gun can't accidentally kill the monster instead of capturing it (because Mega Capture Gun reduces health by a percentage, while a cheap Capture Gun deals flat damage, and that **can** kill the monster).
- Mega Capture Gun is useful for capturing some monsters that require extremely low health (e.g. 1%) to be captured. Ordinarily it would be very difficult to reduce the monster's health to be below this threshold and not kill it. Note that majority of capturable monsters don't need their health to be that low.
- Mega Capture Gun can be placed into Warped Regulator. Have you ever captured 10 Gas Bags at the same moment? This thing has.
