# [FU] Metal Queen

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Metal_Queen

---

The Metal Queen is one of many types of flying insectoids, that make up this category of creature, this one being quite rare! This variety is noted by it's dark steely grey body akin to a metal wasp, if that wasp was the size of a medium sized dog. About thrice as large as a Metal Bee, the Queen is the most impressive specimen of any hive..

Before attacking, the Metal Queen spawns up to five Tiny Metal Bee's to fight for her. These drones are quickly replaced if killed, and can pose quite a threat if not dealt with promptly. When the queen chooses to attack, she swoops at a target surprisingly fast, striking for overall decent damage, and leaving the target with a unique debuff called "Sting". This debuff inflicts radiation damage overtime similar to poison, and causes the player to glow brightly for a few seconds.
