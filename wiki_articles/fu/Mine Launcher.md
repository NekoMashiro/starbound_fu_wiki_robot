# [FU] Mine Launcher

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mine_Launcher

---

Primary fire launches three proximity mines in an arcing pattern that tend to land close, medium, and a long distance away. Mines detonate on contact or after 10 seconds, damaging/destroying blocks. The alt-fire "Mega Mine" consumes all energy to launch a quick-moving mine in a straight trajectory. After 5 seconds it explodes in ~15 tile radius, destroying blocks.
