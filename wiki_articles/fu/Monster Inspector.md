# [FU] Monster Inspector

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Monster_Inspector

---

> *Not to be confused with Monster Detector.*
**Monster Inspector** is a tool that shows Name and Description of a wild monster. It will also tell you if the monster can be captured (in a Capture Pod) or relocated.

Some monsters (such as randomly generated ones) don't have a human-readable name. They will be shown as "Some indescribable horror".
