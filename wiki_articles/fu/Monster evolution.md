# [FU] Monster evolution

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Mechanics | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Monster_evolution

---

**Monster evolution** is when one monster turns into another, e.g. Baby Hen into Hen.

Some monsters can be evolved faster by throwing food at them. For example, Poptop evolves into Juvenile Poptop if given Raw Steak 5 times.

Monsters that require liquids to evolve (such as Magmatop Alpha) need the liquid to be poured on them, not thrown at them as item.
