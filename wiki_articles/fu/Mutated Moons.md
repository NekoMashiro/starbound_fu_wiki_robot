# [FU] Mutated Moons

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mutated_Moons

---

**Mutated Moons** is a class of moon, instances of which are found around Dying Stars. It includes moons of type Desert Moon, Volcanic Moon, Toxic Moon, Rocky Moon, Tar Ball, Fungal, Alien, Wasteland, and Unknown.
