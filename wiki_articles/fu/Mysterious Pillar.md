# [FU] Mysterious Pillar

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Mysterious_Pillar

---

**Mysterious Pillar** was left behind by Precursors for who knows what reasons. Scanning it may reveal some of their secrets!

Appears in Precursor Underground biome (glowing rooms) and in the "Science complex" dungeon (most planets have it as a possible dungeon).
