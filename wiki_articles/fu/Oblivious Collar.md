# [FU] Oblivious Collar

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Oblivious_Collar

---

**Oblivious Collar** will prevent your pet from attacking your enemies. They won't attack your pet either, so this is convenient for non-combat robots like Ore Drone I. Your pet can still be damaged by Lava, harmful weather, etc.

This collar doesn't work inside the Collar Holder (monsters won't ignore you).
