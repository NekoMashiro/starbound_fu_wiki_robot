# [FU] Ocu Cannon

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ocu_Cannon

---

The ocu cannon appears to be a 'cluster'/'mirv' style grenade launcher, firing an eyeball that bounces around and splits into multiple smaller eyeballs. The main eyeball does less damage than if all of the smaller individual grenades hit (requires testing); Like many Bioweapons, it's altfire is a pus/vomit spew- which is essentially a flamethrower with gravity.
