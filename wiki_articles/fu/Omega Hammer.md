# [FU] Omega Hammer

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Omega_Hammer

---

**Omega Hammer ** is a Tier 5 unique weapon. It is randomly found as loot in the Precursor Ruins mission. It will shoot flames out of the back end when held. It has a three hit combo.
1. regular hammer attack but faster
1. stabs forward with some small flames
1. slams ground and causes small explosion
The alt attack will only work if you are in the air and slams the ground. **WARNING: THIS WILL DESTROY BLOCKS.** It will also cause a medium explosion.

## Tips
- Because Alt ability causes you to rapidly move downwards, you can use it to quickly sink to the ocean floor. This may also be useful on Gas Giants.
