# [FU] Omega Laser

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Omega_Laser

---

**WARNING: This weapon uses poison/cosmic damage, which a few space enemies are completely immune to.**

The Omega Laser is a mech arm that charges and fires an instant, piercing laser.

When the player holds down their mouse, the laser will charge for a short period, and then fire an instant beam. It's reload time is fairly short.

The beam can pierce enemies, but not tiles.
