# [FU] One-planet challenge

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Tutorials | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/One-planet_challenge

---

**One-planet challenge** is a popular challenge for experienced FU players who like engineering/automation. You never leave the starting planet!

**Note: This challenge is not recommended for first-time players, and as it is not officially supported some content is inaccessible or very hard to access.**

## Conditions
The goal is to progress (obtain higher-tier resources and reach higher-tier techs) without visiting any planets except your starting Garden planet.

Depending how strict you want the challenge to be, there are some details open to interpretation:
- Visiting missions (such as Verdant Ruins) and other special areas like the Outpost or the Science Outpost may or may not be allowed.
- Trading, on your planet, with your tenants or in special areas, may or may not be allowed, or on a item per item basis and/or depending upon whether the merchant is a tenant or not (notably the tiny variant of deeds requires no outpost interaction),
 - In particular buying ores (e.g. from Geologist, station terminals etc.) is usually not allowed, because it's too easy and cheapens the challenge, but, depending upon how hard the challenge is set, there may be exceptions, such as
 - Buying Zerchesium Ore and Densinium Ore, which are unobtainable otherwise, might be allowed
 - Buying Geodes might or not be allowed

Under these conditions, most things in the game are obtainable.

## Strategy
The Garden planet is tier 1, so it doesn't have tier 2+ ores in the ground. They need to be obtained by other means.

This can be accomplished by using chemistry, plants, liquid interactions, machinery (sifters, crushers, centrifuges, extractors), bees, tenants (including merchant NPCs), etc.

### Early power progression
The early Power Progression naturally offers a relatively fast path to automate extraction of Tungsten and Titanium:
1. on the surface, build Machining Table, a Greenhouse and a Hand Mill, start growing Currentcorn: with the mill extract Sulphuric Acid from its fruit and Silicon from the seeds. While it grows, extract 50 Oxygen from Water and build a Electronics Center. Multiply the acid by mixing with Water (e.g. at the moment most likely in a puddle) and make 4 Silicon Boards from the Silicon you got, use them to make a Copper Quarry, alternatively dig with the Matter Manipulator,
1. * This initial setup requires materials coming from 18 Iron Bars, 14 Copper Bars, 3 Wooden Logs, 20 Dirt, 40 Water, 50 Oxygen, 1 Glass and either 6 Corn Seeds or 4 Living Root to make them at the Greenhouse. Regardless you need 2 Living Roots to research the basic crops in the agriculture tree,
1. * If you do not find Sand for the Glass requirements of the Electronics Center, remember the tutorial quests have a Glass reward, treasure it,
1. Keep digging underground via the help of the Quarry and reach the Garden Core Layer,
1. * there are likely more benefits in arriving fast down with minimum amount of materials than in arriving slowly,
1. * in the core layer you are advised to keep the background core layer Unrefined Conglomerate there, for later usage
1. This act of digging alone should yield enough materials to make at least 1 Hydraulic Dynamo, pumps and an Iron Centrifuge
1. * You do not need at the beginning a pump: if you can manipulate liquids yourself, you can feed manually Lava to the Iron Centrifuge until you have the materials for the pump.
1. * A Hydraulic Dynamo and Iron Centrifuge require in base materials 31 Iron Bars, 19 Copper Bars, 6 Lead, 25 Core Fragment, 1 Wooden Log
1. * If you lack Lead for the Dynamo, but can make an Alternator Generator, you can crush and sift blocks. In particular, if you start the Liquids Acquisition until the point your machines suffice, Sulphuric Dirt is rich in Lead
1. * Optionally, if you have pixels and you are allowed to use your tenants to help you, you can get to Titanium now, but there are routes with no interaction if you continue:
1. ** Make a room near the lava ocean at the bottom with the dynamo, a bed and a light, outside the room wire to it centrifuges to centrifuge lava pumped from the lava ocean,
1. ** Keep spawning a tenant in such room until a [Foundry Merchant](https://starbounder.org/Foundry_Merchant) selling Tungsten and Titanium cheap enough appears, add more hydraulic Dynamos or remove other items from the room until it happens, the room with only Wooden Doors, a Tungsten Bed and a Hydraulic Dynamo is good to to deliver that type of tenant and you can create a room and a tenant like this for each dynamo starting your core layer colony,
1. Pump and centrifuge the lava to obtain iron and tungsten ore in unlimited quantities,
1. * you should have now the means to get a Simple Battery, more dynamos, an Industrial Centrifuge. Glass
1. Liquify the iron, either via extraction or at a Chemistry Lab,
1. All the background blocks that are left in the core are on average Unrefined Conglomerate, if you pour the previously made Liquid Iron on them, they will turn to Volcanic Ash in great quantities with a little amount of liquid (unlike in the mixer), which can be mined by the same quarries that mined the foreground.
1. Volcanic Ash can then be:
1. * sifted to obtain Diamonds or
1. * mixed with Dark Water and crushed for a small initial amount of Titanium Ore and Penumbrite Ore and Iodine,
1. Such initial supply should allow you to purchase enough Teleporter Cores from the outpost (15 Diamonds each) and craft enough Liquid Mixers (you need at least 1) to keep advancing in the tech tree, automate resource transformation effectively (ITDs) and create a Colony Core to start a proper colony. The colony buildings create over time more items, not just from Lava and Water!
1. Now that you have a mixer you should find no problem in continuing the Liquids Acquisition and get Liquid Irradium, Blue Grav-Liquid, Liquid Protocite. In particular now:
1. * you can craft Protocite and some Irradium Ore technologies
1. * you can craft Quietus Ore by creating and crushing Blood Crystals
1. * You can create Aether Dust from Blue Grav-Liquid and Liquid Irradium:
1. ** Sifting it is a way better method to get Titanium Ore and Penumbrite Ore. On paper, it has a slightly worse rate of Penumbrite Ore, but on one planet Dark Water is rare, even now that you have Lab Centrifuge available
1. ** it can be mixed with Lava for Volcanic Ash and more Diamonds, if you are exhausting the biome
1. Tier 3 misses now Zerchesium Ore to then build Advanced Alloy related technologies, in particular the Blast Furnace. Its acquirement strategy depends heavily upon the conditions set on the challenge:
1. * If trading at the Science Outpost is allowed, the easiest method is buying Food Rations at Lorewalker's Shop and selling them for Zerchesium Ore at Silenes' Minerals.
1. * If it is not allowed you need a mission where Zerchesium is in the rewards.

From this point onwards, the tier progression is more dependent on what is available from random sources (like quests or the particular biomes) and is not necessarily linked to the power progression or your command of further liquids. 

### Tenants and quests
Once automated power generation is secured, a large colony near you offers many advantages:
- With a Recycling Center and a Sewage Storage there might be enough waste that farming there radioactive materials to power production might become reasonable,
- A Community Garden will greatly speed up your agriculture research due to the large yield multiplers it carries,
- More tenants there are, more missions more regularly are offered, some of which have loot otherwise difficult to obtain. For instance, a Solstew delivery mission will gift the player Solusberry Seeds to unlock Solarium Stars otherwise unobtainable, while a mission to kill Wretched Rats might result in the rats dropping Essentia Obscura, even before a liquid mixer is secured!
- At the beginning you might want to create colony deeds even if you cannot create Colony Cores. The core will convert the rent to pixels, but at the beginning you might prefer the rent in kind of the vanilla mechanic. For example, it can give you a useful seed. By the time you have reached Tungsten generation, these rewards are not very interesting, while the item generation from the pooled buildings (e.g. Recycling Center or Blood Drive) is, hence it becomes time to shift to Colony Cores.

### Research points
Because the starting planet is tier 1, the passive gain of Research is very low on it. You would want to counter this: produce some pricey materials/crops and throw them into the Computers. Have many computers and automate the computer interactions! Wearing the whole set of Researcher's Breastplate is easily obtainable when Carbon is mastered, with such armor the planet is not too dangerous but the benefit in Research is enormous!!

### Terraforming
At tier 4, it becomes possible to craft Microformers. Microforming a small area creates a subbiome, which will change spawning monsters (including bees), blocks, plants, trees, etc. Some biomes will have Geodes. There are 34 bee types (of which only Xenodaemonae and Reaper can't survive on Garden planets), and these bees produce all sorts of things.

At tier 6, it becomes possible to craft Terraformers. This can change the planet type (if 50% of the planet is terraformed), which will change the outputs of Atmospheric Condenser. For example, you can obtain Trianglium Ore by terraforming the planet to Lightless. It will also change the weather (allowing access to Blue Grav-Liquid, etc.).

Note that microformers/terraformers don't create new ores in the ground.

### Precursor loot
Tenant quests "deal with bandits" can have Precursor drones as enemies (questgiver will call them "mysterious and deadly aliens"). They drop Precursor loot, including machines like Erchius Converter.
