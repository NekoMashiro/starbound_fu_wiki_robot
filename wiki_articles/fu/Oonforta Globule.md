# [FU] Oonforta Globule

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Oonforta_Globule

---

**Oonforta Globule** is a significantly better Flare. When thrown, it flies very quickly and is almost unaffected by gravity (can be thrown far away or even up into the air). It detonates either after 3 seconds or when colliding with an enemy. When detonated, it releases a cloud of glowing bright gas (which doesn't move and can hang even in mid-air).

Resulting gas illuminates the area for 2 minutes before disappearing. It is slightly toxic to enemies (inflicts some Poison and Sulphuric Acid damage, which is small, but ignores defense of the target).
