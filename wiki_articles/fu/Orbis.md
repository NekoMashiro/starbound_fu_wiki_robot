# [FU] Orbis

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Farm Beasts, Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Orbis

---

**Orbis** is a Farm Animal that produces Spare Parts. It hatches from Orbis Egg.

- Egg: Hatch time: 1000s if placed on ground, 600s in Incubator. Sold by various NPC traders.
- Adult: Hunger time: 22 minutes. Has a special Robotic diet, see List of Farm Beast Foods for details.

Unlike normal animals, Orbis produces Spare Parts instead of Poop. It also produces Oil instead of Water.
