# [FU] Ore Drone I

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ore_Drone_I

---

**Ore Drone** is a pet that follows you and periodically fires Multi-ore Detector (you can see the result). It doesn't fight.

## Variants
Ore Drone I can't detect high-tier ores. Ore Drone II can detect some of them. Ore Drone III and Mining Drone can detect all of them. Mining Drone will additionally detect caves.
