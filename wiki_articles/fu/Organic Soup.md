# [FU] Organic Soup

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Organic_Soup

---

**Organic Soup** is the best liquid for Trays (multiplying the speed of plant growth by 1.375). It randomly regenerates health and/or hunger of players who swim in it. Some planets (especially Crystalline) have rains of organic soup (this liquid falls from the sky and accumulates as puddles/lakes on the surface).

Organic Soup can also be used as fertilizer, but its bonuses are average (it is inferior to Advanced Fertilizer or Liquified Crystal). You might still want to use it for simplicity (since Trays accept it as both liquid and fertilizer, you would need less wiring to move it into trays automatically). If you desire higher output, use a better fertilizer.

Organic Soup is infinitely mixable.
