# [FU] Paint Brush

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Paint_Brush

---

**Paint Brush** is an early-game tool. It can paint the blocks without upgrading the Matter Manipulator.

Eventually you can add the painting feature to the Matter Manipulator, but it costs 3 Manipulator Modules.
