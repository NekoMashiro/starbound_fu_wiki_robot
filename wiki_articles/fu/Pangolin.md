# [FU] Pangolin

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Farm Beasts, Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pangolin

---

**Pangolin** is a Farm Animal that produces Hardened Monster Plate. It grows up from Pangolin Pup, which hatches from Pangolin Egg.

- Egg: Hatch time: 1000s if placed on ground, 600s in Incubator. Sold by various NPC traders.
- Baby: doesn't produce anything yet. Hunger time: 20.
- Adult: Hunger time: 20. Diet: Special Omnivore.
- : Harvest time: every 500-900 sec depending on happiness. Will not produce at all if starving.
- : Has a 10% chance to lay an egg once an hour, or every 40 minutes if happy.
