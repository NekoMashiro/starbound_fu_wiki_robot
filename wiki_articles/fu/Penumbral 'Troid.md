# [FU] Penumbral 'Troid

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Penumbral_'Troid

---

**Penumbral 'Troid** is a unique monster found on Penumbra and Strange Sea worlds. The inspiration for this monster came from the Dark Metroid, an enemy that you encounter throughout Metroid Prime: Echoes. The Penumbral 'Troid is much, much bigger than the source material. Objectively cuter than a Micro Poptop.

## Behavior
It tries to sit on your head and bite you. Allowing this to happen gives you some Madness currency.
