# [FU] Perfect Block

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perfect_Block

---

A Perfect Block is the brief moment when first raising a Shield. The shield is given a momentary "glow" or hightlight that fades quickly. During this time, any blocked attacks do not deplete the shields health, allowing the shield to stay from breaking longer.
