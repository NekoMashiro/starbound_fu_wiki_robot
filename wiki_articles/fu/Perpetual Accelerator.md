# [FU] Perpetual Accelerator

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Perpetual_Accelerator

---

**Perpetual Accelerator** is a tool that makes you faster (and faster, and faster, and faster!) the longer you hold it. 

You can achieve **unbelievably tremendous speed** by holding it for too long.

Switching to another item or teleporting resets the speed bonus.
