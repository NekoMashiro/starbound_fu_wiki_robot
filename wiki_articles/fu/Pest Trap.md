# [FU] Pest Trap

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pest_Trap

---

**Pest Trap** catches one Pest every 300-1200 seconds.

If there are other Pest Traps within 8 blocks, this Pest Trap will have significantly reduced production. Scan the Pest Trap to check if it was affected by this.
