# [FU] Plant Fibre

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Plant_Fibre

---

**Plant Fibre** is an omnipresent material. It can be collected with Matter Manipulator on the Surface layer of almost every planet.

Plant Fibre is an important component of clothing, equipment and furniture. You will need a ton of it.

Harvesting most crops (see below) has a small chance to yield some Plant Fibre, so it's relatively easy to get a steady supply of it.
