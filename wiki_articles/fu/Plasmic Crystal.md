# [FU] Plasmic Crystal

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Plasmic_Crystal

---

**Plasmic Crystal** is a tier 5 ore found on Strange Sea and Atropus planets.

It can also be produced and easily automated even in early-mid game through extraction Bloody Rock or Blood Crystal - Both of them can be crafted in Liquid Mixer with liquids that are obtainable using Infinite mixing feature. It is extremely useful if you really need **Plasmic Crystals** and Quietus Ore (Which you can extract from Plasmic Crystal itself) and don't have protection against Atropus Maddening atmosphere.
