# [FU] Poison

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poison

---

**Poison** is an infinitely mixable liquid. It's a very common hazard: swimming in it applies "Poisoned" debuff (damage over time). It exists as puddles/lakes on many planets. It also exists in infinite quantities on Toxic worlds.

It's notable as a component for infinite mixing of Organic Soup.
