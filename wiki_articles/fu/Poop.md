# [FU] Poop

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Poop

---

**Poop** is a low-tier fertilizer for Trays. It increases the yield (number of collected crops) by 1.

It is produced by Farm Beasts (except robotic and Erchibud), but only if they eat something. Unfed beasts will not produce Poop.

Robotic beasts produce Spare Parts instead of Poop, while Erchibud produces Erchius Crystal.
