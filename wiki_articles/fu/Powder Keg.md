# [FU] Powder Keg

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Powder_Keg

---

The **Powder Keg** mines background blocks in circular radius of 5 and foreground blocks in circular radius of 7. It's a cheap alternate to Crude Bomb that doesn't require Ammonium Sulfate, but is crafted in bundles of 5 and less effective compared to it (compared for 10 for every other placeable explosives).
