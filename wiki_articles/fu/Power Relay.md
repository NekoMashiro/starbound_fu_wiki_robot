# [FU] Power Relay

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Relay

---

**Power Relay** takes in sources of energy and distribute them either over a long range or to improve cable management over a short range by using multiple of these relays.

## Inputs and Outputs
- The lower blue circle () accepts power. Connect it to generators.
- The upper blue circle () can be wired to ON/OFF signal (such as Iron Lever or Tiny Wall Button). If off, the power won't be transferred.
- Red circle () outputs power. Connect it to buildings that require power.

The total output will be distributed as needed to any devices connected to the relay.
