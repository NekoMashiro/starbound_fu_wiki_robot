# [FU] Precursor Drone Control Chip

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precursor_Drone_Control_Chip

---

**Precursor Drone Control Chip** summons a "Precursor Drone" crew member (similarly to crew contracts that you buy at the Hiring Office).

Note that Precursor themselves (as a race) are extinct. This is a robot built by them, not a living Precursor.
