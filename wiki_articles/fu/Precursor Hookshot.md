# [FU] Precursor Hookshot

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precursor_Hookshot

---

This hookshot fires insanely fast, insanely far (past the edge of the screen), and reels you in insanely quickly. Note: firing this downwards can cause a LOT of falling damage instantly depending on the terrain distance.
