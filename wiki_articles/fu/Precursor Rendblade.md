# [FU] Precursor Rendblade

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precursor_Rendblade

---

**Precursor Rendblade** is a Tier 5 Broadsword. It is randomly found in precursor chests in the Precursor Ruins mission. It has the attack of a regular broadsword. Its alt ability shoots a wave of radiation out of the blade.
