# [FU] Precursor Trifangle

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Precursor_Trifangle

---

**Precursor Trifangle** is a precursor drone that is similar to the vanilla Trifangle, appearing mostly in Precursor biomes, Metallic Surface, and is guaranteed to spawn in multiple places of the Precursor Ruins mission. It prefers to stay at range to unleash 3 energy orbs at the player every 2 seconds. It is not capturable.

It recuperates from knockback 70% faster, and deals 10 (impact, physical) base damage on contact.

## Drops
It drops all the regular loot any cyber sphere mob would drop, but with a small chance of dropping an anulax battery.

## Fighting
As it has no base resistances and is somewhat unimpressive, the best way to kill it is to insult its meaningless existence.
