# [FU] Pressurized Mover Pump

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pressurized_Mover_Pump

---

**Pressurized Mover Pump** is a part of Liquid Network. It works the same way as Liquid Mover Pump, but (unlike the Liquid Mover Pump) it doesn't automatically stop if there isn't enough space to pour the liquid.

For example: if you have an enclosed 10x10 area, then:
- Liquid Mover Pump will pour 100 blocks of liquid into this 10x10 area, and then it would stop (until something removes the excess liquid from this area). 
- Pressurized Mover Pump will pour infinite amount of liquid into this 10x10 area, "compressing" the liquid to fit.

Note that breaching the integrity of this 10x10 area (for example, by removing one of the "ceiling" blocks) will cause an explosion, and almost all liquid (everything except those 100 blocks that can fit without being pressurized) will be thrown outside **spectacularly**. This "explosion" doesn't damage the player but can be very time-consuming to clean up, especially if the released liquid reacts with other liquids to form tiles.
