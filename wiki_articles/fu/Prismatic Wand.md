# [FU] Prismatic Wand

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Prismatic_Wand

---

**Prismatic Wand** fires a volley of 7 damaging shards. When these shards hit the ground, they remain there as mines, and any enemy that steps on them gets repeatedly stabbed. Shards disappear after several seconds.
