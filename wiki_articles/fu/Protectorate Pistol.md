# [FU] Protectorate Pistol

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Protectorate_Pistol

---

The **Protectorate Pistol** is the first upgrade to the Damaged Pistol. It is unique from the forthcoming upgrades in the upgrade line in that it does not use any Upgrade Modules, instead using Tungsten and Telebrium Bars to upgrade. It gains increased damage, as well as a small increase to the rate of fire.

While it is the 'fixed' upgrade of the Damaged Pistol, it does not stick out from many of the Tier 2 weapons the player can acquire.
