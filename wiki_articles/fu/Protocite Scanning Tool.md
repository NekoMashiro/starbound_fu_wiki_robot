# [FU] Protocite Scanning Tool

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Protocite_Scanning_Tool

---

**Protocite Scanning Tool** works the same way as "Scan Mode" of your Matter Manipulator, but has a much greater range, and its built-in flashlight is more bright.

You can place it into the Scan slot (same way as when installing Morphite Manipulator into the Manipulator slot).
