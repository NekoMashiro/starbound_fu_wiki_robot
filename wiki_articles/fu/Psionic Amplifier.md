# [FU] Psionic Amplifier

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Psionic_Amplifier

---

**Psionic Amplifier** converts brains (such as Inferior Brain) into Psi-energy. It's less efficient than crafting Psi-energy by hand (e.g. Superior Brain -> Superior Brain Housing -> Psi-2 Energy), but it is completely automatic and (unlike hand-crafing) doesn't require any materials other than brains.
