# [FU] Psionic Focus

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Psionic_Focus

---

**Psionic Focus** is a weapon that can load different types of ammo, e.g. Overload Ammo or Healing Ammo. All ammo types are crafted at Psionics Table.

## Loading ammo
Loading it is somewhat tricky (see below). We apologize for any inconvenience:

First you craft item like Cryokinesis Ammo. Then you turn Cryokinesis Ammo (raw ammo) into Cryokinesis Focus (which is basically a gun's magazine). That Cryokinesis Focus can then be loaded **into** the Psionic Focus the same way as you add EPP Augments into EPPs (by holding Cryokinesis Focus and right-clicking at Psionic Focus). Think of it as crafting the bullets, then loading a clip, which you then load into the gun.

To remove the current ammo (e.g. when you want to arm another type of ammo), right-click when Psionic Focus is selected. **Be careful not to right-click in the middle of battle (since it will disarm you).**

If you accidentally removed the magazine (item like Cryokinesis Focus) when it still had many charges in it (e.g. 25 out of 50), you can fill it to maximum number of charges via Psionic Focus Loader.
