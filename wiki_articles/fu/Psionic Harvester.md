# [FU] Psionic Harvester

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Psionic_Harvester

---

**Psionic Harvester** generates small amounts of Psionic Energy based on the number of Tenants (only those summoned with Colony Deed Mark II, not with the regular Colony Deed) within the distance of 128 blocks.

It also makes tenants happy, increasing the rent they pay to the Colony Core.
