# [FU] Purrpetual Energy

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Purrpetual_Energy

---

**Purrpetual Energy** is a cat-themed superfuel.

It is accepted by most power generators (from Alternator Generator to Quantum Reactor), but because it requires non-craftable Kitty Hat or The Un-Cat Hat, it can't be obtained in any meaningful quantities.

## Lore
Cats always land on their feet, and sandwiches with butter always fall "butter to the floor", so if we tie a sandwich to the cat's back, it will rotate infinitely. This rotation powers the reactor.

## Trivia
- It would take 360 Ultra Batteries to store the output of 1 Purrpetual Energy in a Precursor Reactor. Since it's impossible to have this many batteries (the game will lag to a halt), some power will be wasted if the machinery doesn't consume it immediately.
