# [FU] Pus

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Pus

---

**Pus** is a disgusting liquid that exists as infinite ocean on some Atropus Sea planets. It also can exist as puddles on Atropus and Lightless planets.

When making automated factories, beware that Pus has several Mixer-only mixings that don't work in-world (with Essentia Obscura, Black Tar and Liquid Irradium).
