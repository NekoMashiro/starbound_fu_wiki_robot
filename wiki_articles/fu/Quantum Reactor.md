# [FU] Quantum Reactor

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Powered Station, Power, Power Generator, Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Quantum_Reactor

---

The **Quantum Reactor** is the most efficient Power generator crafted at the Power Station and the second-most powerful generator, outclassed only by the ultra-rare Precursor Reactor, and can output 620W at its highest capacity. It can use bio-fuels and high-end fuels such as Solarium Stars and Plutonium.

Unlike the Fission Reactor, Small and Large Fusion Reactors, this reactor emits no radiation and requires no coolant. As a consequence, it produces no Toxic Waste. However, this means it can be safely placed anywhere without having to consider side effects or worry about explosions. 

## Mechanics
Power produced by Quantum Reactor is a sum of two numbers:
 Total power = Power from fuel + Power from heat

Power from fuel depends on the type of fuel (see #Accepted fuel below).

Power from heat is initially 0. When fuel is placed into the Quantum Reactor, the reactor starts heating up (5% per second, reaching maximum heat after 20 seconds), and its power increases as follows:

| Heat Threshold (%) | Power Output (W) |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | 0 |
| 5 | 5 |
| 20 | 9 |
| 30 | 12 |
| 50 | 16 |
| 70 | 20 |

If the reactor is not working, its heat level is decreased by 5% every second.
