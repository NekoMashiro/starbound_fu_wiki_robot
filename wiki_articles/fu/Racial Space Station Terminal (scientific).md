# [FU] Racial Space Station Terminal (scientific)

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Racial_Space_Station_Terminal_(scientific)

---

**Racial Space Station Terminal (scientific)** is a purchasable shop (at Hiring Office) that randomly turns into one of the 3 other terminals when placed (medical, military or trading).

Once it is placed, you can't move it (if you mine it with your Matter Manipulator, it self-destructs).
