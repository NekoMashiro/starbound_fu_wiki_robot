# [FU] Rain Barrel

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rain_Barrel

---

**Rain Barrel** can soak some items in water (for example, soaking Wheat is a step in preparing Malt, which is used in brewing).

Despite what the tooltip claims, Rain Barrel doesn't collect Water. If you want to collect Water, you can use a Well.
