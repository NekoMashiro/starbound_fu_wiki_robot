# [FU] Randomized spawners

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Randomized_spawners

---

Randomized spawners are a feature inserted into certain dungeons and microdungeons in Frackin Universe.

Randomized spawners not only allow a variety of enemies to spawn, but also a varied number. For example: what would have spawned a room of two regular cultists may now spawn 0-4 basic cultists, 'scientist' cultists, assault cultists, sniper cultists, or rocket cultists, in order of rarity ascending.

Randomized spawners are featured in the following dungeons and microdungeons:

### Space
- All 'hazard' areas.

- Bandit Ships

- Cultist Ships

- Cultist Mine

- Apex Weapon Platform (Redirects to Skath Weapons Platform)

- Skath Weapons Platform 

#### Purple-Swirly encounters
- Bandit Asteroid

### Macrodungeons
- Cultist Lair

- Sewer

- Pirate Spaceship

### Microdungeons
- Cultist Altar (Microdungeon)

- Cultist Tents

- Bandit Tower

- Bandit Tents

- Agaran Pods
