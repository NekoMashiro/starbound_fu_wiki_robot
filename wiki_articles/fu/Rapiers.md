# [FU] Rapiers

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapiers

---

Rapier is a one handed procedurally generated melee weapon model. They function in a sort-of forward stabbing motion in whatever direction the player is facing. It can be common, uncommon, and rare.

## Base Statistics
- Base DPS: 5.0-5.5 health/s
- Fire Time: 0.4-0.7 seconds between 1x-damage attacks (~1.4-2.5 1x-damage attacks/s)
- Combo Progression:
1. First Strike: 1.0x damage, 10 default knockback
1. Second Strike: 0.6x damage, 8 default knockback
1. Third Strike: 0.8x damage, 9 default knockback
1. Fourth Strike: 0.7x damage, 8 default knockback
1. Fifth Strike: 0.4x damage, 9 default knockback
1. Sixth Strike: 1.2x damage, 12 default knockback
- Crit Chance: 1% (Common), 2% (Uncommon), 3% (Rare)
- Base Crit Damage Bonus: +2 damage (Common), +3 damage (Uncommon), +4 damage (Rare)

## Elemental Damage
Uncommon and rare Rapiers are assigned one of seven elements (non-physical damage types) on generation. Neither uncommon nor rare Rapiers inflict elemental status effects.

- Fire: Inflicts [Burning](https://starbounder.org/Burning)
- Ice: Inflicts [Frost](https://starbounder.org/Frost)
- Poison: Inflicts [Poisoned](https://starbounder.org/Poisoned)
- Electric: Inflicts [Electrified](https://starbounder.org/Electrified)
- Radiation: Inflicts Radiation Burn
- Shadow: Inflicts Shadow Gas
- Cosmic: Inflicts Armor

## Special Attacks
Rapiers , like most one-handed weapon types, cannot generate with any special attacks.
