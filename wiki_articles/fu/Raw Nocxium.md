# [FU] Raw Nocxium

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Raw_Nocxium

---

**Raw Nocxium** is an Elder-themed ore. It is extraordinarily rare even in the Elder-themed biomes.

Instead of finding this ore, you might choose to pursue Madness research for the recipes to craft synthetic Nocxium Bars without this ore. Alternatively, you can crush large quantities of Elder Stone or Shadow Rock blocks (they have small traces of Nocxium in them).
