# [FU] Refinery

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Refinery

---

**Refinery** is a vanilla building that slowly exchanges ores/bars for money at higher prices than simply selling them. See https://starbounder.org/Refinery for details.

Aside from vanilla ores/bars, some FU-specific materials like Silicon can be exchanged too.
