# [FU] Regeneration Matrix

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Regeneration_Matrix

---

**Regeneration Matrix** heals everyone within a radius of 500 blocks (including player, NPCs, monsters, bees, etc.) by 0.5% of their maximum health per second.

It can be used to notice bees easier, because it shows a healing animation near them (and all other targets).
