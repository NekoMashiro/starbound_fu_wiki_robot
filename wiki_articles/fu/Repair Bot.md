# [FU] Repair Bot

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Repair_Bot

---

**Repair Bots** are passive monsters that does not attack unless provoked. They can be found on ***Cyber Spheres alongside Cleaning Bots.*** They look similar to Rob Repairo (robot on vanilla Outpost).

They can not be captured.
