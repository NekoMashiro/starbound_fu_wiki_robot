## RESOURCES
Resources in Frackin' Universe are quite changed from Vanilla Starbound in some ways, but pretty much the same in others. There's two major categories: Ores and Fluids. The former can be mined from the start with the Matter Manipulator, while the latter (as the category name implies) requires the Liquid Mining Upgrade. On a side note, the Hylotl have the Liquid Mining Upgrade pre-installed when you play as one.
