# [FU] Robotic foods

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Food by diet type | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Robotic_foods

---

Eating these foods is like biting pieces of metal, and the taste is even worse. Only Robots can eat this.

 Herbivores, Carnivores and Omnivores are poisoned by eating these foods.
