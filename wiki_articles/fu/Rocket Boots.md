# [FU] Rocket Boots

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rocket_Boots

---

Instead of a traditional double-jump, Rocket Boots will pause you in place for a moment, and then launch you in one of the cardinal directions you are pressing at the time. The total distance launched is quite small however, and there is no inherent protection from fall damage.
