# [FU] Rocket Turret

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rocket_Turret

---

**Rocket Turret** is a variant of Defense Turret that deals damage to a small area. It can be devastating to slow enemies or enemies that move in close formation.

The weakness of this turret is that its projectile is very slow, so it has a very high chance to miss a moving target.
