# [FU] Rotten Food

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Rotten_Food

---

**Rotten Food** is an early-game fertilizer. Any food will turn into this if its expiration timer reaches 0. You can refine it into Mulch (which is a better fertilizer).

You can produce Rotten Food very quickly by throwing unneeded food into the Compost Bin.
