# [FU] Sapling

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Saplings | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sapling

---

**Sapling** can be placed on any valid soil (like Dirt). In 5-10 minutes it will grow into a tree (assuming there is enough space above it).

Resulting tree can then be chopped to get wood (and maybe other items). 

You can make custom saplings at the Cloning Lab. You can also craft Apple/Peach/Pear saplings at the Greenhouse.
