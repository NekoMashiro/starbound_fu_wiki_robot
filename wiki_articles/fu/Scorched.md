# [FU] Scorched

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Scorched

---

**Scorched** (Decayed) are tier 6 vanilla planets. They are extremely hot.

Its inhabitants didn't believe in climate change, and now you need an EPP to visit their planet.

Similarly to the Wasteland planets, these worlds are full of junk (but otherwise Scorched and Wasteland are very different).
