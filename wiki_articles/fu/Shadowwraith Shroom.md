# [FU] Shadowwraith Shroom

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shadowwraith_Shroom

---

Shadowwraith Shroom is a unique type of [Sporgus](https://starbounder.org/Sporgus) which has adapted to dark, corrupt environments. Possessing a set of ominous red eyes, and a glowing green gem in the center of it's rotting form. This "gem" is actually the Shadowwraith Shrooms third eye. The creatures third eye defines this monster as a force with which to be reckoned. It can lure victims into a state of hypnosis, where all they see is the third eye glowing in the darkness. Inexperienced hunters will die in the seconds that follow. Staring directly into the captivating deep green of the Shadowwraith Shrooms third eye evokes feelings of deep desire and crazed greed. So maddening is the allure of the third eye that it sends many onlookers into a decent of madness, where the only desire is to possess the lustrous gem. To the woe of these individuals, killing a Shadowwraith Shroom and removing it's third eye yields only a slow, and painful death. The spores from the Shadowwraith Shroom's body attach to the broken creature and mutate them into one of their own - this is the curse of the Shadowwraith Shrooms.

The Shadowwraith Shrooms is a hostile entity that seeks to spread it's corruption with any living creature it encounters. Capable of firing dark, electrified clouds of corruption, these projectiles deal significant damage in greatly impact a target. The impacts are that of shattered armor, electrified flesh, and pain so intense that it induces madness.
