# [FU] Shocktop

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Shocktop

---

Poptops are a unique monster type, found generally in tranquil environments. They wander around aimlessly whistling to themselves, but when the player draws near they get aggressive, attacking with leaping bites and now, electrically charged long ranged tackles. Poptops are astoundingly adaptive, and have shown themselves to be quite resilient when faced with even the most extreme environments. Poptops are native to worlds of forest and tranquil environments, however due to the high demand for these creatures as pets, they have been spread to many worlds and planets they otherwise never would have been introduced too. This introduction of Poptops into different environments has lead to the recorded extinction of 300+ indigenous species, countless other wildlife, and lead to numerous biological adaptations and evolution within the species.

A Shocktop can be identified by the sparks, puffs of smoke, and blue aura surrounding it's electrified body as it skips around. The Shocktop now leaves damaging electric clouds after lunging, dealing damage and inflicting the “shocked” debuff. Additionally, the Shocktop moves and attacks much quicker than the common Poptop.
