# [FU] Short-Barreled Shotgun

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Short-Barreled_Shotgun

---

**Short-Barreled Shotgun** is a one-handed procedurally generated ranged weapon model.

It can be common, uncommon, and rare. Common Short-Barreled Shotguns will shoot bullets, while uncommon and rare ones will shoot elemental plasma shots.

## Base Statistics
- **Base DPS:** 7.5-8.5 health/s (Common/Uncommon), 8.0-9.0 health/s (Rare)
- **Fire Time:** 1.0-2.5 seconds between shots (0.4-1.0 shots/s)
- **Energy Usage:** 22.5-25.5 energy/s (Common/Uncommon), 24.0-27.0 energy/s (Rare)
- **Bullets per Shot:** 8-10
- **Knockback:** 10-20 (weak to average knockback)
- **Crit Chance:** 5% (Common), 6% (Uncommon), 7% (Rare)
- **Base Crit Damage Bonus:** +1 damage

## Elemental Damage
Uncommon and rare Short-Barreled Shotguns are assigned one of seven elements (non-physical damage types) on generation. Though both uncommon and rare Short-Barreled Shotguns exhibit elemental visuals, only rare Short-Barreled Shotguns inflict status effects.

- Fire: Inflicts Burning
- Ice: Inflicts Frost
- Poison: Inflicts Poisoned
- Electric: Inflicts Electrified
- Radiation: Inflicts Radiation Burn
- Shadow: Inflicts Shadow Gas
- Cosmic: Inflicts Armor

## Special Attacks
Short-Barreled Shotguns, like most one-handed weapon types, cannot generate with any special attacks.
