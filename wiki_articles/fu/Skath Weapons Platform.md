Also referred to as a "Skath Dreadnought"
## Details
Skath Weapons Platforms are a random dungeon found orbiting any star. It contains Skath blocks, furniture, and NPCs, as well as some Miniknog prisoners. 
Most of its rooms are randomly generated, and the station itself is selected from three possible presets.

The "Strange Map" item located in some of its rooms gives one of the two Apex secondary missions.
