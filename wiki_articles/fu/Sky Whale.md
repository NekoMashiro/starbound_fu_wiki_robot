# [FU] Sky Whale

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sky_Whale

---

"Passive gas-guzzling behemoths."

The Sky Whale is a neutral monster found in Gas Giants. They float around in the air, completely passive to the player until they are hit.

## Properties
Base health: 750 (Multiply by tier)

Detection Radius: 20 Blocks

Hostile Radius: 50 Blocks (Will wait for 5 seconds if player is out of this range before becoming passive again)

If hostile, the Sky whale will follow the player until the distance between is within 14 blocks, before initiating an attack.

### Attacks
The Sky whale has only 1 attack, being a simple charge attack:

Initiation Radius: 15 blocks

Base Damage: 20 health

Behaviour: Aims directly towards target (Easily countered by a small hole in the wall that you can shoot through)
