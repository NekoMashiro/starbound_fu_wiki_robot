# [FU] Slime Grenade Launcher

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Slime_Grenade_Launcher

---

**Slime Grenade Launcher** is an unusual hunting weapon. It quickly fires small grenades that (when detonated) release poisonous gas. These grenades don't explode immediately: instead, you can right-click when you want to detonate them all. If you don't do that, they automatically explode 10 seconds after launch.

This delayed detonation is similar to Blastcap Launcher (which could only fire 1 grenade at a time and was neither poisonous nor a hunting weapon).
