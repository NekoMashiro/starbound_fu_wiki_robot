# [FU] Sniper Drone

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sniper_Drone

---

**Sniper Drone** spawns drones that automatically fire at enemies (even those extremely far away).

If the enemy is too far away to notice the player's mech, being hit by the Sniper Drone won't cause this enemy to aggro on the player.

There is no minimal range: nearby enemies will also be targeted.
