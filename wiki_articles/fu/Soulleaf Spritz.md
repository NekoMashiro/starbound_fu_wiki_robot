# [FU] Soulleaf Spritz

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Soulleaf_Spritz

---

**Soulleaf Spritz** is a very secret and very godly drink, the pinnacle of brewing.

Its recipe is obtained the same way as the recipe of Miracle Ambrosia.

## Effect
Ethereal buff allows the player to escape deadly situations by temporarily turning into a ghost. Ghosts can't be damaged by enemies (are invulnerable to all attacks), but they can't damage the enemies either. Your crew and pet would continue attacking enemies as usual.

The ghost is immune to fall damage, doesn't need to breathe, its hunger doesn't increase.

Ghost's energy is always 0 and doesn't regenerate. Ghosts are drunk and nude (purely visual, armor bonuses are still applied).
