# [FU] Spider Nest

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Spider_Nest

---

**Spider Nest** is an underground biome with spider-like creatures, such as Massive Spider and Megarachnid (boss).

Stay away if you have arachnophobia. You can tell it's the spider nest because there is a very-very-very long fall into an area with normal blocks. Cobwebs also give you an idea. Brave adventurers only.
