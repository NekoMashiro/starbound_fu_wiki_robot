# [FU] Stack Splitter

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Stack_Splitter

---

**Stack Splitter** is a 4-slot storage. If you place a stack of items into slot 1, it will be immediately split between the other 3 slots.

For example, if you place 789 items into slot 1, it will move 700 items into slot 2, 80 items into slot 3, 9 items into slot 4.

See also: Stack Halver.
