# [FU] Star Spawn of Cthulhu

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Elder | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Star_Spawn_of_Cthulhu

---

**Star Spawn of Cthulhu** is a unique monster found in the Delta Freya II mission and Eldritch Field minibiomes. It is extremely strong (deals a lot of damage, can teleport to the player), and it drops valuable Elder-themed items. It is not capturable.

They only spawn at night.
