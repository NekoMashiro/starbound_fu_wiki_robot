# [FU] Star-Spawn Acolyte

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Bosses, Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Star-Spawn_Acolyte

---

Star-Spawn Acolyte is a miniboss found in the Delta Freya II mission. Drops good loot.

**Note:** If the player dies while fighting this monster it will despawn and won't reappear unless they restart the whole mission.
