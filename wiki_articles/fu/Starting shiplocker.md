# [FU] Starting shiplocker

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Mechanics | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Starting_shiplocker

---

**Starting shiplocker** always exists on your ship. At the beginning of the game, it has some basic supplies (Canned Food, Torches, etc.). It also has the tutorial codexes.
