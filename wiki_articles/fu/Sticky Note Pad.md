# [FU] Sticky Note Pad

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sticky_Note_Pad

---

**Sticky Note Pad** is a tool for placing "notes" (decorative objects with any text of your choosing) onto the background walls. Once the note is placed, you can read its text by scanning the note.
