# [FU] Storage Bridge V2

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Storage_Bridge_V2

---

**Storage Bridge V2** has the abilities of both Storage Bridge and Capacity Sensor.

Unless you need the Capacity Sensor functionality, it's recommended to use the regular Storage Bridge instead (it slightly improves performance of the game).
