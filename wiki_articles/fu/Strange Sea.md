# [FU] Strange Sea

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Strange_Sea

---

**Strange Sea** is a special type of ocean planet. It is composed of Plasmic Fluid, and has islands made primarily of Cell.

Other than having poisonous air, it's relatively safe for such a high-tier planet. It's notable for having weird/rare monsters and for its diverse core layers (there are many possibilities as to what its core will be, including Liquid Irradium core or Elder Core).

## Plasmic Disruption hazard
Plasmic Disruption deals no damage, but it reduces Shadow, Radioactive, Cosmic and Electric resistances by 20%. It is fully negated if you have either Gas Immunity or 60% Poison resistance.

## About third-party mods that modify Strange Sea
If you use third-party mods that turn Strange Sea into a "liquid Unknown" planet, note that:
1. The planet will have the same hazards as on Unknown planets. These hazards are challenging, but not undefeatable. **See the page Unknown for strategies to counter them**.
1. It may be a problem to find Plasmic Fluid, because unmodified Strange Sea is the only plentiful source of it, and these mods change that. See the page Plasmic Fluid for other methods to obtain it (sifting, bees, etc.).

Note that you don't need these mods to obtain liquids. FU already has mechanics like infinite mixing, some liquids are extracted from plentiful items/crops, other liquids are directly craftable.
