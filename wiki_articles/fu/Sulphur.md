# [FU] Sulphur

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sulphur

---

**Sulphur** is an omnipresent tier 1 ore (it exists on almost every planet). It is used to make Fertilizers and other complex chemicals. It can also be extracted into Sulphuric Acid.

Easiest way to obtain it is to sift various soil blocks (Dirt, etc.), especially Sulphuric Dirt.
