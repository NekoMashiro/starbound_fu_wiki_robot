# [FU] Sunset Riders: Secrets

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Sunset_Riders:_Secrets

---

You can find 2 hidden room in the Sunset Riders mines, both rooms containing a lot of diamonds and silver. To find them you will need to head to the second puzzle jump room in the mines.

## First room
From the entrance of the room, take the stairs leading down to the lake below. Near the surface of the lake, you can find a hidden door. You just need to interact with it to open it and reach a small room containing diamond and silver deposits.

## Second room
The second room is a bit harder to reach, but contains even more diamonds/silver. From the entrance of the puzzle jump room, head to the left and head into the little cave below. You will need to fight 1 Batong and 2 Peblits there. You will then reach a chasm with spikes at the bottom. A Batong also fly around there. You will need to use ropes or a grappling hook to swing across the chasm. On the other side, you will find 3 silver deposits and a lever. Pull the lever and backtrack to the puzzle jump room. The lever opened a room in the top right of the cavern, where you'll find plenty of diamonds and silver.
