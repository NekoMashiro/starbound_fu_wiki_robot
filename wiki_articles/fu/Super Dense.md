# [FU] Super Dense

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Super_Dense

---

**Super Dense** is a high-gravity planet (beware of fall damage!) composed of randomly chosen biomes (both on the surface and underground), similarly to other randomized planets (Shadow and Unknown).

- Outputs of Atmospheric Condenser on this planet are the same as on normal planets (Garden, Eden, etc.), regardless of the primary biome.
- All species of bees have normal production (no bonus/penalty for biome).

## Environmental hazards
Super Dense planets have randomly chosen hazards, similarly to Unknown planets.
> === List of all possible hazards ===

Possible hazards are picked from a smaller list than for Unknown planets. For example, a Super Dense planet can't have an Erchius Ghost. It also won't have Lethal hazards (which require 80% resistance to negate them). On the other hand, some "easier" hazards (like Mild Poison or Tropical Humidity) are also not available for Super Dense planets.

These are internal IDs of hazards (e.g. ffbiomecold1 is Mild Cold and ffbiomecold3 is Extreme Cold).
