# [FU] Tall Chair

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tall_Chair

---

**Tall Chair** is an impractical but amazing furniture. If you sit on it, it instantly lifts you very high into the air. Dismounting from the chair returns you to the ground.

Combined with a zoom like 2x or 1x (beware of lag if you zoom to 1x), this chair allows you to observe your base from above.

## Limitations
- It can't be used as an elevator (when you are sitting on the chair, any attempt to jump, use Physics Field, etc. will return you to the ground).
- If you exit the game while sitting in this chair, next time you load the game you will appear high in the air (which may result in serious fall damage).

## Trivia
- Tall Chair is 32 times taller than Wooden Chair.
