# [FU] Terramart Shipments

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Terramart_Shipments

---

**Terramart Shipments** is a vanilla shop that allows to you sells foods, seeds, etc. for 30% of their base price (normal shops: 20%).

If you want to sell other items (not only food) or to automate the selling with ITDs, you can use Terramart Shipments 3.0 instead.
