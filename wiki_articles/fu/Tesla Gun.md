# [FU] Tesla Gun

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tesla_Gun

---

The primary fire is a large, piercing bolt of electricity. The alt-fire causes multiple, wide bolts of electricity to rain down the top of the screen, landing roughly on the same plane as the player. The alt-fire *does* work underground (no open sky necessary), and can occasionally hit enemies above your position.
