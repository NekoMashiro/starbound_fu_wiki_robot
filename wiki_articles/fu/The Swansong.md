### Strategy
The Swansong was buffed in FU by giving it resistances to account for the strength of FU mechs. 
The fight is especially difficult due to its length. While the infobox says it has 6000 health, in practice it has **51,000** due to the scaling of the mission and its health bar must be depleted twice (this is unchanged from Vanilla).
The ophanim it spawns have 2,975 health in practice, and are unchanged by FU.

Its lowest resistances are to physical and radioactive, so it is recommended to use the physical mech weapons in the Superior Mech Weapons research node.
It also has a 60% resistance to universal damage types.
