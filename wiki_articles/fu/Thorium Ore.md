# [FU] Thorium Ore

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Thorium_Ore

---

**Thorium Ore** is a tier 4 radioactive ore. It is abundant on Gelatinous and Ice Waste planets. It can be found in smaller quantities on Volcanic Primeval and Irradiated planets.
