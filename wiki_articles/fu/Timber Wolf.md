# [FU] Timber Wolf

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Timber_Wolf

---

**Timber Wolf** is a small, lythe, and powerful hunter. This creature poses a substantial threat alone, and more so when in a pack. Possessing small legs, and dog like traits, this creature can move quickly, but lacks in its ability to jump. Additionally, this creature deals damage solely through contact, small lunging bites and aggressive behavior can quickly become overwhelming. Possesses no outstanding abilities or unique traits.

Primarily found at night, their dark pelts make them ideal night hunters. A pair of glowing yellow eyes closes in quickly, often times the last thing a creature sees before being torn apart.
