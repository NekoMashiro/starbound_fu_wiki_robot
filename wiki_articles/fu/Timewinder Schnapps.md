# [FU] Timewinder Schnapps

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Timewinder_Schnapps

---

**Timewinder Schnapps** is a temporally unstable drink. 5 seconds after you drink it, all your resources (health, etc.) and location will be reverted to where they were at the moment of drinking it. It can be to your benefit if timed correctly.

This is similar to Temporal Instability hazard on Unknown planets.
