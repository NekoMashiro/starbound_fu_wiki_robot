# [FU] Tiny Bee

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tiny_Bee

---

The **Tiny Bee** is one of many types of flying insectoids, that make up this category of creature, this one being the most common. This variety is noted by it's yellow and black striped body that shares similarities with a wasp, and it's incredibly tiny size. This creature is the same as the "Bee" creature, but approximately 1/10th the size. (Too smol)

When it charges, the bee swoops at a target relatively quickly, striking for overall minimal damage, but leaving the target with a unique debuff called "bee sting". This debuff does low damage over time similar to poison. Additionally, this debuff has the effect of attracting any bees within a large radius that are not already attacking the victim - causing them to fly over and begin doing so.

## Luna's Lab Notes
~ .This type of Bee seems to be attracted to flowers or petal blocks. When around, their landing and "gathering" idle animation takes place more often. May be coincidence, but could be useful in environments where they swarm. Additionally, these creatures, while not attacking something, do not fly too far from the ground, typically hovering only a few feet from the surface. This minimizes the threat of a sudden attack from the sky or unexpected angle.

~ Possessing only 1hp may make these creatures seem trivial to deal with, but their increased spawnrate, quick movements, and damage overtime can become troublesome in swarms. Quite befitting of bees really. Interestingly enough, these creatures group together when attacking. Rather than taking different angles of attack, it seems that any attacking Bees will first gather together into a swarm and all dive at once (dealing massive damage depending on the size of the swarm).
