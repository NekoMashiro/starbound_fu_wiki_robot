# [FU] Topling

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Topling

---

**Topling**, also known as **Micro Poptop**, is a tiny baby Poptop. It will murder you with milk teeth instead of permanent teeth.

They are frequently spawned by Mama Poptop boss. When injured, she will eat them to recover her health.

A Micro Poptop can be identified by its tiny stature, and dwarfed body. Much less dangerous than a common poptop, these creatures have been bred as pets. As such, they are much less dangerous than their wild, feral kin.

## Luna's Lab Notes
~ The Micro Poptops whistles a unique melody that is similar to their larger kin, but more high pitched and with more errors. Still learning to create the beautiful melodies that they will use to bring prey in close, the song whistled is "Vast Immortal Suns".

~ With time, or raw meat, the Micro Poptop grows into a normal sized Poptop. If given further resources and time, this creature can be raised and reared to fearsome proportions. Take care in your choice of pet -
