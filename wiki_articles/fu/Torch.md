# [FU] Torch

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Torch

---

**Torch** is the most basic light source. You can place it onto the blocks or background walls. See also: list of other lights.

Unlike in vanilla Starbound, torches in FU are one-handed (you can hold a Torch in one hand and Mining Laser in another).
