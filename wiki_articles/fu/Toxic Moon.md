# [FU] Toxic Moon

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Imported | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Toxic_Moon

---

**Toxic Moons** are airless moons that are full of Raw Sewage. They are mostly safe and offer a good opportunity to mine Erchius fuel of both kinds without a threat of the ghost, as well as Plutonium. They tend to have low gravity.

While not inherently dangerous, you might require Poison EPP II (or higher if your species if vulnerable to Poison) and some means of breathing.

## Notable Features
You can find Crystal Erchius Fuel, Moonstone and Plutonium on toxic moons.

There are no crops or trees.
