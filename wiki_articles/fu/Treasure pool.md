# [FU] Treasure pool

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Mechanics | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Treasure_pool

---

**Treasure pool** is a list of randomly found items (items in chests, items dropped from monsters, etc.). Each item has its own chance to appear (e.g. 10% or 2%).

For example, musical instruments are a pool with items like Accordion, and they can be dropped by many monsters.

Starbound doesn't show the pools to the player when in-game, so some of those pools have non-human-friendly names (such as fucuteequipment).
