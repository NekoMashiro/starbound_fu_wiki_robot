# [FU] Trianglium Ore

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trianglium_Ore

---

**Trianglium Ore** is a tier 4 ore. It is present on many tier 4+ planets, but Tar Ball planets (underground only) are particularly rich with it.

This ore is from vanilla Starbound, but is not used there.
