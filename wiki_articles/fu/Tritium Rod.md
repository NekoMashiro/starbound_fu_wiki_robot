# [FU] Tritium Rod

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Tritium_Rod

---

**Tritium Rod** is a potent fuel (providing 320W power in Quantum Reactor). It is a common byproduct of nuclear reactions: each time 1 fuel is consumed by Fission Reactor, there is a 50% chance that 1 Tritium Rod would appear.
