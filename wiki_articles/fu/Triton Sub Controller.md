# [FU] Triton Sub Controller

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Triton_Sub_Controller

---

**Triton** is a submarine that is capable of deep sea exploration. Gives the player unlimited supply of oxygen and allows fast movement underwater. This vehicle can only operate in liquid. The vehicle can be spawned on ground, but you will not be able to get in. Can be extremely helpful in searching for Hylotl underwater cities. The Triton is unarmed and basically defenseless but fast enough to simply run from predators.

The vehicle has two seats. Passenger in back and pilot in front.

**If your submarine won't move no matter what, then you are in a passenger seat. Exit the sub and reposition yourself to the front seat.**

## Limitations
**WARNING** : The Triton is only suitable to swim in water. ALL liquids/weather that has a negative effect to the player, will affect the Triton too. A much tougher upgrade is available, the Poseidon sub.
