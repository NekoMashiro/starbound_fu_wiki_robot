# [FU] Trollfist Striker

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Joke weapons | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Trollfist_Striker

---

**Trollfist Striker** is a useless weapon. When hit, the enemy becomes invulnerable for a while, and its image is replaced with "trollface" symbol. You can't damage that enemy, but it can attack you.

It's still possible to hurt the enemies with Trollfist Striker, because the enemy will take the damage from the first strike, and can be attacked again after the "trollface" status wears off.

## Non-military uses
- All strikers break blocks, so Trollfist Striker can be used for mining.
- Trollfist Striker can be placed into Warped Regulator.
