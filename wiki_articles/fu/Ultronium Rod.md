# [FU] Ultronium Rod

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Ultronium_Rod

---

**Ultronium** is an ultra-radioactive isotope that does not occur naturally. You cannot mine, obtain, or craft it directly - only as a side product. It is one of the key ingredients in creating a Neutronium Rod. While Ultronium Rods can be used as fuel, it is highly unpractical.

At least **40** Ultronium Rods are required in order to craft an Atom Smasher. You will need to refine/centrifuge an approximate of 450 Solarium Stars, more than 3000 Neptunium Rods or Thorium Rods, or 600 Raw Nocxium to get that amount.
