# [FU] Unknown Device

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Device

---

**Unknown Device** is a mysterious Ancient artifact that can't be crafted manually.
You find it in the secret chamber in Dark Cavern mission (on the first Garden planet). Reaching it will require a Spider Microsphere or Precursor Microsphere.

Currently the only 2 uses for this item is to either be crafted into Atom Smasher, or extracted for valuable resources.

Extracting Unknown Device takes longer than other items - about 45 minutes with Extraction Lab, 5 minutes 40 seconds with Extraction Lab MKII, and 1 minute with Quantum Extractor.
Because extractors are machinery (they work only within loaded parts of the map), this extraction won't progress further while you are on another planet.
