# [FU] Unknown Egg

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Unknown_Egg

---

**Unknown Egg** is a variant of Dinosaur Egg that hatches into a Mega-Fauna. That monster can destroy blocks, so be careful not to hatch it in the middle of your base.

It takes 2900 seconds to hatch (when placed on the ground). Unlike other eggs, it can't be hatched in an Incubator.
