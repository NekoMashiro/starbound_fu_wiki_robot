# [FU] Upgrade Module

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Upgrade_Module

---

Upgrade Modules are extremely important items used for:
1. Upgrading weapons/armor up a tier (increasing stats)
1. Upgrading certain weapons into new weapons (see list)
1. Crafting crew beds
1. Creating certain items/buildings as listed below.

Prior to the mid-to-late game, the primary source of Upgrade Modules will be loot from boxes and treasure chests in the world. Later on, you can create your own in bulk.
