# [FU] Upgrade crafting station

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Mechanics | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Upgrade_crafting_station

---

You can **upgrade some crafting stations** (like Machining Table): Machining Table (you craft it directly) -> Auto-Assembler -> Matter Assembler (ultimate upgrade).

Higher-tier stations allow you to craft more things: for example, you can craft Ore Drone I (a low-tier item) even at the most basic Machining Table, but Ore Drone II (more advanced item) requires Auto-Assembler. Upgraded station can craft anything the lower-tier station could, so if you want to craft Ore Drone I, you can still do so at the Auto-Assembler.
