# [FU] Warm Moon

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Warm_Moon

---

**Warm Moon** is a class of moon, instances of which are found in the hot orbital region around most stars. It includes moons of type Crystalline, Strange Sea, Eden, Desert Moon, Volcanic Moon, Toxic Moon, Rocky Moon, Desert, Forest, Cyber Sphere, Tar Ball, Mountainous, Tidewater, Fungal, Moon, Barren, Irradiated, Volcanic, Primeval, Volcanic Primeval, Atropus, Red Desert, Proto World, Alien, Tropical Ocean, Toxic Moon, Atropus Sea, Gelatinous, Rainforest, and Tabula Rasa.
