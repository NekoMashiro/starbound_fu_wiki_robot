# [FU] Weapon Compressor

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Compressor

---

**Weapon Compressor** makes weapons invisible when held (this doesn't affect damage or any other stats of the weapon).

To revert the process (make weapon visible again), put it through the Weapon Compressor again.

## How to unlock
This item is unlocked by completing Kevin's quest "Feed Khe".
