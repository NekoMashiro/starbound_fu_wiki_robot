# [FU] Weapon Shuffler

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Shuffler

---

**Weapon Shuffler** is a device that transforms a randomly generated (RNG) weapon into another (completely random) weapon of the same type/tier (e.g. T4 Bow -> T4 Bow).

New weapon might have another Alt ability, elemental type, rarity, damage, rate of fire, etc.

## Usage
Place the old weapon into one slot, and one Upgrade Module into another slot. The module will be consumed, and new weapon will appear.
