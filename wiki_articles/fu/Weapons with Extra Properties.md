# [FU] Weapons with Extra Properties

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Weapons | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Weapons_with_Extra_Properties

---

This category is intended for weapons with properties or effects that are not indicated in the in-game tooltip. For example, Kirhosi Blaster is included here because the tooltip fails to mention that the weapon is piercing and deals knockback; this makes the Kirhosi Blaster more interesting than the tooltip would suggest. By contrast, Solarium Quarterstaff is ***not*** listed here because the tooltip describes everything important about the weapon: it's a quarterstaff that deals radiation damage and has the Energy Whirl alt ability.

Note: As of October 18, 2021, this is a new category that needs to be manually populated based on player observations. This is not yet an exhaustive list of ***all*** weapons that have extra properties.
