# [FU] Weapons

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapons

---

Frackin' Universe adds more variety with the weapons. Adding plenty of weapons for each tier in the game, where each weapon has it's own playstyle, pros, and cons. So it's important to have different types of weapons for each planet, monster, or bosses your going up against so you don't end up shredded.

##### Elemental Damage
The mod adds more elemental damage types into Starbound's existing four types. This includes Meow, Silver, Stun, Radioactive, Shadow, and Cosmic.

Currently, these are the types of weapons that have been added in Frackin Universe.

## Melee Weapons
### One Handed

### Two Handed
1. Greataxes
1. Quarterstaffs
1. Scythes
1. Lance - a Spear variant. Heavier, they tend to be more damaging but cannot be "moved" when extended as normal spears.
