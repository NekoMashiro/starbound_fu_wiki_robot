# [FU] Well

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **分类**: Resource Generator | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Well

---

**Well** is a building that slowly generates Water over time. Place it anywhere on your base. If the Well is full (already has 200 Water inside), it will pause production until you remove the existing Water.

Well can be replaced with better Wooden Water Tower (also tier 1, but slightly more expensive), and then by Water Generator (tier 3).

If there are other wells (Well, Wooden Water Tower, Water Generator) within 18 blocks, then Well will have significantly reduced production. Scan the Well to check if it was affected by this.
