# [FU] Wooden Centrifuge

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Wooden_Centrifuge

---

**Wooden Centrifuge** is an early game centrifuge. It can process Honeycombs, Water, Contaminated Water and Milk into various components. It doesn't require power.

## Limitations
1. Processing other liquids (such as Poison or Lava) will require Iron Centrifuge instead.
1. Unlike in better centrifuges, Wooden Centrifuge wouldn't produce any Dark Water when centrifuging Water.
