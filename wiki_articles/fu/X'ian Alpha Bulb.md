# [FU] X'ian Alpha Bulb

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/X'ian_Alpha_Bulb

---

**Alpha Seed Bulb** is an extremely good version of X'ian Seed Bulb. 

Alpha Seed Bulb has a 1% chance to appear instead of X'ian Seed Bulb.

Alpha Seed Bulb applies its heal/food bonus every 0.5 seconds (while normal X'ian Seed Bulb needs 2 seconds, and X'ian Mutant Bulb needs 1 second).
