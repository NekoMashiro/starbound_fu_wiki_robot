# [FU] X'ian Well

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/X'ian_Well

---

**X'ian Well** is a variant of Well used by X'i. It has the same speed, but it can accumulate up to 500 Water (standard Well can only accumulate 200).

If there are other wells (including Well and its upgrades) within 25 blocks, then X'ian Well will have significantly reduced production. Scan the X'ian Well to check if it was affected by this.
