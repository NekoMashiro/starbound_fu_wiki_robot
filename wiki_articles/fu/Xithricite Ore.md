# [FU] Xithricite Ore

**来源**: Frackin' Universe Wiki | **链接**: https://frackinuniverse.miraheze.org/wiki/Xithricite_Ore

---

**Xithricite Ore** is a tier 6 ore found on Aether World planets. It is somewhat scarce near the surface, so you might want to dig deep into the planet.

It glows in the dark, but this is not very useful, because Aether biomes are usually well-illuminated.
