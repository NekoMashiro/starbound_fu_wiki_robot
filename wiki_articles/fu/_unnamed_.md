# [FU] ...

"**...**" is an item shop in the Science Outpost. It sells darkness-themed objects and Loot Boxes.

## Trivia
- The shop is called "...", because when its merchant (who is a Tenebrhae) was asked "what is the name of your shop?", he replied "...".
